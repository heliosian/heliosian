Description: A deleted Loop group's sent mail stays keyed by its name alone, in Loop's history and in Helios Ask's documents, so any signed-in account that makes a new group of that name reads the old group's subjects, senders, recipients and - through Ask - whole posts.
Status: fixed
Severity: medium
---
Anyone may make a group of any free name and becomes its manager (`saveGroup` in `internal/loop/handlers.go`). Every record of a group's mail is keyed by the name: the `Messages` and `Deliveries` rows, the archived messages they name, and Ask's documents of kind `group` whose channel is the name. `history` (`internal/loop/history.go`) and Ask's `canRead` (`internal/ask/access.go`) both answer for whichever group holds the name now, and `repliesTo` (`internal/loop/mail.go`) takes any `sent` row under the name as the group's own thread.

Once a group is deleted nothing can read its record - the messages route answers not found and Ask's readable set is built from the live groups - so the record's only possible reader was a namesake. The fault was a delete that left the record behind.

Fixed: `deleteGroup` now removes the group's `Messages` and `Deliveries` rows in memory (`withoutGroup` in `internal/loop/loop.go`) and in the sheet and calls `Documents.Remove` with the name, which `Filer.Remove` (`internal/artifacts/inbox.go`) answers by deleting the group's `Documents` rows and dropping the documents from the model (`Cache.remove`). The archived messages and the documents' objects stay in their buckets, which are written and never emptied: nothing serves the mail archive, and a document's object is read only through its row, so with the rows gone the objects reach nobody. `TestDeletingAGroupTakesItsMailRecordWithIt` in `internal/loop` and `TestRemovingAGroupsMailTakesItsRowsAndDocumentsAndLeavesTheObjects` in `internal/artifacts` hold it. `docs/loop/data.md`, `docs/loop/loop.md` and `docs/ask/artifacts.md` say what a delete takes.

Rows left in `Messages`, `Deliveries` and `Documents` by groups deleted before this change are still in the sheets, unreadable until a namesake is made; they want one pass by hand to delete them, and a group made later under such a name would read them until that is done.
