Description: Helios When resolves a private event by bare id for any member (`eventFor`, `oneEvent`), so anyone holding or guessing an eight-character id opens an event meant for its invitees - its words, its place, its flyer and its whole guest list with names, photos and answers - and the guest list has a visibility setting of its own that never asked whether the viewer was invited.
Status: fixed
Severity: medium
---
`Events.Status` carries three things at once - approval, cancellation, and one word, Private, for an event off the calendar - and `eventFor` (`internal/calendar/rsvp.go`) and `oneEvent` (`internal/calendar/handlers.go`) hand any Private or Pending event to any member by id. Every route past them inherits that: the page, `GET /api/calendar/invites` with `Coming`, the RSVP, the guest routes. `invitesView` then gates `Coming` on an `Invitations` row's Guest List column, Public or Private, which decides who reads the list but never whether the viewer is invited, so the list follows the event.

Fixed by giving an event one visibility, `Sharing` on `Events` (`docs/calendar/calendar.md`, Who can see an event): Public, Link, or Invite Only, with `Status` left to approval and cancellation. `sees` (`rsvp.go`) is the one rule every id lookup goes through: a Link event to anyone with the link, an Invite Only event to whoever is on the list or a parent of a student on it (`Model.Listed`), a pending or cancelled public event to anyone with the link, a declined one to its host and the admins; hosts and admins always. Whoever can open an event reads its guest list; the Guest List column, its constants, its route case and its picker are gone. The link to a Link event lets its bearer see and answer, not invite others in the hosts' name. `TestInviteOnlyIsForTheInvited` holds the 404s for an uninvited member with the id in hand and the parent's standing with their student; `TestSharingWords` holds the sheet's three words.

The production sheet's three Private rows are migrated by hand to `Sharing` Link with `Status` cleared; the aliases Direct Link Only and RSVP Invite were in no row and are gone. What the public share card still draws by id is its own finding, `calendar-share-card-draws-invite-only-event-by-id`.
