Description: Helios Ask's mail hook files a message as list mail, a school newsletter or an announcement on the strength of headers its sender wrote - `List-Id`, `From`, or `To` and `Cc` (`artifacts.Channel`) - checking nothing about who sent it or how it came, so one mail to the forwarding mailbox, forged or merely addressed the right way, puts an outsider's text into every member's answers and system prompt.
Status: fixed
Severity: high
---
The hook in `internal/artifacts/inbox.go` verified Mailgun's signature on the notification and then filed by `Channel` in `internal/artifacts/channel.go`, whose three branches each read a header the sender wrote: a `List-Id` naming a school list, a `From` holding a mailer's name anywhere in the header (`"veracross.com" <x@elsewhere.example>` passed), or a broadcast address among `To` and `Cc`. Each answered every asker, and a recent document's title goes into every conversation's system prompt (`internal/ask/prompt.go`), so a mail titled "School closed Monday" became the latest word, and a forged newsletter filed first displaced the real issue in `known`.

Mail reaches the hook forwarded from a parent's Google mailbox, which breaks the sender's DKIM but seals an ARC set Mailgun checks. The hook now asks `vouch` in `channel.go` for proof before filing, and logs and drops a message without it. A newsletter is mail with no list header whose `From` is at a mailer's domain or under one, and that `From` must pass `mail.Authenticated` (`internal/mail/headers.go`), the test Loop's posts are held to, which reads Google's newest sealed results once Mailgun's topmost results say `arc=pass`. A list message must show in those sealed results an SPF pass for the list's own Google Groups bounce address, `<list>+…@` a school domain, read by `mail.SealedSender`, so a forged `List-Id` from outside or from a member writing to the mailbox directly is refused. `mailer` matches the parsed address's domain by suffix rather than a substring of the header. The `To`/`Cc` branch and `KindAnnouncement` are gone: no real mail used it, since mail to a broadcast address comes through the list and carries its `List-Id`. A message whose markdown runs past `maxMailMarkdown` is dropped before embedding.

`TestVouchTakesTheForwardingMailboxsSealedResults` and `TestChannelKeepsOnlyBroadcasts` in `internal/artifacts/inbox_test.go` pin the proofs and the forgeries, and `TestInboxImportsOnlyTheCommunitysMailOnce` pins a class-list message that did not pass through the list being dropped by the hook.
