Description: A session is a signed address and an expiry thirty days out that nothing re-checks (`auth.sessionEmail`), and sign-out only clears the browser's copy, so a copied cookie, a lost phone or someone who has left the school keeps every app but Who? for up to a month, the one lever being a new `SESSION_KEY` that signs everybody out.
Status: fixed
Severity: medium
---
A leaver is ended by the member gate every app's sign-in now runs on each request (`member-gate-only-on-who.md`).

For everyone else, the session cookie signs the address and the moment of issue rather than an expiry (`auth.Token`), and `sessionEmail` (`internal/auth/auth.go`) holds a token for `sessionLength` from that moment and refuses one issued at or before the address's row in the Config sheet's new `Signed Out` tab, read from the config cache already in memory (`auth.Sessions`, which `config.Cache` implements). `logout` records the moment for the address really signed in through the cache's mirror-then-write path before it clears the cookies, and answers 500 with the cookies left alone when the record refuses; a super admin ends anyone's sessions with `POST /api/config/sign-out`. The one instance's memory is the truth the moment a sign-out lands, and the sheet carries it across a restart. The cookie is one JSON object, base64url: the session with named keys, `email` and `issued`, and the `signature` over the session's exact bytes. A cookie of the old shape - the address, a pipe and an expiry, base64url, a dot and a signature, which a check reading the same shape would have taken for an issue time up to thirty days ahead, keeping the cookie alive for thirty more and past any sign-out - does not decode, and every session from before the deploy is refused once. The session's length is now the server's constant alone, so a shorter one applies to cookies already out.

Held by `TestSessionEndsAtSignOut` and `TestLogoutClearsEveryDomain` in `internal/auth/auth_test.go` and `TestSignOutIsRecordedOncePerAddress` in `internal/config/config_test.go`; `docs/config.md` and `docs/dev.md` say so. A Workspace account suspended after sign-in still keeps its session until a sign-out or the member gate ends it, since Google is asked once.
