Find a person

Edit the fields that decide which classroom or crew a staff member belongs to, plus their name and facts — Classroom, Crew, Department, Job Title, Grade Band, Full Name, Legal Name, Preferred Name, and Facts. Changes here save straight to the Overrides sheet, whether or not the field also has its own self-service editor elsewhere.

Find a student

Edit a student's Overrides-backed fields — Full Name, Legal Name, Preferred Name, Grade, Classroom, and Crew. Changes here save straight to the Overrides sheet, whether or not the field also has its own self-service editor elsewhere.

Find a parent

Edit a parent's Overrides-backed fields — Full Name, Legal Name, Preferred Name, Phone, Room Parent, and Address. Address is family-level: it's written to this parent's own row, and merges with whatever their co-parent's row supplies.
