The Helios School community, online.

Heliosian is a small set of apps built by parent volunteers for the families and staff of Helios School: a directory to find one another, a link page to the things the community uses, and a portal for volunteering at school events. Sign in with your Helios School Google account to use them.

Open only to accounts in the school's Google Workspace. Signing in shares your name and email address with the apps, and nothing else.

Helios Who?who.heliosian.com

The school directory: students, families and staff, with photos, classrooms, contact details and a map, so a new family can put names to faces and reach a classmate's parents.

Heliosianheliosian.com

The community's link page: the calendars, forms, chat groups and documents families use through the year, kept in one place and in order.

HCA-Teamhca.heliosian.com

The Helios Community Association's volunteer portal: every event, committee and shift of the school year, who is organizing it, and where a hand is needed - sign up in a click.

About

These apps are made and run by volunteers from the school community, in the open, at github.com/heliosian/heliosian. They are not run by the school. Questions, ideas and problems go to help@heliosian.com.
