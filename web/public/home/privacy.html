Privacy

Last updated September 11, 2026

Heliosian is a set of community apps for families and staff of Helios School - the school directory (Helios Who?), the community link page (Heliosian), and the HCA volunteer portal (HCA-Team). They are built and run by volunteers from the school community, not by the school itself, and this page says what they do with information about you.

Who can use them

Every app sits behind Google sign-in and is open only to accounts in the school's Google Workspace. Nothing in them is visible to anyone outside the school community, and nothing is public on the web.

What we use from your Google account

When you sign in, Google tells us your name, your email address, and that the account belongs to the school's domain. We use these to recognize you, to show your name where you have signed up for something, and to keep a session cookie so you stay signed in. We do not read your email, your calendar, your files, or anything else in your Google account, and we ask Google for no other permissions.

Information in the apps

  • Directory information - names, grades, classrooms, family contact details and photos - comes from the school's records and from what families choose to add themselves. It is shown only to signed-in members of the school community. Families can hide their contact details or opt out of the directory through the school; the directory shows an "opt-out" note instead.
  • Volunteer sign-ups - when you sign up for an event or committee, or sign someone in your family up, your name and email are recorded with that activity so the organizers can reach you. Organizers see the contact details of their volunteers; other members see the names of who has signed up, unless the organizers have made a list private.
  • Changes you make - edits to the directory and the volunteer portal are logged with your account, so organizers can see who changed what.

How we use it

We use this information for one purpose: running the community's own apps. Your name and email identify you when you sign in and when you sign up for something; directory information lets members find and reach one another; sign-ups let organizers plan an event and contact their volunteers; the change log lets organizers see who edited what. We do not use it to build profiles, to advertise, or for anything unrelated to the school community.

Who we share it with

Nobody outside the school community. Within it, the apps show each member what the sections above describe. The apps are hosted on Google Cloud, which processes the data on our behalf under Google's own terms; Google does not receive it for any purpose of its own beyond running the service. We do not sell, rent or trade information, and we disclose it only if the law requires.

How long we keep it

Directory information is kept while a family or staff member is part of the school and refreshed from the school's records; when someone leaves, they drop out of the directory at the next refresh. Volunteer sign-ups are kept by school year so organizers can plan from what happened last year; a sign-up you remove is deleted at once. Uploaded photos that nothing refers to any more are deleted. You can ask us to delete anything about you at any time (see Contact).

Security

The apps are reachable only over encrypted connections and only after Google sign-in. The information is held in a Google Cloud project that only the maintaining volunteers can open, and the server keeps no credentials of its own. The sign-in cookie is signed so it cannot be forged and expires on its own.

Google account data

The apps use Google Sign-In only. They receive your name, email address and Google account picture, and the fact that your account is in the school's Workspace. They request no other Google permissions and never access Gmail, Drive, Calendar or any other Google service on your behalf. Signing in creates a session cookie; signing out removes it.

Children

The apps are for the adults of the community - parents, guardians and staff. Students appear in the directory as the school's records list them, for their families and teachers to see; students do not sign in themselves. If you believe a child's information appears that should not, tell us and we will remove it.

Where it lives

The apps store their information in Google Sheets and Google Cloud Storage belonging to the community's own Google Cloud project, and run on Google Cloud in the United States. Access to that project is limited to the volunteers who maintain the apps.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share this information with anyone outside the school community, we show no advertising, and we use no analytics or tracking services. The only cookie is the one that keeps you signed in.

Photos and pictures

Directory photos come from the school's records or are uploaded by families; families may replace or remove their own. Pictures on volunteer events are uploaded by organizers, or chosen from free-to-use image libraries, and are visible only within the community.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time from the account menu. To change or remove information about you or your family, use the edit controls in the apps, or ask us and we will do it for you. To opt out of the directory entirely, contact the school office.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anything substantial is announced to the community. The current version is always at heliosian.com/privacy.html.

Contact

Questions, corrections and deletion requests go to help@heliosian.com. The apps are run by parent volunteers of Helios School, Sunnyvale, California.
