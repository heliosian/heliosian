Terms of use

Last updated September 11, 2026

Heliosian's apps are made by and for the Helios School community. Using them means agreeing to a few plain things.

Community use only

The information in these apps is for the school community's own use - reaching a classmate's family, organizing an event, finding a volunteer. Do not copy it out for any other purpose, share it outside the community, or use it for marketing or solicitation.

Your account

You sign in with your own school Google account, and what you do while signed in is done as you. Keep your account to yourself.

What you add

Anything you add - a photo, a fact about yourself, an event, a sign-up on someone's behalf - should be yours to add and accurate. Organizers may edit or remove what does not belong.

As is

The apps are run by volunteers in their spare time. They may be wrong, slow, or down, and they may change. We do our best, and we make no promises.
