# Helios When

Helios When is the school year as one family sees it: what kind of day today is for their students' classrooms, what is coming up, and the whole year to browse - the school's events, the HCA's activities, Celebrate's parties and what the community adds, one data model under all of them (`docs/datamodel.md`: Calendar import, Saved searches, Guest lists). It lives at the first of its `Hosts` in `hosts.Apps` (`internal/hosts/hosts.go`); the others there only send their root to it and answer 404 for anything else (`route` in `internal/app/server.go`). The pages are `web/when/`: they read everything in one `/api/q` batch (`loadModel` in `web/when/state.js`) and write through `/api/q` batches (`web/when/writes.js`) and `/api/do/photo`, under the policies, so the server holds no When-only rules for them. Its host also serves the page shell for each address (`calendarMux` in `internal/app/wiring.go`), and from `internal/db` the feeds (`RegisterFeeds`), the share cards and previews (`WhenPages`), the guest lists' mail and outside pages (`RegisterInvitations`), and the delivery reports of its mail (`Deliveries`).

## What a viewer sees

Every page is filtered by the top bar's search box (below) and by two choices, remembered per browser (`calendar.filters` in local storage), that sit in a card above the month grid on the calendar page - folded to one line naming what is in view - All classrooms or the classrooms by name, then the categories, with Select all, Clear all, Reset filters (back to the calendar being worked from), each only while it would change something, and Forget my saved view while My Heliosian carries one - the same card on a phone, which the tab bar's Filters unfolds and scrolls to:

- **Classrooms** - which classrooms the pages are about. A parent starts with their children's classrooms and anyone with none with every classroom; All is the whole school. The chips sit gradeband by gradeband, in the classroom colors; a chip that is off is dashed and faded.
- **Categories** - the top-level categories (`GROUP` kind `category` with no parent, in their `order`), each on unless its `default` says otherwise, and two words of the viewer's own: **Going** - what the household holds a ticket, a sign-up or a yes for - and **Waitlisted** - a party someone in the household waits for.

The filters are turned into a saved search's words - a classroom is its slug and its parents group's (`hummingbirds`, `hummingbirds-parents`, and `staff` beside them for staff), a category its slug - and each word becomes a call to the saved search definitions, `(searched_for @e "hummingbirds")`, `(going @e)` or `(waitlisted @e)`, in one query through `QUERY /api/q` that answers which events, parties and activities show (`wordsFor` and `refreshMatches` in `state.js`; `docs/datamodel.md`, Saved searches), so the page never decides on its own what a filter admits. Every classroom or every category chosen is no words at all, so a whole-school view keeps up as classrooms and categories come and go. A link-only event shows only to someone who has answered it, hosts it or is in it.

**My Heliosian** is the viewer's own default calendar: their `SAVED_SEARCH` row with `default` on. It opens to their classrooms and the default categories unless they kept other filters on it; Forget my saved view writes those defaults back into it (`forgetView` in `writes.js`).

## Pages

One shell, `web/when/index.html`, routes every page on the client, under the shared top bar (`docs/toolbar.md`) beside a rail of the two pages - Calendar, My Events - with sections under My Events - **RSVP**, the invitations waiting for the viewer's reply, its count on a red badge; **Attending**, the yeses; **Hosting**; **Pending Approval**, what the viewer added that waits for an admin - each with its count and opening its own list (`/mine/rsvp`, `/mine/attending`, `/mine/hosting`, `/mine/pending`; `/mine` has them all; `myEvents` in `state.js`); a section with nothing in it is left out - and, under them, the day (`web/when/day.js`, drawn there by `chrome.js` for every page): a small month to pick a day from, a dot under each day in the color of what is on it, and the day last opened as a card, today until one is. On a phone the rail becomes a drawer of the two pages and the saved calendars, the day heads the calendar page instead, and a bottom tab bar holds the pages and Filters.

- **Calendar** (`/`, or `/day/{date}` for any other day) - the chosen day in the rail, and the page itself in three parts. The day card: Today, or the weekday, over the date; then the day plan for the chosen classrooms as one card per day type in force - its name, the classrooms it covers when they are not all of them, and its parts with their hours - a weekend or a day outside the school year saying so instead; then the day's events down a timeline. Each classroom has one day type a day: No School over any other, any other over Regular (`dayWins` in `state.js`). The page: the filters first, then the month grid, paged in place with Today at the row's end. Save Calendar keeps what the filters show as a saved calendar (a `SAVED_SEARCH` row, `saveFeed` in `writes.js`), and while the filters have moved from a saved calendar's it saves them back onto it instead, keeping its name, mark and address. A day that is not regular wears its day type as a pill, then the first few events as pills in their color; today is ringed in amber and the day open in the rail in teal; a cell opens that day in the rail and a pill opens that event, and while the pointer rests on a pill a card opens under it with the event - its hours, title, place and description, the household's part in it, and Yes and No (or a party's Add to my calendar / Add Ticket) with Hide event under them (`peek.js`). To the month's right, when the window is wide enough, Upcoming Events: first, while any invitation waits on the viewer's reply, a folded "N events need your RSVP" panel; then the events from the day shown on in the day bars and rows Heliosian's widgets use (`dayrows.js`, `docs/dev.md`), each row's right side saying where the household stands - a ticket, a sign-up, "✓ Going" on a yes, RSVP on an invitation still waiting, else the way into a party or an activity while it is open (Get tickets, Join, Join the waitlist) - and an amber star before the title of one the viewer hosts; a day that is not regular for the chosen classrooms is a row too while Schedule is on. Narrower windows put Upcoming under the month; a phone puts the day card first, then Upcoming, then the month.
- **Admins** - When's admins are the members of the group its `APP` row names (`admin_of "when"` in the policies, `docs/datamodel.md`, Permissions): their own additions go live without approval, they approve and decline what waits, move and run every event, and edit the top-level categories. Admin Tools is in their account menu.
- **Admin Tools** (`/admin`), drawn by the builder every app's Admin Tools shares (`web/common/admin.js`, `docs/toolbar.md`). Events: what waits for approval first, each with Approve (`status` open) and Decline (`status` closed, which takes it off the calendar for everyone); then the events added by hand - those with no calendar source - soonest first, past ones kept out unless asked for, under filters (words in the title, a category, a classroom, a span of dates), each with its start and end as pickers that offer Save once changed (`moveEvent`) and a copy button that opens Add Event filled from it four weeks on (`/admin?clone={id}&weeks=4` opens it the same way). Categories: the top-level categories as one list in their order, dragged to reorder, each with its picture (an upload or the picture libraries), its description and whether it is on by default; Save categories writes each one's `name`, `description`, `default` and `order`, makes a new one with a slug and `adding` approval, deletes one taken off the list (`saveCategories`), and makes a changed picture the category's `PHOTO` in place of the old one (`setPicture`).
- **Event** (`/g/{key}`, the key its slug, ID or an old address's alias; the address bar is corrected to its generated `path`) - a picture across the top with the date card every app shares at its corner (`web/common/datecard.js`; `docs/dev.md`), and for a party or an activity a badge at its foot with that app's mark and the way to its page there; then the chips (the day type when it carries one, who it is for, and for a hand-added event how it is shared - Public, Anyone with the link, Invite only, or Pending approval), the title, the description, and a rail saying where it comes from: the school's Google Calendar, the High School Counseling Google Calendar, the school's year calendar, Helios Celebrate, HCA-Team, or who added it and when (`sourceWords` in `state.js`). The picture is the event's own (its first `PHOTO`), else its first category's, else the calendar's own header (`web/public/when/brand/default-header.jpg`, from `default_header.png` in the exports). The banner is the one Celebrate and HCA-Team wear (`detailHero`, `docs/dev.md`). Who an event is for is its audience group's classrooms, compressed: every classroom is Everyone, both classrooms of a gradeband are the gradeband, the rest are named.
- **Subscribe** - beside the calendar's name: a menu putting the calendar shown into a calendar app - Add to Google Calendar (Google's subscribe page with the `webcal://` address), Add to Apple Calendar (the `webcal://` address itself), Copy calendar feed URL for Outlook and the rest - each the saved calendar's own feed address (Feeds, below).

In every list an event is a row: its hours, its title, the day type it imposes when it does, its place, and who it is for as color - the classroom colors (`GROUP.color`), one dot per color at the row's end, and nothing for an event that is everyone's. Where an event is one thing - the month's pills, the rail's timeline, the upcoming rows, the small month's dots - it wears one color (`eventTint` in `state.js`): its first classroom's, or for an event that is everyone's, one for what it is - Celebrate's parties pink, HCA-Team's activities purple, everything else blue.

Parties and activities are on the calendar as what they are, `GROUP` rows of kind `party` and `activity` read in the same query as the events, so a party posted or an activity filled is on the calendar the moment it is written. One occasion is one group: an event the school's calendar also lists becomes the activity or party it matches when it is imported (`docs/datamodel.md`, Calendar import). A party's or activity's row carries a pill saying what a reader can do there now - Get tickets, Join the waitlist, Sold out for a party; Join or Full for an activity - that goes to its page on the app that runs it (`appOrigin` in `web/common/appswitch.js`). Where the household stands is read from the same rows: a party someone in the household holds a place at, or an activity someone in it signed up for, on it or anything under it, is Going; a party one waits for is Waitlisted; the pill then names whose it is ("Sam has a ticket", "Sam and Ella have tickets", "Sam signed up", "Waitlisted").

Search is the top bar's box, as in the other apps; `/` puts the cursor in it. It asks the data model's search (`POST /api/do/search`, by name and meaning, `docs/datamodel.md`) for up to 200 groups and filters the page to them: the day's events, the small month's dots, the Upcoming panel - headed Matches, the whole year's - and the month's pills. Every keystroke asks at once, without waiting on the asks before it, and once a frame the page shows the newest answer that has come back, unless an answer to later typing already shows, so results follow the typing a round trip behind and never step back (`search` and `showArrived` in `chrome.js`). The day plan is searched by day-type name on the page, so "early" keeps the early dismissals. An amber band across the page's top says what is being matched, with Clear search. Typing on any other page comes back to the calendar with the words.

## The front page

Heliosian's Upcoming widget and the calendar in its rail read the same events, saved searches and school days as this page, through the same code (`web/common/calendar.js`: `calendarQueries`, `buildCalendar`, `matchingQuery`, `roomsOn`), and their RSVP buttons answer as this page's do (`answerEvent`); `docs/home/home.md` describes them.

## Sharing an event

Anyone can put an event on the calendar: **Add Event**, over the month, opens a form (`eventform.js`) - the title, when (a start time sets the end two hours on until the end is set by hand), where, a source, the words and the category - and the choice of who can find it, which the write turns into the event's cells (`addEvent` in `writes.js`):

- **Invite only** (the default) - `visible_to` the event itself, so its guest list alone sees it; open at once.
- **Anyone with the link** - `visible_to` Everyone and `listed` No; open at once.
- **Public** - `visible_to` Everyone and `listed` Yes, with an audience group under it holding a rule per classroom chosen (`replace_with` household), or one on Everyone; `status` pending until an admin approves it, but an admin's own goes live.

The event sits under its category, or Misc. A source is written at the end of the description as `More information: …`, a markdown link when it is an address. An optional web address becomes its `slug`. The same write makes a managers group holding the person who added it and names it the event's `managed_by`, so whoever adds an event hosts it. When's admins get an email of every event a person adds (`docs/datamodel.md`, Guest lists). A pending event shows to the person who added it and to admins, dashed in amber, its page opening with a Waiting for approval band - for an admin with **Approve** (`status` open) and **Decline** (`status` closed: off the calendar for everyone). **Edit event** on the page writes its title, time, place, description and source, its category, its address, and for a public event its classrooms, replacing its audience's rules (`editEvent`). The form opens on how the event is shared now; choosing another writes `listed`, `visible_to` and `members_visible_to` as adding it would, a person who isn't an admin making it public sends it back to pending, and making a pending event invite-only or link-only opens it. The picture is changed from the strip across the banner's foot, an upload or the picture libraries, saved as the event's `PHOTO` (`setPicture`).

## Who can see an event

An event is open to someone or it is not, and whoever can open it gets the whole of it - the words, the place, the picture, and, unless the hosts keep it to themselves (Invitations, below), the guest list. Who that is is the policies' `visible` (`docs/datamodel.md`, Permissions): Everyone for a public or link-only event, its guest list for an invite-only one, and its hosts and When's admins for every one. Someone from outside Helios is never sent `/g/{key}`; their `/ext/{token}` page (Invitations, below) is opened by the token of the copy of the invitation they were sent, and shows them the event, the hosts and their own household.

Every event takes the viewer's word - on its page, in a band under the event's body with **Are you going?**, which once answered steps aside for a **My RSVP** card in the rail, and on the month's hover card: **Yes**, **No**, and **Hide event** (Show event once hidden). Each is a `MEMBER` row of the viewer's in the event's Going, Not Going or hidden group (`rsvp_yes`, `rsvp_no`, `hidden_for`), every other one of theirs deleted in the same write (`answer` in `state.js`); clearing deletes them all. An event nobody has been invited to shows its answers in an **RSVPs** card in the rail, as Who's coming shows an invited list's (Invitations, below): the yeses to everyone who can open it, and to its hosts and When's admins the nos too, or "Nobody has answered yet". A yes files the event under Going for them, here and in their feeds; a no grays it on the month; hiding takes it out of every list and feed of theirs while it stays on the month in gray words. A party has no yes or no: with a ticket in the household it offers **Add to my calendar**, which is a yes, and without one **Add Ticket**, the way to the party page. A yes on an event sends a calendar invite by email to someone whose invitation did not carry one, and an invitation's calendar file names a reply address of that copy's own, so the Accept or Decline tapped in a calendar app comes back as the answer (`docs/datamodel.md`, Guest lists). An event the viewer hosts wears an amber star before its title on the page, in Upcoming and in My Events.

## Invitations

Every event, party and activity has a guest list - an event's the people put on it, a party's its ticket holders, an activity's its sign-ups - built in one step and sent in another, so the list is finished before anyone hears of it. How it is kept, sent and answered is the data model's (`docs/datamodel.md`, Guest lists); the page is `web/when/invites.js` over `web/when/guestlist.js`. Its **hosts** are the event's managers: the **Hosts** card in the rail shows them to everyone who can open the event, and for a host carries Add co-host (who hears of it by email), a cross on each host to take them off or step down, and **Hide hosts**, which keeps the card to the hosts. A host's **Guest list** card in the rail counts the list - invited, yes, no, no reply yet, unsent (pending) and, once the invitation is out, opened - each opening the people it counts; **See table** opens it in the shared data grid; **Send** offers the pending invitations, an RSVP reminder, an event reminder and a message, the reminders with the calendar invite attached; the gear opens **Guest List Settings**: Guests, the list's rules in the editor Loop uses (`web/common/listrules.js`) beside everyone on it with why, and Settings, four switches - Show the guest list to everyone, People can invite others, Send new guests their invitation (`auto_invite`) and Notify me when people respond. **Add People** adds from the directory, by a rule (Add Group, the hosts' alone) or from outside by family, everyone from outside answering for their family on their own page. The **Pending** band over Who's coming sends everyone pending at once, or chosen people from More info. Anyone a host lets invite others adds people through the same picker, sent their invitation at once. **Cancel event** on a hand-added event, once the invitation is out, sets it cancelled and, if asked, mails everyone sent it the note and a calendar cancellation; before then **Delete event** closes it. Once the invitation is out, changing the title, the time or the place offers to send everyone but those who said no the updated invitation. Someone from outside is sent `/ext/{token}`, a page of their own with the event, who invites them, their answer, their family's and their guests', and nothing of anyone else.
Whoever is on a list the household is invited to sees, on the event's page, a band asking the household: a row for the viewer and each of their family on the list, each with Yes and No, and once everyone has answered the band leaves the page's head for **My RSVP** in the rail, where each answer is changed in place, the guests the household brought have a cross to take them back, and **Add a guest** brings someone from Helios or someone else by name and address, answered Yes and sent their own invitation when asked. **Who's coming** takes the page's width under it: the faces under Yes, No response and, for the hosts, No and Pending, grouped by family, with the shared filters (`listFilters` in `web/when/guesttable.js`), each opening a card where a host corrects the answer, sends a pending invitation or takes the person off the list. A party's faces carry, for a host, a mark for a bought ticket or a free place. The page reads all of it from the data model (`fetchInvites` in `web/when/guestlist.js`) as the viewer may see it, and notes when the viewer first opens an invitation.

## Sharing

A link to an event pasted into a chat app previews the way HCA-Team's and Celebrate's do (`WhenPages` in `internal/db/whenpages.go`, on the shared drawer `internal/sharecard`): the sign-in page served at the event's address (`/g/`, then its ID, slug or alias) carries Open Graph tags - the title, the day and hours, the place, and the description cut short - and names a card at `/open/share/{id}.png`, drawn with the mark and wordmark, the topmost category above it as a kicker, the title, the lines, a button in the brand teal saying what the page asks - RSVP for an event, Sign Up for an activity, Get Tickets for a party - and its flyer whole, else the first picture of it or of the nearest group above it that has one, else the calendar's header. Every event, party and activity has its card and preview, invite-only and pending ones too, with no check of who may see it (`docs/dev.md`). An outside guest's `/ext/{token}` page carries the same preview, and the invitation mail and every other mail about the event show the card at their head, linked to the page. A link to any other page - the calendar, a saved calendar's `/c/` address, the feeds - previews Helios When itself: a line on what it is in words, and a card at `/open/share/upcoming.png` that says so in three lines (school days and dismissals, events and sign-ups, calendars to save and subscribe to) with an Open Helios When button, over the calendar's own header picture.

## Feeds

A feed is an address a calendar app subscribes to: `https://when.heliosian.com/open/feed/{token}.ics`, served without sign-in (`auth.Public`), since Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and Outlook fetch it themselves. The token is a saved search's `SAVED_SEARCH.token` (`docs/datamodel.md`, IDs), or an old feed token a hidden `ALIAS` row names it by; it is the whole secret, so anyone holding the address reads the feed, and removing the calendar from the rail is how it stops.

The rail lists the viewer's calendars under Calendar: My Heliosian, which nobody removes, and their other saved calendars in their `order` (`allCalendars` in `state.js`), each opening the calendar with the filters set to its words, at `/c/{token}` too. The first is their default, the one the page opens to; Make Default moves `default` to another (`makeDefault`). A pencil at Calendar's end turns on, beside each, a grip to drag it into a new order (`orderFeeds`, writing `order` keys), a pencil (name and emoji, with Make default and Delete calendar) and a cross. A saved calendar is its owner's alone: only they write it (`docs/datamodel.md`, Saved searches).

The document (`RegisterFeeds` in `internal/db/savedsearch.go`) runs the saved search's words as its owner (`docs/datamodel.md`, Saved searches), so it carries what their saved calendar shows them, less what they hid: every event, party and activity that is not closed, from a year back to two years ahead, all-day ones as dates with the exclusive end iCalendar wants, timed ones in the school's zone, the description followed by the thing's page, and that page as its URL. It asks the client to refresh hourly; Google refreshes on its own schedule, every several hours. The day plan itself is not in the feed: the departures from a regular day are events already, and a regular day is not.

## Sign-in and install

Everything sits behind Google sign-in restricted to the school domain, sharing a session with the other apps on the same tier. It installs to a home screen with the calendar-with-sun-and-leaf mark under `web/public/when/brand/`, cut from the Helios When exports on the recipe the other apps use (`docs/brand.md`): `appicon.png` for the home-screen icons, the bare mark (`favicon.png` there) for the favicons, `logo-mark.png` (the drawer's head), the app switch row (`web/public/common/brand/apps/when.png`) and the maskables at 62% on the icon's own pale ground; the teal long horizontal lockup, name and tagline on two lines beside the mark, for the rail (`logo-lockup-wide.png`, from `- long horizontal.png`, 900px wide, up to 310px on screen) and the white vertical one for the login page (`logo-lockup-light.png`); the vertical teal, the two-line horizontals and the white long horizontal (`logo-lockup-light-wide.png`) unused so far. The iOS splash screens are under `brand/splash/`.
