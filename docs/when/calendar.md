# Helios When

Helios When is the school year as one family sees it: what kind of day today is for their students' classrooms, what is coming up, and the whole year to browse, with the school's two published sources merged, classified, and corrected in one sheet. It lives at the first of its `Hosts` in `internal/model/home.go`; the others there only send their root to it and answer 404 for anything else (`route` in `internal/app/server.go`). The sheet is `Calendar` (`data.md`), the code is in `internal/model` (`calendar.go` and the files beside it), and it is served by the resource API (`docs/api.md`, Helios When), with its preview, options and image routes under `/api/when/`.

## What a viewer sees

Every page is filtered by the top bar's search box (below) and by two choices, remembered per browser, that sit in a card above the month grid on the calendar page - folded to one line on every visit naming what is in view - All classrooms or the classrooms by name, then each category line as All School day or its chosen names, labelled rows when unfolded, and under the rows Select all (every classroom and category on), Clear all (every category off), Reset filters (back to the calendar being worked from), each only while it would change something, and Forget my saved view while My Heliosian carries one - the same card on a phone, which the tab bar's Filters unfolds and scrolls to:

- **Classrooms** - which classrooms the pages are about. A parent starts with their children's classrooms, a student with their own, a classroom teacher with theirs, and anyone with none - the office, a specialist - with every classroom. Mine puts a parent's own back; All is the whole school. The chips sit band by band, in the classroom colors; a chip that is off is dashed and faded. An event shows when any of its classrooms is chosen; an event with no classroom at all shows to everyone.
- **Categories** - which tags, one line per group the `Tags` tab files them under (`data.md`), each line's tags in alphabetical order with All and None for that line alone; tags with no group are the plain Categories line at the end. A tag starts on unless the `Tags` tab's Default column (set from Admin Tools) says otherwise, and Reset filters returns to that set; a line's None clears it for a quick look at one kind of thing. An event shows when any of its category tags is chosen; the classrooms among its tags are the audience, not a category. Five of the tags are built in (`data.md`, Tags and day types by ID), on whatever line their rows give them - Other in the sample: Celebrate and HCA for what the other apps run (below), Going and Waitlisted for where the viewer's own household stands with those (Going also holds every event they said yes to), and Misc for an event the sheet gave classrooms but no category (`data.md`). Every line's None, then Going, is the family's own calendar.

A saved view (`Settings` tab, one row per address: `when-settings`' `save-view`, `forget-view` to forget it) stands as the person's own defaults: the calendar opens to it on every device, Reset filters returns to it, the folded head says "your saved view", and My Heliosian, wherever it is read - Heliosian's widgets among them - takes it in place of the person's classrooms and the default categories (`myHeliosianView` in `internal/model/calendar_upcoming.go`). The page no longer offers to save one - a saved calendar is the way - but one saved earlier still stands inside My Heliosian, and Forget my saved view in the filters' footer lets it go. The classrooms and tags are the sheet's live vocabulary, so a renamed classroom drops out of a remembered filter, and of a saved view, rather than hiding everything; the filters remember tags by ID, so a renamed tag stays chosen. The feeds form picks classrooms and categories with the same chips, the categories on the same lines, the built-ins among them.

## Pages

One shell, `web/when/index.html`, routes every page on the client, under the shared top bar (`docs/toolbar.md`) beside a rail of the two pages - Calendar, My Events - with sections under My Events - **RSVP**, the invitations waiting for the viewer's reply, its count on a red badge as Who?'s rail counts what is owed; **Attending**, the yeses; **Hosting**; **Pending Approval**, what the viewer shared that waits for an admin, kept out of Hosting until it is approved - each with its count and opening its own list (`/mine/rsvp`, `/mine/attending`, `/mine/hosting`, `/mine/pending`; `/mine` has them all, as cards like HCA-Team's and Celebrate's); a section with nothing in it is left out, and My Events altogether when all three are empty, on the page as in the rail - and, under them, the day (`web/when/day.js`, drawn there by `chrome.js` for every page): a small month to pick a day from, paged on its own, the days of the months either side faded at its ends, a dot under each day in the color of what is on it, and the day last opened - on the calendar page, or an event's - as a card, today until one is. On a phone the rail becomes a drawer of the two pages and the saved calendars, the day heads the calendar page instead, the filters card stays on the page, and a bottom tab bar holds the three pages and Filters, which unfolds that card.

- **Calendar** (`/`, or `/day/{date}` for any other day) - the chosen day in the rail, and the page itself in three parts. The day card: Today, or the weekday, over the date; then the day plan for the chosen classrooms as one card per day type in force - its name, the classrooms it covers when they are not all of them, and the four blocks, Dropoff, School, Pickup, Aftercare, with their hours, a block the day does not have greyed - a weekend or a day outside the school year saying so instead; then the day's events down a timeline, each a dot in its color, its hours, its title and its place. The page: the filters first, then the month grid, paged in place with Today at the row's end (and Get Feed beside it while the filters are a saved calendar's) - Save Calendar, beside the headline's "Filters changed · not saved" once they have moved, keeps what the filters show as a saved calendar - a feed row - and is there only while the filters are not a saved calendar's already. Working from a saved calendar (the one last opened from the rail or matched on load, remembered with the filters) it is a split button: a click saves the filters back onto it, keeping its name, mark and address, and the caret drops a menu with that and Save New Calendar; working from My Heliosian, whose filters are locked, it is a plain Save New Calendar, which asks for a name in a small popup (the viewer's first name on it to start, a number after it if taken) and lists the new calendar under Calendar in the rail from then on, with no address handed out until wanted: a day that is not regular wears its day type as a pill in its wash, with a count when it is only some of the classrooms, then the first few events as pills in a wash of their color, title over hours (the hours dropped when the cells are narrow); today is ringed in amber and the day open in the rail in teal, days outside the school year are washed, a cell opens that day in the rail and a pill in it opens that event, and while the pointer rests on a cell a card opens under it with the day - its name and its plan with the hours - and, over one of the cell's pills, that event alone: its hours, title, place and description, the household's part in it, and Yes, Maybe and No (or a party's Add to my calendar / Add Ticket) with a small Hide event under them, which answer without leaving the month. The card touches the cell's bottom edge (or its top, when there is no room below), reaches a little past its own edges, and keeps while the pointer is on it or for most of a second after it leaves - the cell's own ground under a pill, and the neighbouring cells, hold the card that long too - so the pointer can cross from a pill to the card's buttons without it changing or closing. To the month's right, when the window is wide enough, Upcoming Events in a panel as tall as the month that scrolls on its own: first, while any invitation waits on the viewer's reply, the same folded "N events need your RSVP" panel Heliosian's Upcoming widget leads with (`rsvps.js`, `docs/home/home.md`), holding the invitations the rail's RSVP counts but those called off, each opening its page; then the events from the day shown on, that day's own first, in the day bars and rows Heliosian's widgets use (`dayrows.js`, `docs/dev.md`) - each day's bar opening that day, and each event's row a dot in its color on a line down the day, its title, and at the right its hours with, under them, the household's standing in a linked event ("✓ Ella has a ticket"), else "✓ Going" on one answered Yes, or "Maybe", with no check, on one answered Maybe, else RSVP on an invitation still waiting on the viewer's word, else the way into a linked event while it is still open (Get tickets, Join, Join the waitlist), and an amber star before the title of one the viewer hosts - each row built as Heliosian's are (`eventRow`, `docs/dev.md`) - the first two weeks under Upcoming and the rest under Later This Year; a day that is not regular for the chosen classrooms and has no event of its own is a row too, in its day type's color, while the Schedule tag is on. Narrower windows put Upcoming under the month; a phone puts the day card first, then Upcoming, then the month.
- **Admins** - a calendar admin holds every power the admin list confers, always, with no mode (`docs/toolbar.md`, Admins). They have every waiting, declined and invite-only event on the calendar and in the lists, and approve, decline, bring back, tag and correct them (`when.see-all`, `when.curate`); they may act in someone else's place (`when.act-as-host`, `when.feeds-for-anyone`), doing whatever a host may on any event and changing anyone's feeds, and the page shows them the answers and For admins lines on every event. The shared toolbar's approvals badge (`docs/toolbar.md`), and a waiting event's page with Approve and Decline, reach every admin. Admin Tools is in their account menu, and the Add Event form goes by the admin list, since the server puts an admin's public event straight onto the calendar.
- **Admin Tools** (`/admin`, calendar admins only - anyone else gets "Admin access required" - reached from the account menu under the avatar) - its own window over the shell, drawn by the builder every app's Admin Tools shares (`web/common/admin.js`, `docs/toolbar.md`): a teal header with the tile, Admin, the address and a close button, a rail of tabs, a card per tool. Events: the hand-added events as a list, soonest first, past ones kept out unless asked for and faded then, under filters - words in the title, a category, a classroom, a span of dates - with how many match, each with its start and end as date and time pickers that offer Save once changed (the event's `move` action, the `Events` tab row's Start and End; an imported event is corrected in Overrides instead) and a copy button; Add Event opens a form in a sheet that puts an event in the `Events` tab under the admin's name - an Event tab with the title, start and end (a date each, and a time unless it is all day), location, description and source, and a Tags tab with the classrooms it is for, its categories and its search words (`POST /api/events`, the row checked by the sheet's rules before anything is written; the route also takes a repeat, every N weeks M more times, which the form does not offer); opened from a row's copy button (`/admin?clone={id}&weeks=4` opens it the same way), the form starts filled from that event with its dates moved on. Categories: the tags as cards, one per group in the filters' order - Add category group makes an empty one, kept only once a tag is in it - each card's head with the name (Rename group makes it a box; renaming renames the group for every tag in it), how many it holds, arrows to move the group and a chevron to fold it; and a row per tag - a handle to drag it before or after another row, or onto a group's head to join that group, its picture (a thumbnail that opens a file chooser, a magnifier that opens the picture search the other apps' editors have - `imagesearch`, on `/api/when/images/search`, `/thumb` and `/import`, uploads on `/api/when/image`, all stored content addressed under `category-images/` - and a cross that takes it off), its description (a box on focus), whether it is on by default, and a picker to move it to another group, an unnamed one, or a new one named on the spot - then a form to add a category (name, description, group), and Save categories, which sends the whole list in order (`when-settings`' `tags` action): a changed description, group, default or image is written to its row, a new tag added, and the order shown kept as the tags' Order sort keys, only the moved tags' written (`data.md`), each change in the Change Log. A new tag is minted its ID; a tag's name is changed in its `Tags` row rather than here, and no tag can be removed from here; the built-in tags are named under their groups rather than listed, their rows changed in the sheet.
- **Event** (`/e/{id}`; `/events/{id}` still opens it, for links in the wild) - a picture across the top with the date card every app shares at its corner (`web/common/datecard.js`; `docs/dev.md`) - the day's tile with the sun as it stands at the start, a second tile for the last day of a span, the hours and the place or the count of days, and Add at the hours' end, a pill that puts it on Google Calendar - and, for an event another app runs, a badge at the picture's foot with that app's mark, what the event is there (a Fun(d)raiser party, an HCA volunteer event) and who hosts it (the household's own standing, or where the tickets or sign-ups stand, until the page knows the hosts), the whole badge the way to the event's page on that app; on a phone both follow the picture instead - then the chips (the day type when it carries one, then who it is for, and for a hand-added event how it is shared - Public, Anyone with the link, Invite only, Pending approval or Declined and what it is), the title, the description with its links live, the day type's blocks when it changes the day, and a rail that, for an event another app runs, keeps only what the guest list gives it - the banner says whose the event is - and otherwise ends with where the event's dates came from, linked to the original - the feed's event in Google Calendar (`GoogleEventURL`, the feed's calendar and the event's id as Google encodes them, an instance's start in UTC after an underscore), the school's calendar page for a line of the year calendar with the school year it was read from and the line as printed when the title has since changed, the page on the app that runs a party or an HCA event, or who added a hand-added event and when, with its Source cell as the proof - a web address linked and named by its host (Veracross's with a word about the parent login), other words as written; the school's listing of an HCA event links to HCA-Team's page too. A calendar admin also sees, under For admins (for an event another app runs, that section alone, and only when it has something to say), the classifier's filing (model and date), any correction the Overrides tab made (the columns and its note) - `Provenance` in the view, sent to admins alone - who answered the event - the yeses and the nos by name, with counts, never who hid it (`Responses` in the view, admins alone) - the event's search words with Edit search words, which writes them to the event's Overrides row (the event's `keywords` action; an empty list is the clearing mark), and for a hand-added event Clone this event 4 weeks on; the rail's day jumps to the event's, so its month, plan and events sit beside the page. The picture is the event's own where the app that runs it has one (a party's, an HCA event's, fetched from that app on the page's own tier), else the picture of the first of its tags that has one in the order the event carries them (`eventImage` in `state.js`), else the calendar's own header (`web/public/when/brand/default-header.jpg`, from `default_header.png` in the exports). The banner is the one Celebrate and HCA-Team wear (`detailHero`, `docs/dev.md`): Share at its top left, the date card at its top right, the picture opened full size on a click. Who an event is for is compressed: every classroom is Everyone, both classrooms of a band are the band, the rest are named.
- **Subscribe** - beside the calendar's name, before Default Calendar: a menu putting the calendar shown into a calendar app - Add to Google Calendar (Google's subscribe page with the `webcal://` address), Add to Apple Calendar (the `webcal://` address itself), Copy calendar feed URL for Outlook and the rest. A saved calendar's address is its own token's; My Heliosian's is the owner's own, made the first time they ask (`when-settings`' `feed-token` action, the `Settings` tab's Feed Token). It takes the place of the month's Get Feed.

In every list an event is a row: its hours, its title, the day type it imposes when it does, its place, and who it is for as color - the Config sheet's classroom colors (`docs/config.md`), one dot per color at the row's end and a stripe down its left, and nothing at all for an event that is everyone's. The classroom chips in the filters wear the same colors. Where an event is one thing - the month's pills, the rail's timeline, the upcoming rows, the small month's dots - it wears one color (`eventTint`): its first classroom's, or for an event that is everyone's, one for where it comes from - Celebrate's parties pink, HCA's events purple, the school's own blue. Tags show only on the event page.

What the other apps run is on the calendar too, read from their models at request time rather than from the sheet, so a party posted or an event filled is on the calendar the moment it is there (`LinkedEvents` in `internal/model/linked_events.go`, fed by `Linked` in `internal/model/parties_linked.go` and `internal/model/activities_linked.go`). Helios Celebrate's parties (`docs/celebrate/celebrate.md`): every open party with a date, tagged Celebrate. HCA-Team's events (`docs/team/team.md`): every dated root event that is open or done, tagged HCA; the things under an event stay off, since the event stands for them. The two tags are built in, known to the code by constant IDs, each with its row in the `Tags` tab like any other so the filters can switch it off (`data.md`, Tags and day types by ID). A linked event is everyone's, so it shows whatever the classroom filter says. An HCA event the school's calendar also lists is one row, not two: when the two fall on the same day with hours that overlap (or either is whole-day) and their titles agree once filler, the association's and the school's names, and years are dropped - one title's words all in the other's, or at least half the words between them shared - the school's event stands, with its tags and audience, and gains the HCA tag, the pill, the way to sign up, HCA-Team's picture, the longer of the two descriptions, and HCA-Team's place where the school's feed gives none. A pair the rule misses shows twice, and an Overrides row hiding the school's is the fix; a pair it wrongly joins is told apart by retitling one. Its row carries a pill saying what a reader can do there now - Get tickets, Join the waitlist, Sold out for a party; Join or Full for an HCA event - that goes straight to its page on the app that runs it, on the page's own tier (`appOrigin` in `web/common/appswitch.js`); one that has passed, is done, or has closed its sales has no pill. Its event page leads the rail with the same call as a button and says where it comes from; a party's description is its summary and description. Linked events are in the personal feeds too, read for the feed's owner (`ICS` takes the owner's linked list), so a feed on Celebrate carries the parties and one on Going is the family's own calendar in their own calendar app.

A linked event is also read for the viewer (`Linked` on `model.Parties` and `model.Activities`, given the household `Directory.HouseholdOf` in `internal/model/household.go` reads for the signed-in address): a party someone in their household bought or holds a ticket to is theirs, Going; one the household has only asked for a place at is Waitlisted; an HCA event someone in the household signed up for, on the event or anything under it, is Going. The household is the viewer and everyone in their families as the directory lists them, the same reckoning Celebrate's My Family's Parties and HCA-Team's own sign-ups use. Such an event carries the standing as a tag beside Celebrate or HCA, so the filters narrow the calendar to what the family is in, and its pill wears the standing in amber with a check in place of the way in, still leading to the page - You have a ticket, Signed up, Waitlisted when the viewer is among those it belongs to, else the household members it does belong to by first name ("Sam has a ticket", "Sam and Ella have tickets", "You have a ticket", "Sam and Ella are waitlisted", "Sam signed up"; a ticket names its holder, a guest still to be named is the buyer's); the event page's rail says the same and offers the page.

Search is the top bar's box, as in the other apps; `/` puts the cursor in it. It matches every word typed against the title, the description, the place, the tags, and the hidden keywords the classifier wrote, so "half day" finds an early dismissal and "coffee" finds a CAFE, and it filters the whole page at once: the day's events, the small month's dots, the Upcoming panel - headed Matches, the whole year's from today - and the month's pills. The schedule is searched too, by day-type name: while there are words, the day plan and the month's pills stay only for a day type the words name, so "early" keeps the early dismissals and "coffee" leaves nothing but the coffee. So it is plain why the page shows less, an amber band across its top says what is being matched, with Clear search, and the top bar's box wears the same amber. Typing on any other page comes back to the calendar with the words.

## The front page

Heliosian's Upcoming widget and the calendar in its rail are the calendar's too (`docs/home/home.md`), read from the resource API: `events?from=&to=&calendar=` lists the viewer's events that touch the dates under one of their calendars - its classrooms and tags, as `InView` in `internal/model/calendar_upcoming.go` admits an event, the linked events folded in - each with its page here and which app serves it, and for a linked event its way in and the words its pill would carry, so a row on the front page says what a row here says; the calendar's `days` (`calendar-feeds`, `dayKinds` in `calendar_month.go`) gives every school day with the day types in force for that calendar's classrooms other than Regular, worded as the day words are (`docs/api.md`, Helios When).

## Sharing an event

Anyone can put an event on the calendar: **Add Event**, over the month beside Today, opens the same form an admin's Add Event does - the event (its title, when - a start time sets the end two hours on until the end is set by hand - where, a More Information or Source address and the words) and, for a public event, a second tab, Who: its classrooms, categories and search words. An event that is not public has no second tab and no tags at all - it is for whoever is sent it, wears no audience chip, and is filed under nothing, not even Misc; the inviting happens on its page once it exists (Invitations, below). Added, the event's own page opens, where its link and its guest list are. The form's first choice is who can find it - **Invite only** (the default), **Anyone with the link** or **Public**, the `Events` row's `Sharing` (Who can see an event, below) - and under it an optional web address, `/e/{address}`, a name of the host's own (3 to 40 letters, digits and dashes - `address` on the add, refused if it is another event's ID or address; set once), which the server writes to the event's `Overrides` row as its Address (`data.md`). The event's ID is the server's, never the form's: every event the `Events` tab gets is minted one (`internal/id`), and `/e/{id}` opens it whether or not it has an address. A public one adds a row to the `Events` tab under their name with `Status` Pending (`POST /api/events`, `sharing` one of the three words; repeats and a day type are an admin's alone) - an admin's own waits for approval like anyone's. A pending event is off the calendar, the feeds and the front page for everyone else - though its direct link works right away, as a link event's does, for anyone it is sent to, whose answer puts it on their calendar; the person who shared it and the admins see it dashed in amber on the month, the timeline and Upcoming, and its page opens with a Waiting for approval band - for an admin, with **Approve** (the event's `approve` action, `Status` Approved) and **Decline** (the `decline` action, `Status` Declined, refused for an event that is not public: the row kept, the event off the calendar and dashed in red for the same two, its link working as a waiting one's does, its band saying so, and Approve still offered). Admin Tools' Events list keeps the waiting and the declined ones at its head with the same buttons, and an amber badge in the top bar counts the waiting ones for the admins - its card a chip per event, each opening the event's page to approve or decline it there, and a line to Admin Tools. When anyone adds an event - whichever way it is shared, an admin's own included - the admins get one email (`tellAdmins`, through `CalendarMail`, every admin on the To line): who, what and when, and a button to its page - Review the event for one waiting for approval, See the event for a link or invite-only one, which needs nothing of them. A **link** or **invite-only** event (`Sharing` Link or Invite Only, `Status` blank) needs no approval and is on nobody's calendar to start: its page is opened as Who can see an event says (`GET /api/events/{id}` hands the page an event the calendar's list, `/api/events`, does not carry), its page previews and its share card draws for a link sent anywhere (the card finds every event by id; [issue 32](https://github.com/heliosian/heliosian/issues/32) says what that gives away), its page opens with an Anyone with the link or Invite only band and Copy link, whoever answers it - Yes, Maybe or No - has it on their calendar from then on (`eventsFor` folds in the off-calendar events the person has answered), a yes under Going and across classrooms (an event the viewer said yes to passes the classroom filter so long as Going is on - on the page and in Upcoming alike), and so does everyone the host invites from its page once the invites go out (Invitations, below). The sharing can be switched on the event later: turning an event public puts it up for approval, an admin's own too - the admins told by email again, the link still working in the meantime - and turning a public one away from public ends its approval (`Status` cleared). Whoever shares an event is going to it - a yes recorded as it is added, with no invite mailed back to them - and sees who answered it on its page, the way an admin does (`responses` in the view carries their own events' answers), and corrects it from **Edit event** at the page's top, or its picture from the strip across the banner's foot (Add or Replace image - an upload or the libraries - and Remove, each saved at once, as the other apps' pages have it) - the same form, filled in, saved with the event's `edit` action, which writes only the fields sent (the poster's or an admin's; an approved event stays approved, each changed column logged). The form asks for no picture: the picture is the banner's strip alone - the picker the categories use, an upload into the shared store (`POST /api/when/image`), a search of the picture libraries (`/api/when/images/search` and `import`), or none - open to anyone signed in; the row's `Image` cell names the picture; the page and the share card wear it ahead of the first category's.

## Who can see an event

An event is open to someone or it is not, and whoever can open it gets the whole of it - the words, the place, the picture, and, unless the hosts keep it to themselves (Permissions, under Invitations), the guest list with everyone's answers but the nos, which are the hosts' alone as a matter of manners rather than privacy (`InviteView.Coming`; the hosts'-side columns - tickets, sending, an address's trouble, who answered for whom, an outside person's own link - stay the hosts'). No visibility turns on the viewer's own answer. A hand-added event's `Sharing` says who that is (`sees` in `internal/model/rsvp.go`, which every route resolving an event by id goes through - `eventFor`):

- **Public** - everyone, once an admin has approved it; while it waits, once it is declined, or once it is cancelled, anyone with its link.
- **Anyone with the link** (`Link`) - anyone holding `/e/{id}`, who can pass it on; the link lets its bearer see the event and answer it, not invite others in the hosts' name.
- **Invite only** - whoever is on its guest list, sent their invitation or not, and a parent of a student on it (`Calendar.Listed`; anything a student can do, their parent can do); to anyone else, the page, the guest list and the RSVP answer 404 whether or not they hold the id.

The hosts and the calendar's admins open every event. Someone from outside Helios is never sent `/e/{id}`; their `/ext/{token}` page (Invitations, below) is opened by the token their row on the list carries, which is proof enough of being invited, and shows them the event, the hosts and their own household - not the guest list, since the directory's names and faces stay behind sign-in. An event another app runs - a party on Celebrate, an HCA event - is public here, its own app saying who may act on it.

Every event takes the viewer's word - on its page here, in a green band under the event's body with **Are you going?** beside a ticked calendar - which, once a yes, maybe or no is given, steps aside for a quieter **My RSVP** card in the rail under the Hosts, the answer behind its mark (You're going, Maybe, Not going) with Change response - which opens the three answers there, small, with Clear my RSVP - and Hide event under it, until the answer is cleared or the event hidden - on Heliosian's Upcoming Events cards in place of Add to Calendar, and on the month's hover card: **Yes** (outlined in green), **Maybe** (in amber, on the page and the hover card; Heliosian's cards say a maybe back but offer Yes and No) and **No** (in gray), the one given filled and ringed, small links under them on the page - **Clear my RSVP** once one is given, **Hide event** (the event shows on the month in gray), or **Show event** once hidden - and **Hide** on Heliosian's cards. A party has no yes or no: with a ticket in the household it offers **Add to my calendar**, which is a yes (the invite goes out), and without one **Add Ticket**, the way to the party page - unless a host has sent the household an invitation, when the page asks the household instead (Invitations, below). Above the buttons, a linked event names the household's part in it on one line behind a ticket or a people icon, a dot between the names - each ticket holder (a guest still to be named by the words on the ticket, a waitlist place, in parentheses), each volunteer with the thing under the event they signed up for or their position on it, in parentheses (`MinePeople` on the event, `people` on Heliosian's card) - in place of a You're going button. Heliosian's rail calendar carries the same Yes and No under each event of the day it shows. The word is kept in the `RSVPs` tab, one row per person and event, with who gave it when it was not the person themselves (`data.md`), and every list reads it: a yes, maybe or no marks the event where it is listed for them (`answer` on Heliosian's cards, `user.answers` in the view) - a maybe changing nothing else - a no also turns it to plain gray words on the month grid, fades it in the rail's timeline and keeps it out of their personal feeds, the way hiding does (it stays in their Upcoming lists, No filled, for a change of mind), and a yes files the event under Going for them - in the view, their feeds and their Upcoming, on a copy of the event (`eventsFor` in `internal/model/linked_events.go`), the way a ticket does - so the Going filter and a Going feed hold everything they are going to; hidden takes the event out of their Upcoming lists, here and on Heliosian, and out of their personal feeds, while it stays on the month grid as plain gray words rather than a pill, in the rail's timeline faded, and in the search. A yes also sends them a calendar invite by email - the event's words and its page, with an `.ics` (`METHOD:REQUEST`, the person as attendee, the same UID the feeds use so the two do not double) they accept into their own calendar; `CalendarMail`, from `calendarMailFrom` in `internal/app/environment.go`, sends it, the dev server writes it to the mail directory. An invitation's calendar file names a reply address of that copy's own, so the Accept or Decline a person taps in their calendar app comes back as their answer through `POST /hooks/replies/mime` (`docs/datamodel.md`, Guest lists; `docs/deploy.md` says what receiving it takes); a reply from anyone but that person's own authenticated address, or to anything but a copy's reply address, is logged and dropped. The event's `answer` action (`{answer}`), which Heliosian's cards send too, writes through `answerOps`; answer yes, no, maybe, hidden, or blank to clear. A yes sends no invite to someone whose invitation already carried one. **My Events** (`/mine`, the rail's second page, between Calendar and Feeds, and on the phone's tab bar) is the viewer's own standing with what is coming up, a group per standing: Waiting for your reply, the invitations sent to their household they have not answered; Hosting, the events they run; Going, the rest they said yes to - each event a row to its page. The rail lists the first six under the page's name, in that order and each by date - a clock at the end for a reply owed, a star for one they host, a check for a yes - and a count of the rest. An event the viewer hosts - one they shared, a party they host on Celebrate, a guest list they co-host, never one they merely admin - wears an amber star before its title there, on the page and in Upcoming Events (`hosted` on the event, set for the viewer alone).

## Invitations

Every event, party and activity has a guest list - an event's the people put on it, a party's its ticket holders, an activity's its sign-ups - built in one step and sent in another, so the list is finished before anyone hears of it. How it is kept, sent and answered is the data model's (`docs/datamodel.md`, Guest lists); the page is `web/when/invites.js` over `web/when/guestlist.js`. Its **hosts** are the event's managers: the **Hosts** card in the rail shows them to everyone who can open the event, and for a host carries Add co-host (who hears of it by email), a cross on each host to take them off or step down, and **Hide hosts**, which keeps the card to the hosts. A host's **Guest list** card in the rail counts the list - invited, yes, no, no reply yet, unsent (pending) and, once the invitation is out, opened - each opening the people it counts; **See table** opens it in the shared data grid; **Send** offers the pending invitations, an RSVP reminder, an event reminder and a message, the reminders with the calendar invite attached; the gear opens **Guest List Settings**: Guests, the list's rules in the editor Loop uses (`web/common/listrules.js`) beside everyone on it with why, and Settings, four switches - Show the guest list to everyone, People can invite others, Send new guests their invitation (`auto_invite`) and Notify me when people respond. **Add People** adds from the directory, by a rule (Add Group, the hosts' alone) or from outside by family, everyone from outside answering for their family on their own page. The **Pending** band over Who's coming sends everyone pending at once, or chosen people from More info. Anyone a host lets invite others adds people through the same picker, sent their invitation at once. **Cancel event** on a hand-added event, once the invitation is out, sets it cancelled and, if asked, mails everyone sent it the note and a calendar cancellation; before then **Delete event** closes it. Once the invitation is out, changing the title, the time or the place offers to send everyone but those who said no the updated invitation. Someone from outside is sent `/ext/{token}`, a page of their own with the event, who invites them, their answer, their family's and their guests', and nothing of anyone else.
Whoever is on a list the household is invited to sees, on the event's page, a band asking the household: a row for the viewer and each of their family on the list, each with Yes and No, and once everyone has answered the band leaves the page's head for **My RSVP** in the rail, where each answer is changed in place, the guests the household brought have a cross to take them back, and **Add a guest** brings someone from Helios or someone else by name and address, answered Yes and sent their own invitation when asked. **Who's coming** takes the page's width under it: the faces under Yes, No response and, for the hosts, No and Pending, grouped by family, with the shared filters (`listFilters` in `web/when/guesttable.js`), each opening a card where a host corrects the answer, sends a pending invitation or takes the person off the list. A party's faces carry, for a host, a mark for a bought ticket or a free place. The page reads all of it from the data model (`fetchInvites` in `web/when/guestlist.js`) as the viewer may see it, and notes when the viewer first opens an invitation.

## Sharing

A link to an event pasted into a chat app previews the way HCA-Team's and Celebrate's do (`WhenPages` in `internal/db/whenpages.go`, on the shared drawer `internal/sharecard`): the sign-in page served at the event's address (`/e/` or `/events/`, then its ID, slug or alias) carries Open Graph tags - the title, the day and hours, the place, and the description cut short - and names a card at `/open/share/{id}.png`, drawn with the mark and wordmark, the topmost category above it as a kicker, the title, the lines, a button in the brand teal saying what the page asks - RSVP for an event, Sign Up for an activity, Get Tickets for a party - and its flyer whole, else the first picture of it or of the nearest group above it that has one, else the calendar's header. Every event, party and activity has its card and preview, invite-only and pending ones too, with no check of who may see it (`docs/dev.md`). An outside guest's `/ext/{token}` page carries the same preview, and the invitation mail and every other mail about the event show the card at their head, linked to the page. A link to any other page - the calendar, a saved calendar's `/c/` address, the feeds - previews Helios When itself: a line on what it is in words, and a card at `/open/share/upcoming.png` that says so in three lines (school days and dismissals, events and sign-ups, calendars to save and subscribe to) with an Open Helios When button, over the calendar's own header picture. The cards are public, as every `/open/share/` path is, and carry only what the school's own calendar says.

## Feeds

A feed is an address a calendar app subscribes to: `https://when.heliosian.com/open/feed/{token}.ics`, served without sign-in (`auth.Public`), since Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and Outlook fetch it themselves. The token is a saved search's `SAVED_SEARCH.token` (`docs/datamodel.md`, IDs), or an old feed token a hidden `ALIAS` row names it by; it is the whole secret, so anyone holding the address reads the feed, and removing the calendar from the rail is how it stops.

The rail's nav lists the viewer's calendars under Calendar: **My Heliosian** - the calendar's own view (their classrooms and the default categories; a view saved earlier in their place still stands until forgotten), which everyone has and nobody removes or refilters - a lock beside its headline, and in place of the cross while editing, says so - though each person may rename it, mark it and drag it among their own (the `Settings` tab's Home Name, Home Emoji and Home Position; `user.home` in the view, `Calendar.MyHeliosian`) - first to start (`MyHeliosianToken` stands for it where a saved calendar's token would) - then their own saved feeds in their Order (`data.md`). The first of them is their default calendar - My Heliosian to start; Make Default (`when-settings`' `default` action with a token, My Heliosian's included) moves one to the top, and dragging one to the top is the same - with a star at its end, a Default Calendar badge at the headline's end (any other calendar's headline ends in a faint Make Default instead), and it is the view the page opens to and Reset filters returns to (`ViewOf`), and the one Heliosian's widgets are first read under - Heliosian names it beside the Upcoming heading and at the head of its rail's month as a quiet dropdown of My Heliosian and every saved calendar (`/api/calendar-feeds`), the default marked: picking another re-reads that one under it (`events?calendar=`, `docs/api.md`) until the page loads again. A pencil at Calendar's end turns on, beside each, a grip to drag it into a new order (the drop saves it: `when-settings`' `order-feeds` action with the tokens, My Heliosian's among them setting its place, the saved ones given Order sort keys for the new order, only those that moved written), a pencil (the name-and-emoji popup, with Make default and Delete calendar at its foot - no Delete for My Heliosian) and a cross (remove, after a confirm; a lock in its place for My Heliosian), until it is pressed again: each opens the calendar with the filters set to what that feed carries, at `/c/{token}` (an address that opens it the same way), and while the viewer works from it its name heads the page over a swoosh - the name itself dropping a menu of every calendar to switch by, which is how a phone, with no rail, switches - lit in the rail while the filters are its own, amber with a "Filters changed · not saved" word beside the headline once they have moved, since Save Calendar saves back onto it - behind its mark - which, clicked, opens a small popup to change the name and emoji - and Subscribe beside its name puts it into a calendar app (Pages, above). A viewer makes one with Save Calendar beside the month, naming it (it starts as "Sam's Heliosian Calendar", their own first name on it; a name one of their feeds already has gets the first free number after it, "… 2", on the page and again on the server) and picking its classrooms (their own to start) and tags (all to start); every classroom or every tag is stored as no filter at all, so a whole-school feed keeps up as classrooms come and go. It lands in the `Feeds` tab with the viewer's address and is theirs alone, marked by the emoji the owner picked, else the calendar icon, and only they, or an admin, can change or remove it. Saving the filters back onto it keeps them in place (`POST /api/calendar-feeds/{id}/edit`) at the same address, so a calendar app subscribed to it carries the new choice from its next refresh.

The document (`RegisterFeeds` in `internal/db/savedsearch.go`) runs the saved search's words as its owner (`docs/datamodel.md`, Saved searches), so it carries what their saved calendar shows them, less what they hid: every event, party and activity that is not closed, from a year back to two years ahead, all-day ones as dates with the exclusive end iCalendar wants, timed ones in the school's zone, the description followed by the thing's page, and that page as its URL. It asks the client to refresh hourly; Google refreshes on its own schedule, every several hours. The day plan itself is not in the feed: the departures from a regular day are events already, and a regular day is not.

## What it does not do yet

Nothing is edited in the app: the `Events`, `Overrides`, and `Day Overrides` tabs are the input surface, and the `Admins` tab names who will get the editor when it comes. Notifications - a note the week before a short day, an event that moved - are not built; the Change Log (`docs/storage.md`) holds every change with what it replaced, for one to read.
