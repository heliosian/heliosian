# Helios Calendar

Helios Calendar is the school year as one family sees it: what kind of day today is for their students' classrooms, what is coming up, and the whole year to browse, with the school's two published sources merged, classified, and corrected in one sheet. It lives at when.heliosian.com; calendar.heliosian.com and cal.heliosian.com are ways of typing it, and a page load at either is sent there (`canonicalHost` in `internal/app`), while a feed fetch or an API call is answered where it arrives, so a calendar subscribed at an older address keeps working. The sheet is `Calendar` (`data.md`), the package is `internal/calendar`, and the API is `/api/calendar/`.

## What a viewer sees

Every page is filtered by the top bar's search box (below) and by two choices, remembered per browser, that sit in a card above the month grid on the calendar page - folded to one line saying what they hold until opened, as labelled rows when open, the fold remembered per browser too - and in the drawer on a phone:

- **Classrooms** - which classrooms the pages are about. A parent starts with their children's classrooms, a student with their own, a classroom teacher with theirs, and anyone with none - the office, a specialist - with every classroom. Mine puts a parent's own back; All is the whole school. The chips sit band by band, in the classroom colors; a chip that is off is dashed and faded. An event shows when any of its classrooms is chosen; an event with no classroom at all shows to everyone.
- **Categories** - which tags, one line per group the `Tags` tab files them under (`data.md`), each line's tags in alphabetical order with All and None for that line alone; tags with no group are the plain Categories line at the end. A tag starts on unless the `Tags` tab's Default column (set from Admin Tools) says otherwise, and Reset filters returns to that set; a line's None clears it for a quick look at one kind of thing. An event shows when any of its category tags is chosen; the classrooms among its tags are the audience, not a category. After the sheet's tags come five the app supplies, on the Other line: Celebrate and HCA for what the other apps run (below), Going and Waitlisted for where the viewer's own household stands with those, and Misc for an event the sheet gave classrooms but no category (`data.md`). Every line's None, then Going, is the family's own calendar.

The classrooms and tags are the sheet's live vocabulary, so a renamed classroom drops out of a remembered filter rather than hiding everything. The feeds form picks classrooms and categories with the same chips, the categories on the same lines.

## Pages

One shell, `web/calendar/index.html`, routes every page on the client, under the shared top bar (`docs/toolbar.md`) beside a rail of the two pages and, under them, the day (`web/calendar/day.js`, drawn there by `chrome.js` for every page): a small month to pick a day from, paged on its own, the days of the months either side faded at its ends, a dot under each day in the color of what is on it, and the day last opened on the calendar page (today until one is) as a card. On a phone the rail becomes a drawer of the two pages and the filters, the day heads the calendar page instead, and a bottom tab bar holds the two pages and a way into the filters.

- **Calendar** (`/`, or `/day/{date}` for any other day) - the chosen day in the rail, and the page itself in three parts. The day card: Today, or the weekday, over the date; then the day plan for the chosen classrooms as one card per day type in force - its name, the classrooms it covers when they are not all of them, and the four blocks, Dropoff, School, Pickup, Aftercare, with their hours, a block the day does not have greyed - a weekend or a day outside the school year saying so instead; then the day's events down a timeline, each a dot in its color, its hours, its title and its place. The page: the filters first, then the month grid, paged in place with Today at the row's end to come back: a day that is not regular wears its day type as a pill in its wash, with a count when it is only some of the classrooms, then the first few events as pills in a wash of their color, title over hours (the hours dropped when the cells are narrow); today is ringed in amber and the day open in the rail in teal, days outside the school year are washed, a cell opens that day in the rail and a pill in it opens that event, and while the pointer rests on a cell a card opens under it with the whole day - its name, its kind, and every event in full with its hours, place and classroom colors, each a link to the event. To the month's right, when the window is wide enough, Upcoming Events in a panel as tall as the month that scrolls on its own: one row per event from the day shown on, that day's own first - the date at the left, a bar and a dot in the event's color, the title, hours, place, and for a linked event the way to it - the first two weeks under Upcoming and the rest under Later This Year; a day that is not regular for the chosen classrooms and has no event of its own is a row too, in its day type's color, while the Schedule tag is on. Narrower windows put Upcoming under the month; a phone puts the day card first, then Upcoming, then the month.
- **Admin Tools** (`/admin`, calendar admins only, reached from the account menu under the avatar) - what admins change without opening the sheet. Categories: the tags as cards, one per group in the filters' order - Add category group makes an empty one, kept only once a tag is in it - each card's head with the name (Rename group makes it a box; renaming renames the group for every tag in it), how many it holds, arrows to move the group and a chevron to fold it; and a row per tag - a handle to drag it before or after another row, or onto a group's head to join that group, its description (a box on focus), whether it is on by default, and a picker to move it to another group, an unnamed one, or a new one named on the spot - then a form to add a category (name, description, group), and Save categories, which sends the whole list in order (`POST /api/calendar/tags`): a changed description, group or default is written to its row, a new tag appended, and the tab's rows put in the order shown, each change in the Change Log. A tag's name cannot change and no tag can be removed from here, since events carry tags by name; the built-in tags are named under their fixed group rather than listed.
- **Event** (`/events/{id}`) - the chips (the day type when it carries one, then who it is for and what it is), the title, when, the description with its links live, the day type's blocks when it changes the day, a rail with the date and Add to Google Calendar, the place, where the event came from, and Copy link. Who an event is for is compressed: every classroom is Everyone, both classrooms of a band are the band, the rest are named.
- **Feeds** (`/feeds`) - the viewer's personal calendar feeds and a form for a new one, below.

In every list an event is a row: its hours, its title, the day type it imposes when it does, its place, and who it is for as color - the Config sheet's classroom colors (`docs/config.md`), one dot per color at the row's end and a stripe down its left, and nothing at all for an event that is everyone's. The classroom chips in the filters wear the same colors. Where an event is one thing - the month's pills, the rail's timeline, the upcoming rows, the small month's dots - it wears one color (`eventTint`): its first classroom's, or for an event that is everyone's, one for where it comes from - Celebrate's parties pink, HCA's events purple, the school's own blue. Tags show only on the event page.

What the other apps run is on the calendar too, read from their models at request time rather than from the sheet, so a party posted or an event filled is on the calendar the moment it is there (`internal/calendar/linked.go`, fed by `internal/app/calendar.go`). Helios Celebrate's parties (`docs/celebrate/celebrate.md`): every open party with a date, tagged Celebrate. HCA-Team's events (`docs/events/events.md`): every dated root event that is open or done, tagged HCA; the things under an event stay off, since the event stands for them. The two tags are built in, carried by every view beside the sheet's own vocabulary so the filters can switch each off, and no sheet row names them. A linked event is everyone's, so it shows whatever the classroom filter says. An HCA event the school's calendar also lists is one row, not two: when the two fall on the same day with hours that overlap (or either is whole-day) and their titles agree once filler, the association's and the school's names, and years are dropped - one title's words all in the other's, or at least half the words between them shared - the school's event stands, with its tags and audience, and gains the HCA tag, the pill, and the way to sign up. A pair the rule misses shows twice, and an Overrides row hiding the school's is the fix; a pair it wrongly joins is told apart by retitling one. Its row carries a pill saying what a reader can do there now - Get tickets, Join the waitlist, Sold out for a party; Join or Full for an HCA event - that goes straight to its page on the app that runs it, on the page's own tier (`web/common/toolbar.js`); one that has passed, is done, or has closed its sales has no pill. Its event page leads the rail with the same call as a button and says where it comes from; a party's description is its summary and description. Linked events are not in the personal feeds.

A linked event is also read for the viewer (`calendarLinked` in `internal/app/calendar.go`, given the signed-in address): a party someone in their household bought or holds a ticket to is theirs, Going; one the household has only asked for a place at is Waitlisted; an HCA event someone in the household signed up for, on the event or anything under it, is Going. The household is the viewer and everyone in their families as the directory lists them, the same reckoning Celebrate's My Family's Parties and HCA-Team's own sign-ups use. Such an event carries the standing as a tag beside Celebrate or HCA, so the filters narrow the calendar to what the family is in, and its pill wears the standing in amber with a check - You're going, Signed up, Waitlisted - in place of the way in, still leading to the page; the event page's rail says the same and offers the page.

Search is the top bar's box, as in the other apps; `/` puts the cursor in it. It matches every word typed against the title, the description, the place, the tags, and the hidden keywords the classifier wrote, so "half day" finds an early dismissal and "coffee" finds a CAFE, and it filters the whole page at once: the day's events, the small month's dots, the Upcoming panel - headed Matches, the whole year's from today - and the month's pills. The schedule is searched too, by day-type name: while there are words, the day plan and the month's pills stay only for a day type the words name, so "early" keeps the early dismissals and "coffee" leaves nothing but the coffee. So it is plain why the page shows less, an amber band across its top says what is being matched, with Clear search, and the top bar's box wears the same amber. Typing on any other page comes back to the calendar with the words.

## Feeds

A feed is an address a calendar app subscribes to: `https://when.heliosian.com/feed/{token}.ics`, served without sign-in (`auth.Public`), since Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and Outlook fetch it themselves. The token is the whole secret - 24 characters from a 31-symbol alphabet, minted by the server - so the page says so: anyone holding the address reads the feed, and removing it here is how it stops.

A viewer makes one from the Feeds page by naming it and picking its classrooms (their own to start) and tags (all to start); every classroom or every tag is stored as no filter at all, so a whole-school feed keeps up as classrooms come and go. It lands in the `Feeds` tab with the viewer's address and is theirs alone: the page lists only their own, and only they, or an admin, can remove it. The address is copied to the clipboard the moment it is made. Subscribe in my calendar app is the same address as `webcal://`, which Apple's and most others open straight into a subscription; Add to Google Calendar opens Google's From URL page to paste it into.

The document (`internal/calendar/feed.go`) carries every visible event the feed's filter admits: all-day events as dates with the exclusive end iCalendar wants, timed events in UTC with the school's zone named on the calendar, the tags as categories, the day type at the head of the description, and each event's page as its URL. It asks the client to refresh hourly; Google refreshes on its own schedule, every several hours. The day plan itself is not in the feed: the departures from a regular day are events already, and a regular day is not.

## What it does not do yet

Nothing is edited in the app: the `Events`, `Overrides`, and `Day Overrides` tabs are the input surface, and the `Admins` tab names who will get the editor when it comes. Notifications - a note the week before a short day, an event that moved - are not built; the Change Log the import keeps is shaped for one.

## Sign-in and install

Everything sits behind Google sign-in restricted to the school domain, sharing a session with the other apps on the same tier. It installs to a home screen with the calendar-with-sun-and-leaf mark under `web/public/calendar/brand/`, cut from the Helios When exports in `~/Dropbox/Kids/Heliosian/images/when/` (`Helios When Logo.psd` is the master) on the recipe the other apps use: `appicon.png` for the home-screen icons, the bare mark (`favicon.png` there) for the favicons, `logo-mark.png` (the drawer's head), the app switch row (`web/public/common/brand/apps/calendar.png`) and the maskables at 62% on the icon's own pale ground; the teal long horizontal lockup, name and tagline on two lines beside the mark, for the rail (`logo-lockup-wide.png`, from `- long horizontal.png`, 900px wide, up to 310px on screen) and the white vertical one for the login page (`logo-lockup-light.png`); the vertical teal, the two-line horizontals and the white long horizontal (`logo-lockup-light-wide.png`) unused so far. There is no splash battery yet. The brand teal is the art's `#0e4d54`.
