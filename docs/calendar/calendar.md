# Helios Calendar

Helios Calendar is the school year as one family sees it: what kind of day today is for their students' classrooms, what is coming up, and the whole year to browse, with the school's two published sources merged, classified, and corrected in one sheet. It lives at when.heliosian.com; calendar.heliosian.com and cal.heliosian.com are ways of typing it, and a page load at either is sent there (`canonicalHost` in `internal/app`), while a feed fetch or an API call is answered where it arrives, so a calendar subscribed at an older address keeps working. The sheet is `Calendar` (`data.md`), the package is `internal/calendar`, and the API is `/api/calendar/`.

## What a viewer sees

Every page is filtered by the top bar's search box (below) and by two choices, remembered per browser, that sit in a card above the month grid on the calendar page - folded to one line on every visit naming what is in view - All classrooms or the classrooms by name, then each category line as All School day or its chosen names, labelled rows when unfolded, and under the rows Select all (every classroom and category on), Clear all (every category off), Reset filters (back to the calendar being worked from), each only while it would change something, and Forget my saved view while My Heliosian carries one - the same card on a phone, which the tab bar's Filters unfolds and scrolls to:

- **Classrooms** - which classrooms the pages are about. A parent starts with their children's classrooms, a student with their own, a classroom teacher with theirs, and anyone with none - the office, a specialist - with every classroom. Mine puts a parent's own back; All is the whole school. The chips sit band by band, in the classroom colors; a chip that is off is dashed and faded. An event shows when any of its classrooms is chosen; an event with no classroom at all shows to everyone.
- **Categories** - which tags, one line per group the `Tags` tab files them under (`data.md`), each line's tags in alphabetical order with All and None for that line alone; tags with no group are the plain Categories line at the end. A tag starts on unless the `Tags` tab's Default column (set from Admin Tools) says otherwise, and Reset filters returns to that set; a line's None clears it for a quick look at one kind of thing. An event shows when any of its category tags is chosen; the classrooms among its tags are the audience, not a category. After the sheet's tags come five the app supplies, on the Other line: Celebrate and HCA for what the other apps run (below), Going and Waitlisted for where the viewer's own household stands with those (Going also holds every event they said yes to), and Misc for an event the sheet gave classrooms but no category (`data.md`). Every line's None, then Going, is the family's own calendar.

A saved view (`Settings` tab, one row per address: `POST /api/calendar/settings`, `DELETE` to forget it) stands as the person's own defaults: the calendar opens to it on every device, Reset filters returns to it, the folded head says "your saved view", and Heliosian's Upcoming Events are read under it in place of the person's classrooms and the default categories (`Model.Upcoming`). The page no longer offers to save one - a saved calendar is the way - but one saved earlier still stands inside My Heliosian, and Forget my saved view in the filters' footer lets it go. The classrooms and tags are the sheet's live vocabulary, so a renamed classroom drops out of a remembered filter, and of a saved view, rather than hiding everything. The feeds form picks classrooms and categories with the same chips, the categories on the same lines, the built-ins among them.

## Pages

One shell, `web/calendar/index.html`, routes every page on the client, under the shared top bar (`docs/toolbar.md`) beside a rail of the two pages and, under them, the day (`web/calendar/day.js`, drawn there by `chrome.js` for every page): a small month to pick a day from, paged on its own, the days of the months either side faded at its ends, a dot under each day in the color of what is on it, and the day last opened - on the calendar page, or an event's - as a card, today until one is. On a phone the rail becomes a drawer of the two pages and the saved calendars, the day heads the calendar page instead, the filters card stays on the page, and a bottom tab bar holds the two pages and Filters, which unfolds that card.

- **Calendar** (`/`, or `/day/{date}` for any other day) - the chosen day in the rail, and the page itself in three parts. The day card: Today, or the weekday, over the date; then the day plan for the chosen classrooms as one card per day type in force - its name, the classrooms it covers when they are not all of them, and the four blocks, Dropoff, School, Pickup, Aftercare, with their hours, a block the day does not have greyed - a weekend or a day outside the school year saying so instead; then the day's events down a timeline, each a dot in its color, its hours, its title and its place. The page: the filters first, then the month grid, paged in place with Today at the row's end (and Get Feed beside it while the filters are a saved calendar's) - Save Calendar, beside the headline's "Filters changed · not saved" once they have moved, keeps what the filters show as a saved calendar - a feed row - and is there only while the filters are not a saved calendar's already. Working from a saved calendar (the one last opened from the rail or matched on load, remembered with the filters) it is a split button: a click saves the filters back onto it, keeping its name, mark and address, and the caret drops a menu with that and Save New Calendar; working from My Heliosian, whose filters are locked, it is a plain Save New Calendar, which asks for a name in a small popup (the viewer's first name on it to start, a number after it if taken) and lists the new calendar under Calendar in the rail from then on, with no address handed out until wanted: a day that is not regular wears its day type as a pill in its wash, with a count when it is only some of the classrooms, then the first few events as pills in a wash of their color, title over hours (the hours dropped when the cells are narrow); today is ringed in amber and the day open in the rail in teal, days outside the school year are washed, a cell opens that day in the rail and a pill in it opens that event, and while the pointer rests on a cell a card opens under it with the day - its name and its plan with the hours - and, over one of the cell's pills, that event alone: its hours, title, place and description, the household's part in it, and Yes and No (or a party's Send Invite / Add Ticket) with a small Hide event under them, which answer without leaving the month. The card touches the cell's bottom edge (or its top, when there is no room below), reaches a little past its own edges, and keeps while the pointer is on it or for most of a second after it leaves - the cell's own ground under a pill, and the neighbouring cells, hold the card that long too - so the pointer can cross from a pill to the card's buttons without it changing or closing. To the month's right, when the window is wide enough, Upcoming Events in a panel as tall as the month that scrolls on its own: one row per event from the day shown on, that day's own first - the date at the left, a bar and a dot in the event's color, the title, hours, place, and for a linked event the way to it - the first two weeks under Upcoming and the rest under Later This Year; a day that is not regular for the chosen classrooms and has no event of its own is a row too, in its day type's color, while the Schedule tag is on. Narrower windows put Upcoming under the month; a phone puts the day card first, then Upcoming, then the month.
- **Admin Tools** (`/admin`, calendar admins only, reached from the account menu under the avatar) - its own window over the shell, on the chrome the other apps' admin pages share (`web/common/admin.css`): a teal header with the tile, Admin, the address and a close button, a rail of tabs, a card per tool. Events: the hand-added events as a list, soonest first, past ones kept out unless asked for and faded then, under filters - words in the title, a category, a classroom, a span of dates - with how many match, each with its start and end as date and time pickers that offer Save once changed (`POST /api/calendar/events/when`, the `Events` tab row's Start and End; an imported event is corrected in Overrides instead) and a copy button; Add Event opens a form in a sheet that puts an event in the `Events` tab under the admin's name - an Event tab with the title, start and end (a date each, and a time unless it is all day), location, description and source, and a Tags tab with the classrooms it is for, its categories and its search words (`POST /api/calendar/events`, the row checked by the sheet's rules before anything is written; the route also takes a repeat, every N weeks M more times, which the form does not offer); opened from a row's copy button (`/admin?clone={id}&weeks=4` opens it the same way), the form starts filled from that event with its dates moved on. Categories: the tags as cards, one per group in the filters' order - Add category group makes an empty one, kept only once a tag is in it - each card's head with the name (Rename group makes it a box; renaming renames the group for every tag in it), how many it holds, arrows to move the group and a chevron to fold it; and a row per tag - a handle to drag it before or after another row, or onto a group's head to join that group, its picture (a thumbnail that opens a file chooser, a magnifier that opens the picture search the other apps' editors have - `imagesearch`, on `/api/calendar/images/search` and `/import`, uploads on `/api/calendar/image`, all stored content addressed under `category-images/` - and a cross that takes it off), its description (a box on focus), whether it is on by default, and a picker to move it to another group, an unnamed one, or a new one named on the spot - then a form to add a category (name, description, group), and Save categories, which sends the whole list in order (`POST /api/calendar/tags`): a changed description, group, default or image is written to its row, a new tag appended, and the tab's rows put in the order shown, each change in the Change Log. A tag's name cannot change and no tag can be removed from here, since events carry tags by name; the built-in tags are named under their fixed group rather than listed.
- **Event** (`/e/{id}`; `/events/{id}` still opens it, for links in the wild) - a picture across the top with the date on a card at its corner (the weekday, the day, the hours), then the chips (the day type when it carries one, then who it is for, and for a hand-added event how it is shared - Public, Direct link only, Pending approval or Declined and what it is), the title, the description with its links live, the day type's blocks when it changes the day, and a rail with the date and Add to Google Calendar, the place, the way into the app that runs a linked event, and where the event's dates came from, linked to the original - the feed's event in Google Calendar (`GoogleEventURL`, the feed's calendar and the event's id as Google encodes them, an instance's start in UTC after an underscore), the school's calendar page for a line of the year calendar with the school year it was read from and the line as printed when the title has since changed, the page on the app that runs a party or an HCA event, or who added a hand-added event and when, with its Source cell as the proof - a web address linked and named by its host (Veracross's with a word about the parent login), other words as written; the school's listing of an HCA event links to HCA-Team's page too. A calendar admin also sees, under For admins, the classifier's filing (model and date), any correction the Overrides tab made (the columns and its note) - `Provenance` in the view, sent to admins alone - who answered the event - the yeses and the nos by name, with counts, never who hid it (`Responses` in the view, admins alone) - the event's search words with Edit search words, which writes them to the event's Overrides row (`POST /api/calendar/keywords`; an empty list is the clearing mark), and for a hand-added event Clone this event 4 weeks on; the rail's day jumps to the event's, so its month, plan and events sit beside the page. The picture is the event's own where the app that runs it has one (a party's, an HCA event's, fetched from that app on the page's own tier), else the picture of the first of its tags that has one in the order the event carries them (`eventImage` in `state.js`), else the calendar's own header (`web/public/calendar/brand/default-header.jpg`, from `default_header.png` in the exports). Who an event is for is compressed: every classroom is Everyone, both classrooms of a band are the band, the rest are named.
- **Feeds** (`/feeds`) - the viewer's personal calendar feeds and a form for a new one, below.

In every list an event is a row: its hours, its title, the day type it imposes when it does, its place, and who it is for as color - the Config sheet's classroom colors (`docs/config.md`), one dot per color at the row's end and a stripe down its left, and nothing at all for an event that is everyone's. The classroom chips in the filters wear the same colors. Where an event is one thing - the month's pills, the rail's timeline, the upcoming rows, the small month's dots - it wears one color (`eventTint`): its first classroom's, or for an event that is everyone's, one for where it comes from - Celebrate's parties pink, HCA's events purple, the school's own blue. Tags show only on the event page.

What the other apps run is on the calendar too, read from their models at request time rather than from the sheet, so a party posted or an event filled is on the calendar the moment it is there (`internal/calendar/linked.go`, fed by `internal/app/calendar.go`). Helios Celebrate's parties (`docs/celebrate/celebrate.md`): every open party with a date, tagged Celebrate. HCA-Team's events (`docs/events/events.md`): every dated root event that is open or done, tagged HCA; the things under an event stay off, since the event stands for them. The two tags are built in, carried by every view beside the sheet's own vocabulary so the filters can switch each off, and no sheet row names them. A linked event is everyone's, so it shows whatever the classroom filter says. An HCA event the school's calendar also lists is one row, not two: when the two fall on the same day with hours that overlap (or either is whole-day) and their titles agree once filler, the association's and the school's names, and years are dropped - one title's words all in the other's, or at least half the words between them shared - the school's event stands, with its tags and audience, and gains the HCA tag, the pill, the way to sign up, HCA-Team's picture, the longer of the two descriptions, and HCA-Team's place where the school's feed gives none. A pair the rule misses shows twice, and an Overrides row hiding the school's is the fix; a pair it wrongly joins is told apart by retitling one. Its row carries a pill saying what a reader can do there now - Get tickets, Join the waitlist, Sold out for a party; Join or Full for an HCA event - that goes straight to its page on the app that runs it, on the page's own tier (`web/common/toolbar.js`); one that has passed, is done, or has closed its sales has no pill. Its event page leads the rail with the same call as a button and says where it comes from; a party's description is its summary and description. Linked events are in the personal feeds too, read for the feed's owner (`ICS` takes the owner's linked list), so a feed on Celebrate carries the parties and one on Going is the family's own calendar in their own calendar app.

A linked event is also read for the viewer (`calendarLinked` in `internal/app/calendar.go`, given the signed-in address): a party someone in their household bought or holds a ticket to is theirs, Going; one the household has only asked for a place at is Waitlisted; an HCA event someone in the household signed up for, on the event or anything under it, is Going. The household is the viewer and everyone in their families as the directory lists them, the same reckoning Celebrate's My Family's Parties and HCA-Team's own sign-ups use. Such an event carries the standing as a tag beside Celebrate or HCA, so the filters narrow the calendar to what the family is in, and its pill wears the standing in amber with a check in place of the way in, still leading to the page - You're going, Signed up, Waitlisted when the viewer is among those it belongs to, else the household members it does belong to by first name ("Sam is going", "Sam and Ella are waitlisted", "Sam signed up"; a ticket names its holder, a guest still to be named is the buyer's); the event page's rail says the same and offers the page.

Search is the top bar's box, as in the other apps; `/` puts the cursor in it. It matches every word typed against the title, the description, the place, the tags, and the hidden keywords the classifier wrote, so "half day" finds an early dismissal and "coffee" finds a CAFE, and it filters the whole page at once: the day's events, the small month's dots, the Upcoming panel - headed Matches, the whole year's from today - and the month's pills. The schedule is searched too, by day-type name: while there are words, the day plan and the month's pills stay only for a day type the words name, so "early" keeps the early dismissals and "coffee" leaves nothing but the coffee. So it is plain why the page shows less, an amber band across its top says what is being matched, with Clear search, and the top bar's box wears the same amber. Typing on any other page comes back to the calendar with the words.

## The front page

Heliosian's Upcoming Events section and the calendar in its rail are the calendar's too (`docs/home/home.md`). `Model.Upcoming` in `internal/calendar/upcoming.go` lists, for the signed-in viewer, what the calendar page would first show them - from today on, admitted by their own classrooms and the categories on by default, the linked events folded in - the first six of them, each with its page here, the picture its page wears and which app serves it, and for a linked event its way in and the words its pill would carry, so a card on the front page says what a row here says. `Model.Month` in `month.go` reckons one month under the same filter for the rail's small month and day card: every school day in it with the day types in force for the viewer's classrooms other than Regular, worded as the day words are, and every event of theirs that touches the month, past days included.

## Sharing an event

Anyone can put an event on the calendar: **Share Event** under the day card (the rail's, or the phone's) opens the same two-tab form an admin's Add Event does - the event (its title, when - a start time sets the end two hours on until the end is set by hand - where, a More Information or Source address and the words), then its classrooms, categories and search words. The form's first choice is who can find it: **Direct link only** (the default) or **Public**; under it the event's web address, `/e/{id}`, a random id to start that the host may replace with a name of their own (3 to 40 letters, digits and dashes, free - `id` on the add, refused if taken; set once). A public one adds a row to the `Events` tab under their name with `Status` Pending (`POST /api/calendar/events`; repeats and a day type are an admin's alone) - an admin's own waits for approval like anyone's. A pending event is off the calendar, the feeds and the front page for everyone else - though its direct link works right away, as a direct-link event's does, for anyone it is sent to, whose answer puts it on their calendar; the person who shared it and the admins see it dashed in amber on the month, the timeline and Upcoming, and its page opens with a Waiting for approval band - for an admin, with **Approve** (`POST /api/calendar/events/approve`, `Status` Approved) and **Decline** (`POST /api/calendar/events/decline`, `Status` Declined: the row kept, the event off the calendar and dashed in red for the same two, its band saying so, and Approve still offered). Admin Tools' Events list keeps the waiting and the declined ones at its head with the same buttons, and an amber badge in the top bar counts the waiting ones for the admins - its card a chip per event, each opening the event's page to approve or decline it there, and a line to Admin Tools. When anyone adds an event - public or direct link only, an admin's own included - the admins get one email (`tellAdmins`, through `CalendarMail`, every admin on the To line): who, what and when, and a button to its page - Review the event for one waiting for approval, See the event for a direct-link one, which needs nothing of them. A **direct-link-only** event (`Status` Direct Link Only) needs no approval and is on nobody's calendar: it is found by its link alone (`GET /api/calendar/event?id=` hands the page an event the model does not carry), its page previews and its share card draws for a link sent anywhere (the card finds pending and direct-link events by id), its page opens with a Direct link only band and Copy link, and whoever answers it - Yes or No - has it on their calendar from then on (`eventsFor` folds in the invite-only events the person has answered), a yes under Going and across classrooms (an event the viewer said yes to passes the classroom filter so long as Going is on - on the page and in Upcoming alike). Public and direct link only can be switched on the event later: turning a direct-link event public puts it up for approval, an admin's own too - the admins told by email again, the link still working in the meantime. Whoever shares an event is going to it - a yes recorded as it is added, with no invite mailed back to them - and sees who answered it on its page, the way an admin does (`responses` in the view carries their own events' answers), and corrects it from **Edit event** at the page's top, or its picture from the strip across the banner's foot (Add or Replace image - an upload or the libraries - and Remove, each saved at once, as the other apps' pages have it) - the same form, filled in, saved with `PUT /api/calendar/events` (the poster's or an admin's; an approved event stays approved, each changed column logged). The form asks for no picture when an event is first posted; while editing, its Picture field is the picker the categories use - an upload into the shared store (`POST /api/calendar/image`), a search of the picture libraries (`/api/calendar/images/search` and `import`), or none - all open to anyone signed in; the row's `Image` cell names the picture; the page and the share card wear it ahead of the first category's.

## Answering

Every event takes the viewer's word - on its page here, in a green band under the event's body with **Are you going?** beside a ticked calendar, on Heliosian's Upcoming Events cards in place of Add to Calendar, and on the month's hover card: **Yes** (outlined in green) and **No** (in gray), the one given filled and ringed, small links under them on the page - **Clear my RSVP** once one is given, **Hide event** (the event shows on the month in gray), or **Show event** once hidden - and **Hide** on Heliosian's cards. A party has no yes or no: with a ticket in the household it offers **Send Invite**, which is a yes (the invite goes out), and without one **Add Ticket**, the way to the party page. Above the buttons, a linked event names the household's part in it on one line behind a ticket or a people icon, a dot between the names - each ticket holder (a guest still to be named by the words on the ticket, a waitlist place, in parentheses), each volunteer with the thing under the event they signed up for or their position on it, in parentheses (`MinePeople` on the event, `people` on Heliosian's card) - in place of a You're going button. Heliosian's rail calendar carries the same Yes and No under each event of the day it shows. The word is kept in the `RSVPs` tab, one row per person and event (`data.md`), and every list reads it: a yes or no marks the event where it is listed for them (`answer` on Heliosian's cards, `user.answers` in the view), a no also turns it to plain gray words on the month grid, fades it in the rail's timeline and keeps it out of their personal feeds, the way hiding does (it stays in their Upcoming lists, No filled, for a change of mind), and a yes files the event under Going for them - in the view, their feeds and their Upcoming, on a copy of the event (`eventsFor` in `internal/calendar/linked.go`), the way a ticket does - so the Going filter and a Going feed hold everything they are going to; hidden takes the event out of their Upcoming lists, here and on Heliosian, and out of their personal feeds, while it stays on the month grid as plain gray words rather than a pill, in the rail's timeline faded, and in the search. A yes also sends them a calendar invite by email - the event's words and its page, with an `.ics` (`METHOD:REQUEST`, the person as attendee, the same UID the feeds use so the two do not double) they accept into their own calendar; `CalendarMail`, from `CALENDAR_MAIL_FROM` (`Helios Calendar <when@heliosian.com>`), sends it, the dev server writes it to the mail directory. The invite's organizer and the message's reply address are `CALENDAR_REPLY_TO` (`Helios Calendar <rsvp@reply.heliosian.com>`), a receiving domain on Resend (`docs/deploy.md`), so the Accept or Decline a person taps in their own calendar app comes back: Resend takes the reply in and calls `POST /api/calendar/replies` (public, signed the Svix way with `RESEND_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, `internal/mail/receive.go`), the calendar fetches the raw message back by its id, finds the iCalendar `REPLY` in it - the `text/calendar` part or the `.ics` attachment - and records the attendee's `PARTSTAT` as their answer (`internal/calendar/replies.go`): accepted a yes, declined a no, tentative nothing; without sending another invite. A reply counts only for someone the directory knows, sent from their own address, for an event on the calendar; anything else received there is logged and dropped. Without the secret the route answers 404 and replies go unread. The calendar's `POST /api/calendar/rsvp` and Heliosian's `POST /api/apps/rsvp` (`{id, answer}`, answer yes, no, hidden, or blank to clear) both go through one `Answerer` the calendar hands Heliosian at start.

## Sharing

A link to an event pasted into a chat app previews the way HCA-Team's and Celebrate's do (`internal/calendar/share.go`, on the shared drawer `internal/sharecard`): the sign-in page served at the event's address carries Open Graph tags - the title, the day and hours, the place, and the description cut short - and names a card at `/share/{id}.png`, drawn with the mark and wordmark, the first category as a kicker, the title, the lines, a button in the brand teal saying what the page asks - RSVP, Sign Up for an HCA event, Get Tickets for a party - and the picture the event's page wears (its own, its first category's, or the calendar's header), fetched from the shared blob store or the bundled files whichever app serves them. A link to any other page - the calendar, a saved calendar's `/c/` address, the feeds - previews Helios When itself: a line on what it is in words, and a card at `/share/upcoming.png` that says so in three lines (school days and dismissals, events and sign-ups, calendars to save and subscribe to) with an Open Helios When button, over the calendar's own header picture. The cards are public, as every `/share/` path is, and carry only what the school's own calendar says.

## Feeds

A feed is an address a calendar app subscribes to: `https://when.heliosian.com/feed/{token}.ics`, served without sign-in (`auth.Public`), since Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and Outlook fetch it themselves. The token is the whole secret - 24 characters from a 31-symbol alphabet, minted by the server - so the page says so: anyone holding the address reads the feed, and removing it here is how it stops.

The rail's nav lists the viewer's calendars under Calendar: **My Heliosian** - the calendar's own view (their classrooms and the default categories; a view saved earlier in their place still stands until forgotten), which everyone has and nobody removes or refilters - a lock beside its headline, and in place of the cross while editing, says so - though each person may rename it, mark it and drag it among their own (the `Settings` tab's Home Name, Home Emoji and Home Position; `user.home` in the view, `Model.MyHeliosian`) - first to start (`MyHeliosianToken` stands for it where a saved calendar's token would) - then their own saved feeds in the sheet's order. The first of them is their default calendar - My Heliosian to start; Make Default (`POST /api/calendar/default` with a token, My Heliosian's included) moves one to the top, and dragging one to the top is the same - with a star at its end, a Default Calendar badge at the headline's end (any other calendar's headline ends in a faint Make Default instead), and it is the view the page opens to, Reset filters returns to, and Heliosian's Upcoming Events are read under (`viewOf`) - Heliosian names it beside the Upcoming Events heading as a quiet dropdown of My Heliosian and every saved calendar (`upcomingCalendar` in its model): picking another re-reads the events under it (`GET /api/apps/upcoming?calendar=`, `Model.UpcomingUnder`), and a Make default beside a pick that is not the default makes it so (`POST /api/apps/calendar/default`, through the `MakeDefault` hook the calendar hands Heliosian at start beside `Answer`). A pencil at Calendar's end turns on, beside each, a grip to drag it into a new order (the drop saves it: `PUT /api/calendar/feeds/order` with the tokens, My Heliosian's among them setting its place, the saved ones moved among the places their rows already hold), a pencil (the name-and-emoji popup, with Make default and Delete calendar at its foot - no Delete for My Heliosian) and a cross (remove, after a confirm; a lock in its place for My Heliosian), until it is pressed again: each opens the calendar with the filters set to what that feed carries, at `/c/{token}` (an address that opens it the same way), and while the viewer works from it its name heads the page over a swoosh - the name itself dropping a menu of every calendar to switch by, which is how a phone, with no rail, switches - lit in the rail while the filters are its own, amber with a "Filters changed · not saved" word beside the headline once they have moved, since Save Calendar saves back onto it - behind its mark - which, clicked, opens a small popup to change the name and emoji - and Get Feed beside the month opens a popup with its address, Copy, Subscribe in my calendar app, Add to Google Calendar and a word on each app - in place of Save Calendar. A viewer makes one with Save Calendar beside the month, or with New feed at the Feeds page's head, which opens the fuller form there, naming it (it starts as "Sam's Heliosian Calendar", their own first name on it; a name one of their feeds already has gets the first free number after it, "… 2", on the page and again on the server) and picking its classrooms (their own to start) and tags (all to start); every classroom or every tag is stored as no filter at all, so a whole-school feed keeps up as classrooms come and go. It lands in the `Feeds` tab with the viewer's address and is theirs alone: the page lists only their own, each behind its mark - the emoji the owner picked in its form, from a row of the usual ones or typed, else the calendar icon, the same mark the rail and the headline show - with the day it was made and, under its address, small chips for what it shows - its classrooms in their colors, then its categories, or Every classroom / Every category where it carries no filter - and only they, or an admin, can change or remove it. The pencil beside a feed's name, or Edit at the end of the Shows row, opens its own form under the card - the name, classrooms and categories as they stand - and Save changes keeps them in place (`PUT /api/calendar/feeds` with the token) at the same address, so a calendar app subscribed to it carries the new choice from its next refresh. The address is copied to the clipboard the moment it is made. Subscribe in my calendar app is the same address as `webcal://`, which Apple's and most others open straight into a subscription; Add to Google Calendar opens Google's From URL page to paste it into.

The document (`internal/calendar/feed.go`) carries every visible event the feed's filter admits - less any the owner hid or said no to -: all-day events as dates with the exclusive end iCalendar wants, timed events in UTC with the school's zone named on the calendar, the tags as categories, the day type at the head of the description, and each event's page as its URL. It asks the client to refresh hourly; Google refreshes on its own schedule, every several hours. The day plan itself is not in the feed: the departures from a regular day are events already, and a regular day is not.

## What it does not do yet

Nothing is edited in the app: the `Events`, `Overrides`, and `Day Overrides` tabs are the input surface, and the `Admins` tab names who will get the editor when it comes. Notifications - a note the week before a short day, an event that moved - are not built; the Change Log the import keeps is shaped for one.

## Sign-in and install

Everything sits behind Google sign-in restricted to the school domain, sharing a session with the other apps on the same tier. It installs to a home screen with the calendar-with-sun-and-leaf mark under `web/public/calendar/brand/`, cut from the Helios When exports in `~/Dropbox/Kids/Heliosian/images/when/` (`Helios When Logo.psd` is the master) on the recipe the other apps use: `appicon.png` for the home-screen icons, the bare mark (`favicon.png` there) for the favicons, `logo-mark.png` (the drawer's head), the app switch row (`web/public/common/brand/apps/calendar.png`) and the maskables at 62% on the icon's own pale ground; the teal long horizontal lockup, name and tagline on two lines beside the mark, for the rail (`logo-lockup-wide.png`, from `- long horizontal.png`, 900px wide, up to 310px on screen) and the white vertical one for the login page (`logo-lockup-light.png`); the vertical teal, the two-line horizontals and the white long horizontal (`logo-lockup-light-wide.png`) unused so far. There is no splash battery yet. The brand teal is the art's `#0e4d54`.
