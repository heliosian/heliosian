# Helios Gather

Helios Gather draws an invitation as an envelope that opens to a card. A Gather invitation is a When event - a `GROUP` of kind `event` - whose `theme` names its design, so its guest list, its Going and Not Going groups, its sending and its outside guests are When's, as `docs/datamodel.md` (Guest lists) and `docs/when/calendar.md` (Invitations) describe them; Gather adds the design and the pages a guest opens. It lives at the first of its `Hosts` in `hosts.Apps` (`internal/hosts/hosts.go`). The signed-in pages are `web/gather/`: they read through `/api/q` queries (`web/gather/state.js`) and write through `/api/q` batches under the policies, so the server holds no Gather-only rules for them. What a page needs without signing in - the theme code, the envelope and card, and the question fields - is in `web/public/gather/` and `web/public/common/`, which the signed-in pages load too. Its host serves the page shell for `/` and `/g/{key}` (`gatherMux` in `internal/app/wiring.go`), and through `WhenPages.RegisterGather` the event share cards and link previews When draws, the page for a guest from outside, the envelope picture, the themes and their pictures, and the theme kit.

## Designs

A design is a `THEME` row: the site's own, or one a host uploaded for their own invitations. `docs/gather/themes.md` covers where themes live, how they're uploaded and checked, and the kit they're made from. `plain`, a blank white card, is what Create Your Own starts from.

An invitation's look is several columns of its `GROUP`, all set by its hosts (`runs_list` in the policies):

- `theme` - the `THEME` of the card, the page behind it and the sidebar.
- `envelope`, `seal`, `liner` - the themes whose envelope, seal sticker and liner wrap it; blank for the theme's envelope, and for the envelope's own seal and liner.
- `design` - the invitation's own changes laid over its theme, as JSON in the shape of a theme's design, limited to the card's background, writing and accent colors, the page's color, the title and text fonts (stacks some design already uses) and `card.words` false for a card picture that already carries its words. `designChecked` in `internal/db/envelopecard.go` refuses anything else as it is written. A page color of the invitation's own also colors the sidebar and top bar, a dark gradient mixed from it with white words (`sidebarIn` in `web/public/gather/themes.js`), in place of the theme's sidebar.
- `card_picture`, `backdrop_picture` - pictures that fill the card and the page behind it, uploaded through `POST /api/do/design-picture` into their `_original` columns and made into tag-free JPEGs as flyers are (`madeColumns` in `internal/db/pictures.go`). Signed in, pages load them from `/api/blob/{id}/{column}`; a guest from outside from `/open/ext/{token}/picture/{card|backdrop}`.
- `hosted_by` - what the card says after Hosted by; blank for the hosts' names.

`lookTheme`, `themed` and `wrapped` in `web/public/gather/themes.js` turn a look into the CSS properties the card and envelope are drawn from.

## Pages

- **Invitations** (`/`) - the events with a design the viewer's household is on the guest list of, under Invited to, and those the viewer hosts, under Hosting, each a small envelope in its design. **New invitation** opens the editor. The top bar's search box narrows both lists to the invitations whose title, subtitle, summary, place, day, hours, hosts and pages' titles and words hold every word typed; typed on any other page, it returns here first (`web/gather/pages/home.js`).
- **Invitation** (`/g/{key}`, the key its slug, ID or an old address's alias) - the sealed envelope, addressed to the viewer and their household on the guest list; it opens itself a moment after the page shows, or at once on the seal, the flap lifting and the card rising out, then the card whole. The page's backdrop and the app's sidebar and top bar take the design's colors while it is open, the top of the sidebar, in place of Gather's logo, shows the invitation's seal as a tile beside its title and Hosted by line, then its day, hours and place, the place linking to a map, and its foot says Made with Gather from Helios Community Apps, linking to Gather's invitations and to the Heliosian home site (`inviteHead` and `madeWith` in `web/gather/chrome.js`), and the sidebar lists only this invitation: the invitation, its pages, the guest list (or Who's coming, where the hosts show the list), and for the hosts Questions, Notes from guests and Add a page. Opening it stamps the viewer's `MEMBER.opened`, as When's page does. Beside the card, for whoever is on the list, a row for them and each of their household on it, each with Yes and No, written to the event's Going and Not Going groups as When's answers are, with the questions under them (Questions, below); for its hosts, a small **Edit** button at the page's top right. The guest list is its own page (`/g/{key}/guests`), drawn as When draws it - its counts, Send, See table and Add People, **Guest List Settings**, or, before anyone is on it, the way to start one - from the same shared modules (`web/common/invites.js`; `docs/when/calendar.md`, Invitations), Gather handing them its own pieces through `setGuestHost` in `web/gather/app.js`. Changing an invitation's title, time or place offers to send the updated invitation, as When does. An event without a design sends the page on to When.
- **Invitation pages** (`/g/{key}/p/{id}`) - the invitation's own pages - a schedule, what to bring - each an `INVITE_PAGE` row naming the event, in its `order`, with a title and a markdown body, drawn on the card's background unless the page has its own: a `background` color (a `#rrggbb` color, which `pageBackgroundChecked` in `internal/db/envelopecard.go` holds it to) and a picture over it, uploaded through `POST /api/do/page-picture` into `picture_original` and made into `picture` as the card picture is. The invitation and its pages are one deck in that order: the one open shows whole, those before it peek out above it and those after it below, each a strip of the design's paper with its title; clicking one turns to it (`web/gather/pages/invite.js`). The hosts add a page from the sidebar, change it - its words, background color and picture - or remove it from its Edit page (`web/gather/pageeditor.js`), and drag the pages in the sidebar to reorder them, which gives the moved page an `order` between its new neighbours. The policies let whoever sees the event read its pages and its hosts write them, and a page goes with its event.
- **For a guest from outside** (`/ext/{token}`) - the same envelope, card and answers for someone outside the directory, without signing in, the token in the address being their copy of the invitation mail (`web/public/gather/ext.html`, `ext.js`). It reads and answers through the same `/open/ext/{token}` endpoints as When's page for them (`internal/db/invitehooks.go`), which also carry the design, the questions and the answers already given, and lets them bring guests where the event allows it.

The editor (`web/gather/editor.js`) is one popup with its pages in a side panel. Details holds the title, a subtitle (`subtitle`, a line under the title on the card and beside it at the left of the top bar), the start and end, the place and the summary (`description`). Essentials holds the hosts, whose changes save as they are made, and what the card says after Hosted by, starting as their names. Theme first offers Create Your Own, Upload a theme, and as small cards the site's themes and the host's own uploads; once one is chosen it shows that theme with Change theme and the card as it now looks, and its options appear as pages under Theme in the side panel, each beside the card as it now looks: Card (the card picture, with My picture already has the words on it), Background (the page background picture and color), Colors, Fonts, and Envelope (the sealed envelope as guests see it, with the envelopes, seals and liners to pick from). Pictures upload as they are picked, so they need the invitation to exist. The link the hosts share is set in Guest List Settings' Custom URL tab, the same tab When's event page has (`urlPanel` in `web/common/invites.js`): the word after `/g/`, the event's `slug`, as When's web address is (`docs/when/calendar.md`, Sharing an event); a word another group already has is refused, and a changed or cleared word keeps working as a hidden `ALIAS` of the event, so links already sent still open it. A new invitation is an invite-only event under the Misc category with its guest list shown to everyone who can open it (Show the guest list to everyone, `members_visible_to` the Everyone group), hosted by a managers group holding the person who made it, who is also on its guest list with no answer yet, so they RSVP as any guest does; a co-host added from Gather joins the guest list too (`hostsAreGuests` on Gather's events, `addHost` in `web/common/guestlist.js`), and either can be taken off it like any guest, as When's Add Event makes one (`docs/when/calendar.md`, Sharing an event).

## Questions

An invitation's hosts ask its guests things as they answer: each is a `QUESTION` row naming the event, in the hosts' order, its words, how it is answered (words, one choice, any of several choices, a number, yes or no), and whom it is asked of - every guest, adults, kids, or once per family - required or not, and asked of everyone or only of those who say yes. A kid is a student, or a guest from outside marked `PERSON.child`, which the Child box beside a family member in the shared adder for people from outside (`outsideAdder` in `web/common/members.js`) sets through `add-guests`. Each answer is an `ANSWER` row: for a question asked of each guest it names the guest, for one asked once per family the family, never both. The hosts add, change and remove questions from the Questions card beside the card (`web/gather/questions.js`), each in a popup, and read every answer in See answers.

Any event can have questions, and they are asked wherever its guests answer: on Gather's invitation page, in the family RSVP band on When's event page (`familyBand` in `web/common/invites.js`), and on the page for a guest from outside. Each shows, under each person's Yes and No, the questions asked of that person, then those asked of their family, each saved as it is changed. A required question not yet answered is marked, and a Still to answer note lists them; it does not stop anyone answering Yes, and When keeps the RSVP band open while one is left. The fields are `web/public/common/questionfields.js`, which each page hands a way to save; signed in, `web/common/rsvpquestions.js` loads them and saves through `/api/q`, and from outside they save through `POST /open/ext/{token}/reply` (`extReply` in `internal/db/extquestions.go`), which takes answers only for the guest, their household and guests they brought, and a family's only for their own family.

The policies keep a question the hosts' (`runs_list` on its event) and readable by whoever sees the event; an answer is given and changed by the event's hosts, by anyone who sees the event for a guest in their household, or by a member of a family for that family (`answers_question`), and read by the hosts and by the household it is for. A question goes when its event does, and an answer when its question or its family does (`deleteTriggers` in `internal/db/trigger.go`).

## Notes to the hosts

A guest writes the hosts of an event a note - a question, a word of thanks - from the invitation's page on Gather, from When's event page, or from the page for a guest from outside (`POST /open/ext/{token}/comment`, `extComment` in `internal/db/extcomments.go`). Each is a `COMMENT` row naming the event and its writer; a host's reply is one naming the note it answers in `reply_to`. They are the hosts' alone: the policies let the hosts read every note on their event, and anyone else only their own household's notes and the replies to them, so guests never see each other's. A writer takes their note back and the hosts take away any; a note goes with its event, and its replies with it (`deleteTriggers`). Each new note is emailed to every host but its writer, and each reply to the guest whose note it answers, with the link to their own page (`passNote` in `internal/db/invitemail.go`). The pages draw them from `web/common/hostnotes.js`: for a guest, their household's notes with the hosts' replies and a box to write; for a host, every note with a box to reply under it.

## Mail

Every guest-list mail of an event with a design - invitations, reminders, notes, updates, cancellations and the notices to hosts - goes out from Gather's own address, `gatherMailFrom` in `internal/app/environment.go` (`senderFor` in `internal/db/invitemail.go`), under the hosts' names followed by "via Gather" (`sendCopy`), so the name isn't taken for someone impersonating the hosts on an address not theirs; replies and calendar answers still go to When's reply address with the person's token, which the replies hook reads. A cancellation's subject and words are the hosts' own when they write them, in place of the standard ones. The invitation mail of an event with a design links to Gather rather than When (`linkFor` in `internal/db/invitemail.go`): a guest from the directory to its page, a guest from outside to their `/ext/{token}` page there. It is drawn in the design (`gatherLetter`): on the page's color, the hosts' message, an Open your invitation button in the accent color, then the card picture when the invitation has one (through the guest's own `/open/ext/{token}/picture/card`) or else the envelope picture, then a panel in the card's colors and fonts with the design's kicker, the title, Hosted by, the summary, a row for when with Add to Google Calendar and one for where with View location, and the button again, signed Sent with Helios Gather; the fonts load from Google Fonts where the mail reader allows it and fall back to the stack's last face where it doesn't. Its picture is the envelope in the event's design, opened with the card showing the title, drawn by the server at `/open/envelope/{id}.png` (`envelopePicture` in `internal/db/envelopecard.go`, `DrawEnvelope` in `internal/sharecard/envelope.go`) from the colors and kicker of its themes - the envelope, seal and liner of their own designs, the card and page of `theme` with the colors in `design` over it - in the app's own typeface; pictures are left out of it. The seal carries the title's first letter, or the seal design's own mark when that is a letter or digit.
