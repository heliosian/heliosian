# The shared tools

`internal/tools` holds every read-only tool Claude is given over the data model (`docs/datamodel.md`), written once: Helios MCP serves them to any client that connects (`docs/mcp/mcp.md`), and Helios Ask hands the same ones to the model in its chat (`docs/ask/data.md`). Whatever one of them answers, the other answers the same way.

Each is a `Tool`: a name starting `helios_`, a description that says it is Helios School's, the words Ask's page shows while it runs (`Words`), and an input struct whose JSON Schema (`jsonschema.For`, field descriptions from its `jsonschema` tags) both servers offer as its input. `Set.Run` runs one as a viewer, a person ID the server resolved from the session or the token (`Model.SignedIn`), never from the model's arguments, and every read goes through `Model.Run`, so the read policies and the consent step decide what it answers, exactly as for `/api/q`. A tool answers text: JSON written without HTML escaping, so an address in it reads whole, or for a few of them their own text. Alongside it, `Answer.Links` maps every `href` the tool wrote for a person or group to that row's ID, which is how Ask draws a link as a chip. Each server holds tools' answers to its own length (`maxOutput` in `internal/mcp`, `maxToolOutput` in `internal/ask`) and refuses a longer one with a word on narrowing it. Each run logs a `tools: run` line with the tool, the viewer, how long it took and any error.

A person in any answer is marked by their roles - `student`, `parent` or `staff`, from the role groups they are effective members of - and listed staff first, then parents, then students, by sorting name. Every record with a page on the Helios apps carries `href` (`Model.Link`).

| Tool | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| `helios_whoami` | the viewer: their roles, grade, classroom, crew, department and job title, their addresses, each family with its members, the groups they are in by name with their kind and whether they lead it, the groups they manage directly (through `managed_by`, families and self-managing groups aside), and the apps they are an admin of |
| `helios_search` | `Searcher.Search` (`docs/datamodel.md`, the search): for each of GROUP, PERSON and DOCUMENT, the rows whose name holds every word and then those close in meaning |
| `helios_similar` | `Searcher.Similar`: every near-identical copy of an email's, part's or extract's text |
| `helios_events` | events, parties and HCA-Team activities (not their shifts) starting from a day for two weeks, or the whole calendar for words alone, each with its category, whether it is for the viewer's household (an effective member of it is the viewer or in their families) and the viewer's own answer (`rsvp_yes` or `rsvp_no`); a classroom narrows to what an effective member of the classroom's families is in |
| `helios_days` | the `day` groups on a date with their `day_part` hours, the classrooms each is for (its effective members' classrooms; none means every classroom), whether it is the viewer's household's, the calendar entries that set it (`GROUP_SOURCE.name`), and the school year's first and last days (`GROUP_SOURCE.marker`) |
| `helios_find_people` | people by search words (the PERSON list `Searcher.Search` answers), role, grade, classroom and department, with primary email and phone as shared |
| `helios_classroom` | a classroom by name: its band and color, the grades of its students, its teachers (staff whose classroom it is), its crews with their teachers and students, its students, and the plain groups under its band with their members - the band's room parents; without a name, every classroom with its band and teachers |
| `helios_group` | a group by ID: its managers (the effective members of each `managed_by` up its parents), its effective members with their reasons and roles, leads first, its rules and the groups under it |
| `helios_nearby_families` | families nearest a family (the viewer's own first family placed on the map, or one by ID), by straight-line miles between their addresses' `GEOCODE` coordinates, street addresses only (`db.Placeable`), narrowed by a classroom, grade or distance, each with its members |
| `helios_activities` | a school year's HCA-Team activities (the root activity whose name starts with the year; the current school year by default), each with its heading, the activity it sits under, timing, place, spots, whether it is full or wants a co-chair, its co-chairs and volunteers as the viewer may see them, and the viewer's own place; past ones left out unless asked; Team's `Intro` and `Expense Form URL` settings |
| `helios_parties` | Celebrate's parties still to come (all, when asked), each with its celebration, date, place, price, capacity, whether it is full or takes a waitlist, who it is for (`eligible`), its hosts, the tickets the viewer may see and their household's own; Celebrate's `Parties Intro`, `Ticket Note` and `Current` celebration |
| `helios_lists` | the groups with an address (a `slug`) the viewer may see, each with its address, managers, member count, members as the viewer may see them, and whether the viewer is on it |
| `helios_links` | the groups with a `url` under each `links` widget's group, each with its widget's name as its section |
| `helios_read_document` | a document's tree of parts, links, images and extracts, and the Markdown of each extract read from the bucket through `Model.BlobCell`, at most `documentText` bytes of it, naming the extracts left out |
| `helios_get` | one row with the names of what it references and the first few rows of each table and column that point at it; a person also with each family and its members |
| `helios_query` | a query in the language, run as `/api/q` runs it: the canonical form, the count, the rows with their filled columns and the rows the includes brought |
| `helios_describe_schema` | every table with its description and column names, from `db.Tables` |
| `helios_describe_table` | one table's columns in full, from `db.DescribeTable`, and the columns elsewhere that point at it |
| `helios_policies` | `db.PolicySource` |

`internal/tools/tools_test.go` runs every tool as the sample parent over the sample data (`sampledata/data*/`, with a school day added in the test from `testkit.SchoolDay`), and pins that each `href` a tool writes is noted with its row and that every tool has words and an object schema.
