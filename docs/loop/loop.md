# Helios Loop

Helios Loop is where the community's email lists are made: each is an address at `loop.heliosian.com` whose members follow from rules over the data model, and every message sent to it goes on to those members as they stand when it arrives. It serves at loop.heliosian.com. Every group in the data model is an email list (`docs/datamodel.md`); one made in Loop is a `GROUP` of kind `group`, under nothing and linking nowhere. The pages are `web/loop/`, reading and writing through the query API (`docs/api.md`); the mail path is `internal/db/listmail.go` (`data.md`, Mail).

## Entities

- **Email list** — a `GROUP`: its `slug` is the local part of its address and the page's address (`/groups/{slug}`), fixed once made and unique across every group; its other addresses are `ALIAS` rows naming it (`hummingbirds-families` beside `hummingbird-families`), added and removed by a manager; `name` is its title and `description` a line or two about it; `visible_to` says who sees it beyond its managers (Visible, below), `posting` and `replying` who may post and reply (below); `managed_by` names its managers group; and its `RULE` rows choose its members. Addresses are lowercase letters, digits, dots and hyphens, starting and ending with a letter or digit, and a handful mail systems reserve (postmaster, abuse, and the like) are refused, as is `unsubscribe`, the address the mail form of unsubscribing goes to. Mail to an alias is the email list's mail in every way. Every message goes out with the email list's name in brackets before its subject.
- **Rule** — a `RULE` row: one group (`target`) whose effective members it takes in, or in place of them their parents, children or households (`replace_with`), kept, if it says so, to those also in a role group (`within`: students, parents, staff, adults). A rule that leaves out (`exclude`) keeps whoever it picks off the list. A rule may name only a group whose members its author may see (`sees_members`), so a rule never reaches people its author could not have listed. Members are the effective members the data model works out (`EFFECTIVE_MEMBER`), and why each is on it is the rule or hand addition that put them there.
- **Hand addition** — someone on the email list by hand, a `MEMBER` row with `member` yes: someone from the directory a manager picked, or someone outside it (a coach, a league office, a family friend), who is a guest `PERSON` with the address and the name the manager typed (`/api/do/guest`, which finds the person already holding an address before making a guest, since an address belongs to one guest at most).
- **Kept off** — someone a manager keeps off the email list whatever the rules say, a `MEMBER` row with `member` excluded. A manager puts them back in the editor.
- **Unsubscribed** — someone in the email list's unsubscribed group (`GROUP.unsubscribed`): they stay a member and get none of its mail. The first unsubscribe makes the group, hidden and managed by the list's managers, named `<title> Unsubscribed`. Someone unsubscribes by the link or address in their copy or with Unsubscribe on the email list's page, and puts themselves back with Resubscribe.
- **Manager** — an effective member of the email list's managers group, a plain group named `<title> Managers` that runs itself: they change the email list's words, rules, hand additions, kept-off people, aliases and managers, and close it. Whoever makes an email list is its first manager. Super admins see and change every email list as a manager would, but for a family's members, which only the import changes. A manager gets the email list's mail only when they are among its members.
- **Visible** — who sees an email list beyond its managers and the super admins: nobody (hidden, the default), its own members, or everyone. Whoever sees it sees its members and its description; the rules and why each member is on go to its managers and the super admins alone.
- **Posting** and **Replying** — whose new messages, and whose replies to the email list's own mail, go out: everyone, members (the email list's members and managers, the default), managers alone, or nobody. A reply is a message whose `In-Reply-To` or `References` names the `Message-ID` of a post the email list sent on. The sender is the person holding the address in the message's `From`, once the mail has proved it (Mail, below).
- **Closed** — an email list a manager closed (`status` closed): gone from every page and taking no mail, kept for the posts that name it.

## Pages

- **My Email Lists** (`/`) — the groups the viewer runs or is in that the rail lists (`sidebar_group`, `docs/datamodel.md`), each a card with its title, its address to copy when it has one, its description, its member count, Subscribed when the viewer is on it, a star when they run it and its visibility when they run it and it is not hidden. The rail is the one Who? draws (`web/common/rail.js`), without My Family; its group rows open each group's page here. The toolbar's search narrows the cards.
- **An email list** (`/groups/{slug}`) — the address to mail or copy, with a count of its aliases that shows them on hover, leaving out any alias with an uppercase letter (neither shows on a group without a slug), lines saying how subjects go out, who sees it, who posts and who replies when those are narrowed, a line when the viewer unsubscribed, and the description. Then tabs, the chosen one kept in the address's `tab` parameter. Members: a tile for each member, opening the person's window, its tooltip for a manager saying why they are on (the rule's words, or Added by hand), with a search box; under it, for a manager, the rules read out with a sign each and a count of hand additions and kept-off people, and a pencil to the editor. Managers: a card each with Subscribed or Not subscribed, and for a manager a pencil that opens them for changes, each saved at once. History, for a manager: every post the email list took in, newest first, with its sender, subject and when it came; a post sent on carries its copies' count of delivered, failed and pending, opening to a row per copy; a post dropped or failed says why. Edit opens the editor over the page. Unsubscribe, for someone on the email list, or Resubscribe once they have, does the one thing and reloads the page.
- **The editor** (an email list's Edit, `?edit=1`, or `/new`, New Email List) — Members first, then Details. Members: the rules, a line each - Include or Leave out, the group (typed and picked from every group whose members the viewer may see), who (the people, their parents, their children, their households), and anyone or only one role - with how many people each picks out once the preview answers; the members the draft would hold, worked out by the server without saving (`/api/do/draft-members`) as any of it changes, with Add Helios and Add Non-Helios at the card's head (`web/common/members.js`) and a red cross on each row that keeps a directory member off or takes a hand addition away; on an existing email list, who joins and who leaves; and Kept off, with Put back on each. Details: the title, the address (fixed once made) with Add alias, the description with Generate with AI (`/api/do/describe-group`, Claude writing a sentence or two from the title, the rules in words and a tally of who they pick out by role, grade and classroom, never their names), who sees it, who posts and who replies, and for a new email list its managers. Save writes everything that changed in one batch; Make the email list writes the managers group, the email list, its rules, hand additions and aliases in one batch, then adds anyone from outside the directory. Close email list, after a word of confirmation, closes it.

## In Helios Who?

Every email list a person runs or is on is one of their lists in Who? (`docs/who/directory.md`, Lists), its page there linking back to the email list here.

## Mail

Mail to an email list's address is received by Mailgun and sent on by the server to every member, one copy each, with nobody's address on it but the email list's and the sender's: a member never learns who else is on the email list from the mail. Who may post is the email list's Posting, and who may reply its Replying (Entities); there is no moderation. What arrives is kept whole (`data.md`, Mail) before anything goes out.

**The copy each member gets** is the message as sent, its body and attachments untouched, its `Message-ID`, `In-Reply-To` and `References` kept so every member's copy is the same message and replies thread, its `To` and `Cc` as the sender wrote them, and a few headers rewritten. `From` becomes the sender's name "via" the email list's title at the email list's address - `Alice Smith via Soccer Team Families <soccer-team@loop.heliosian.com>` - since the sender's own domain will not vouch for a message it did not send (DMARC), and Mailgun signs only for `loop.heliosian.com`; `Reply-To` is the sender's own address (or the Reply-To they set), so a plain reply goes back to them and reply-all reaches them and the email list, and `X-Original-From` carries the From as it was. The subject gets `[Title] ` in front, once, kept behind a `Re:`, so a reply to a prefixed subject is `Re: [Title] …` and never `[Title] Re: [Title] …`. Every copy carries `List-Id`, `List-Post`, `Precedence: list`, a Loop marker, and its recipient's own `List-Unsubscribe` in both forms, a mail address and a one-click link, with `List-Unsubscribe-Post` beside it.

**Unsubscribe** is that header: a mail app that offers an Unsubscribe button posts to the link or writes to the address, and a person who follows the link by hand lands on a small page naming the email list and their address with one button. Whichever way, they are kept off the email list (Entities) with a note saying which way, and get nothing more from it; the page says so, and says a manager is the way back. The link and the address's subject both carry a token signed with the email list's address and the person's, so neither can be made up or bent to another person. Whether a mail app shows the button is the app's own call: Gmail and Fastmail both withhold it from senders they have no history with, and Loop adds no footer to make up for that.

**Authentication**: every post has to prove its `From`. Mailgun puts an `Authentication-Results` header on top of what it receives, with its DKIM, SPF and DMARC verdicts, and a post goes on only when DMARC passed for the `From`'s domain, or DKIM or SPF passed for a domain aligned with it - the same domain, or one inside the other. Only the topmost such header counts, and only when Mailgun wrote it (`mxa.mailgun.org` and its kin); one further down is the sender's own and is ignored. A post relayed through a Google Group arrives re-signed as the Google Group's own domain, which no longer proves its `From`, so when Mailgun's header also says `arc=pass` - the ARC chain of forwarders' seals is intact - the newest ARC set is read instead: if `google.com` sealed it, its `ARC-Authentication-Results`, Google's verdicts on the post as it first arrived, are held to the same test. No other forwarder's seal is trusted.

**What is not sent on**: a message already sent through Loop (the marker, so two email lists that hold each other's address cannot ring), auto-submitted mail such as vacation replies, a mail system's own notices, mail that fails authentication, and mail from a sender the email list's Posting leaves out, or for a reply its Replying. Each is recorded as dropped, with why, and none is filed in Helios Ask. The last gets a notice back at its `From`, from `HCA-Team <team@loop.heliosian.com>`, threaded under the post, saying it was not sent and who can post, or reply; it carries `Auto-Submitted: auto-replied` and the Loop marker so nothing answers it in turn. The rest get nothing: a post that fails authentication has no sender to trust with an answer.

**Delivery**: each copy is recorded as it goes out, and Mailgun tells the server when a copy is delivered or bounces for good, which the copy's row records (`data.md`); History shows them under the post, so a manager sees who has the message, who is still waiting on it and whose address has gone dead.

## Sample data

`sampledata/datagroups/` holds one email list, `hummingbirds-parents`, visible to everyone, open to its members' posts, its one rule the Hummingbirds classroom's parents, with an alias. In sample mode nothing receives mail: the routes say they are not set up, and a copy, were one sent, would land as `.eml` files beside the other sample mail.
