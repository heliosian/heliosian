# Data model

The email lists are mail lists in the data model (`loop.md`, Entities; `docs/datamodel.md`), and Loop's pages and mail path read and write them there: a list is a `GROUP`, its address its `slug` and its other addresses `ALIAS` rows, its managers the group its `managed_by` names, its rules `RULE` rows, the people added by hand or kept off `MEMBER` rows, its posts `MESSAGE` rows and each copy sent a `RECIPIENT` row.

**Saving writes what changed**, in the data model, in one `POST /api/q` batch: the cells of the email list's `GROUP` that changed; its `RULE` rows, all replaced in the editor's order when any rule changed, since a rule's only settable cell is its `order`; its hand additions and kept-off people as `MEMBER` rows inserted, set or deleted by person; and its `ALIAS` rows by alias. Making one writes its managers group, the email list naming it in `managed_by`, its rules, hand additions and aliases in one batch. Anyone from outside the directory goes on it in the same batch: `/api/do/person-at` finds whoever already holds their address, and a new guest `PERSON` and `PERSON_EMAIL` are inserted for an address nobody holds. Closing one sets its `status` to closed: it takes no mail and leaves every page, and its posts and their copies stay.

## What stays keyed by the name

The name is the email list's address, and what leaves the server in mail carries the name rather than the ID. A manager may change the name (`GROUP.slug`), and what carries the old one then reaches the list only while an `ALIAS` row keeps it:

- the mail's own headers: `List-Id`, `List-Post`, `X-Helios-Loop`, and the email list's address in `From` (`internal/db/listrewrite.go`).

The email list's page, `/g/<name>`, is found by its name, any alias or its ID (`group` in `web/loop/state.js`), so a link still opens it.

## The membership

An email list's members are its effective members (`docs/datamodel.md`, Effective members): its `MEMBER` rows with `member=yes`, everyone its rules select, less its exclude rules, everyone with `member=excluded` and everyone deactivated. Someone in its unsubscribed group stays a member and gets no copies (Unsubscribe, below).

## Mail

**Why Mailgun.** Loop needs a sender that takes a message as bytes and passes it on as they are: `To` and `Cc` as the sender wrote them, so reply-all reaches the email list, and the sender's `Message-ID`, so every member's copy is one message and replies thread. Resend rebuilds every message on both its API and its SMTP relay - `To` becomes the envelope recipient, `Cc` is dropped, `Message-ID` and `Date` are replaced, and a `List-Unsubscribe` with two forms is corrupted - and has no setting for it. Mailgun's `messages.mime` endpoint delivered a probe byte for byte, adding only its own `Return-Path`, a `Sender`, two `X-Mailgun` headers and a DKIM signature that covers the list headers. Amazon SES, which Resend runs on, keeps `To` and `Cc` on a raw send but documents replacing `Message-ID`.

**Receiving.** `loop.heliosian.com` has to be a domain in Mailgun, verified for sending, with its MX records pointing at Mailgun, so that every message to any address under it lands there, and needs one route matching every recipient under the domain that forwards the message whole, `forward("https://loop.heliosian.com/hooks/mail/mime")` - the address ends in `mime`, which is what makes Mailgun post the raw message rather than its parsed parts; `docs/deploy.md` says how to read what the account holds. On a message, Mailgun calls the route with the recipient it matched, the sender, the From, the subject, and the message itself in `body-mime`, signed with the HTTP webhook signing key (`MAILGUN_WEBHOOK_KEY`; `docs/deploy.md`) - the HMAC-SHA256 of timestamp and token, which `mail.VerifyMailgun` checks, allowing a quarter of an hour of clock drift. The call comes as JSON or as a form and the handler reads either, up to forty megabytes (`mail.MaxNotification`), which holds the largest message Mailgun accepts, twenty-five megabytes, with room for the form's encoding of it. The mail path runs on the data model (`internal/db/listmail.go`), writing as the system `mailer` (`docs/datamodel.md`). Every open `GROUP` is an email list there, its address its `slug` and its other addresses `ALIAS` rows naming it; `posting` and `replying` say who may post and reply (`everyone`, `members`, `managers` or `none`; every group carries both, `members` when made without them), and a post refused is answered with a note saying who may. A recipient matches a slug or alias whatever its case, but an alias with an uppercase letter is a case-sensitive old ID and is never an address, and an alias of a group with no slug is never one either, since its copies would have no address to come from. A recipient that is an email list's address or alias makes the message a post to that list, once however many of its addresses the message named; `unsubscribe@loop.heliosian.com` is the mail form of unsubscribing (below), a name no email list may take or alias, so the token in its subject reaches no email list; anything else under the domain is logged and ignored. The handler answers 200 for what it took and 406 for a signature that does not check, which Mailgun does not retry; any other answer it retries for eight hours.

**The pipeline.** The handler keeps the message whole as `CONTENT` (`message/rfc822`, in the media bucket under its hash) and records, for each email list it was sent to, an inbound `MESSAGE` of kind `post` in state `received` - its subject, its `From` address and the person who has it, its `Message-ID` as `header_id` - all in one write before it answers, so that once Mailgun has its 200 the post is stored whatever happens next; a failed write is answered 500 and Mailgun brings the message again. A route fires once per matched recipient and a retried call carries the same message again; an email list that already holds a post of the same `CONTENT` takes nothing more. One worker then takes the posts in turn: drops a post (state `dropped`, the reason in `detail`) if it is one Loop already sent, auto-submitted, a mail system's notice, fails authentication (`mail.Authenticated`, over Mailgun's topmost `Authentication-Results`; `loop.md`, Mail) or is from a sender the list's `posting` leaves out, or its `replying` for a reply - one whose `In-Reply-To` or `References` names the `header_id` of a post of the list's that was sent - the last answered with a notice to its `From` through `messages.mime`; rewrites the headers (`loop.md`, Mail; `internal/db/listrewrite.go`), always putting the list's name in brackets before the subject; records an outbound `MESSAGE` under the post; and posts Mailgun one copy per effective member outside the list's unsubscribed group to `messages.mime`, the message as bytes with that member's primary address as the envelope recipient, each copy a `RECIPIENT` with when it was `sent`. The post ends `sent`, any copies that failed named in `detail`, or `failed` when none went or an earlier step failed, with the step and the error. A post that becomes `sent` is filed into Helios Ask's documents by the trigger that files every sent post (`docs/datamodel.md`), sent to its list. A restart, once the first refresh is in, takes up every post still `received`; a `failed` post is a person's to look at, and a message can be resent by posting again.

**Delivery.** Each copy is its own send, its envelope the one member's address and the message its headers as written, carrying `v:recipient` with the copy's `RECIPIENT` ID; every app's mail with a `RECIPIENT` row is sent the same way (`docs/datamodel.md`, Mail). The domain's Mailgun webhooks for delivery, permanent failure, temporary failure and complaint point at `POST /hooks/events` (public, the same signature, the signature object inside the JSON; `docs/deploy.md`, again, for reading where they point), where `Deliveries` (`internal/db/delivery.go`) reads the copy's ID back from the event's `user-variables`. A delivery sets the copy's `delivered`, a permanent failure its `failed` and `detail`; a temporary failure, which Mailgun retries for up to eight hours, and a complaint are logged and leave the copy as it stands. An event naming no copy - mail no `RECIPIENT` row records, such as a refused post's bounce - is ignored. Reports gather while a write is in flight and go in the next one, so a burst from a large email list spends a few writes rather than one each.

**Unsubscribe.** Every copy's `List-Unsubscribe` names both forms: `mailto:unsubscribe@loop.heliosian.com?subject=<token>` and `https://loop.heliosian.com/open/unsubscribe/<token>`, the token the email list's ID and the recipient's address signed with the session key, the email list found again by its ID, so a token outlives a change of address, with `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` beside them. `GET` on the link is the page with the button; `POST`, from the button or from a mail app's one-click, puts the person in the list's unsubscribed group (`GROUP.unsubscribed`, made by the first unsubscribe, hidden, managed by the list's managers), its note saying which way, and answers an empty 200 to a one-click and the page to the button. Mail to the address comes through the same route as a post, and its subject, less any `Re:`, is the token; the sender is recorded but not checked, since the token reaches only the one person's copy. All of it is public (`internal/auth`); the signature is the whole of the check. Someone unsubscribed stays a member - they still see a members-only list and may post to it - and gets no copies. Loop's Unsubscribe and Resubscribe buttons call `POST /api/do/subscription` with `{"group", "subscribed"}`, which puts the signed-in person in the unsubscribed group or takes them out, for someone in the group or already unsubscribed from it, writing as the mailer.

**Every other app** sends through Mailgun too, by its JSON endpoint (`mail.Mailgun.Send`), and the calendar's replies come in through a Mailgun route at `reply.heliosian.com` the same way Loop's posts do (`docs/deploy.md`).
