# Data model

The tabs, columns, and validation rules are in `internal/model`'s `email_lists*.go`; this file carries only what reading that code cannot tell you.

The email lists live in one Google Sheet, `Groups`, in the community shared drive, reached through drive membership like the other sheets. `GROUPS_SHEET` names it, and `tools/createtabs` reads that variable to lay it out from an empty spreadsheet titled `Groups`, or to add the tabs and columns an older one lacks. The sheet's tabs and columns call an email list a group.

## Tabs

- `Groups` — Group ID, Name, Title, Description, Created By, Created, Subject Prefix, Visible, Posting, Replying. One row per email list, keyed by Group ID: the email list's ID, minted when the email list is made (`id.New` in `internal/id`), which every other tab's Group column holds and every request to the API names the email list by. Name is the address's local part, fixed once made (What stays keyed by the name, below). Subject Prefix is `off` for an email list whose subjects go out as written; blank, or anything else, means the title goes in front. Visible is `everyone` for an email list everyone in Loop sees, `members` for one the people on it see, and `hidden` or blank for one its managers and the admins alone see (`loop.md`, Visible); any other value refuses the load. Posting is `members` for an email list whose managers and members alone may post, `managers` for one whose managers alone may, and `everyone` or blank for one anyone may post to (`loop.md`, Posting); any other value refuses the load. Replying takes the same values for who may reply to the email list's own mail (`loop.md`, Replying).
- `Managers` — Group, Email. One row per manager per email list; every email list needs at least one.
- `Rules` — Group, Kind, Roles, Search, Classrooms, Grades, Tags, Family. One row per rule, in the order the editor shows them. Kind is `include` or `exclude`. Roles, Classrooms, Grades, Tags and Family are comma-separated lists; Tags holds tags by their ID in Who?'s `Tag List` (`tag:<id>`, whoever wrote the rule; `docs/who/data.md`, Tags are kept by ID) and Magic Tag keys (`party:<id>`, `activity:<id>`, `room:<band>`), each read for the email list's managers (`loop.md`, A rule discloses the tags it names); a tag named any other way refuses the load. A tag with a comma in its name cannot be used in a rule. Every email list needs at least one include rule, and every rule at least one of the facets.
- `Additions` — Group, Email, Name. One row per person added by hand, from the directory or outside it (`loop.md`, Addition). Email is lowercased and must be an address; Name may be blank, the address standing in for it, and for someone the directory holds the directory's name is shown whatever it says. An email list holds at most two hundred, once each by address.
- `Excluded` — Group, Email, Note, Timestamp. One row per address kept off an email list (`loop.md`, Excluded): the address, lowercased; a note saying why, which an unsubscribe fills in itself (`Unsubscribed by one-click`, `by the page`, `by mail from <address>`, `by the email list's page in Loop`) and a manager types by hand, up to a hundred and twenty characters; and when it went on. An email list holds at most two hundred, once each by address, listed by when they went on, a blank Timestamp last. The evaluator leaves them off whatever the rules and additions say; a manager's Remove in the editor deletes the row, as does the person's own Resubscribe on the email list's page (the `unsubscribe` and `resubscribe` actions on `email-lists`, `docs/api.md`, which add and delete the row for someone the rules or additions place on an email list they can see).
- `Aliases` — Alias, ID. One row per other local part an email list answers as (`loop.md`, Email list), ID the email list's Group ID; the shape every sheet's Aliases tab has (`internal/id`), read by `id.ParseAliases`, which lowercases the alias and refuses a blank one, a repeated one or an ID that is not one. Each is held to the same form as a name and unique across every email list's name and aliases: a load with an alias that is another email list's name or alias, or an email list named for another's alias, refuses. At most ten per email list.
- `Messages` — ID, Group, Received, From, Subject, State, Recipients, Object, Detail, Message ID. The posts the old mailer took in, one row per message per email list, which the email list's History (`loop.md`, Pages) shows for the rows `sent`, newest Received first. Nothing writes it any more: posts are `MESSAGE` rows in the data model (Mail, below), and the sync carries these rows there.
- `Deliveries` — Timestamp, Group, Email, Event, Message, Detail. The old mailer's record of each copy's sending and Mailgun's reports on it, which History shows under the `Messages` row whose Message ID is the same, angle brackets aside. Nothing writes it any more: each copy is a `RECIPIENT` row (Mail, below).
- `Admins` — Email. Who sees and may change every email list, beyond the platform super admins (`docs/config.md`).
- `Archived` — Group, Email. One row per person per email list they have archived for themselves (`loop.md`, Archived): the address, lowercased. Archive on the email list's page adds the row once and Unarchive deletes it (the `archive` and `unarchive` actions on `email-lists`); a row naming an email list the sheet does not have refuses the load, like every other tab's.
- `Change Log` — the store's, one row per changed cell holding what it held before and one per inserted row (`docs/storage.md`). Never read back. An unsubscribe or resubscribe on the email list's page is made as the person themselves.

**A load either succeeds whole or refuses**, the stance every app takes: an email list with a Group ID missing, malformed or another email list's, an email list with no manager or no include rule, a rule, addition, exclusion or alias naming an email list the sheet does not have, an unknown kind, role, relation, visibility, posting or replying, a name or alias that cannot be an address, or an alias two email lists would share refuses the load, so a hand edit that breaks a rule surfaces as a refused start or a failed refresh rather than an email list quietly matching nobody. The app checks the same rules before it writes, so nothing it saves can refuse the next load. `Messages` and `Deliveries` are records, not rules: a row of either naming an email list the sheet does not have is kept and ignored.

**Saving writes what changed.** A save sets the email list's `Groups` row, adds and deletes `Managers` and `Aliases` rows by address and alias, sets or deletes `Additions` and `Excluded` rows by address, and, when any rule changed, replaces the email list's `Rules` rows in the editor's order, so the sheet's rows for an email list are always what the editor last held and the Change Log holds only what the save changed (`groupOps` in `internal/model/email_lists_writes.go`). A save names only the fields it changes and the server lays them over the email list as it stands (`edit` on `email-lists`, `docs/api.md`). Deleting the `Groups` row takes the email list's `Managers`, `Rules`, `Additions`, `Excluded`, `Aliases`, `Archived`, `Messages` and `Deliveries` rows with it in the same commit (`carryGroup` in `internal/model/email_lists_cache.go`), and its posts among Helios Ask's documents (`docs/ask/artifacts.md`, What is in it and what is not) are removed on the write queue after; the buckets are written, never emptied, so the archived messages and the documents' objects stay, named by no row and read by nothing. The name goes back into the pool with the email list, and an email list made later under it gets an ID of its own, so none of these records reach it.

## What stays keyed by the name

The name is the email list's address and never changes, so what leaves the sheet - in mail, in links, in another store - carries the name rather than the ID, and finds the email list by it (`EmailLists.Named`, exact, never through an alias):

- the mail's own headers: `List-Id`, `List-Post`, `X-Helios-Loop`, and the email list's address in `From` (`internal/db/loopmessage.go`), which the data model's `GROUP.slug` holds;
- the unsubscribe token, the name and the address signed together (Unsubscribe, below), so every token already in someone's mailbox keeps working;
- the earlier mailer's archive object names, `loop/<name>/...`, and so the `Messages` rows' Object;
- Helios Ask's documents of an email list's posts, whose Channel is the name (`docs/ask/artifacts.md`); re-keying them would mean embedding every post again;
- the email list's page, `/groups/<name>`, and every link to it: Team's email list link (the activity's Magic Tag's `email-lists`, `docs/api.md`, Magic Tags) and the email list's Magic Tag in Who?, keyed `group:<Group ID>` and carrying the name as its `slug` for the link.

## The membership

An email list's members are never stored. They are computed from the directory model, the tags the rules name (`model.Directory.Tags`, which a tool reads off a model it loaded itself), each read while one of the email list's managers owns it or is among its managers (`model.Directory.SharedTags`), and the managers' Magic Tags (`model.Audience` in `internal/model/magic_tags.go`: the directory's room-parent tags, `RoomParentTags`, and `MagicTags` on `model.Parties` and `model.Activities`, with every activity's for an HCA-Team admin; and the email lists they manage, whose Magic Tag is that list's members, worked out the same way and once per evaluation, a list already being worked out counting as nobody) whenever anything asks: the email list's page, the editor's preview (which also asks it how many people each rule touches, `RuleCounts`), every forward and `tools/loadcheck` all call the one evaluator, `model.Audience` in `internal/model/audience.go` - Heliosian's audiences read through it too - which `EmailList.Members` and `Reasons` in `internal/model/email_lists_audience.go` read an email list's rules through, reading a person's grade and classroom as every filter does - `model.Directory.Facets`, the server's counterpart of Who?'s client-side `personFacets`: a student's own, a parent's children's, none for staff, and the same order: the roles keep their kind after Add family has widened the matches. The rules' result is then unioned with the email list's additions, each on once by address whatever the exclude rules say, with `Added` as its reason, and the excluded addresses taken away last. The tests pin it to the sample community.

The email list's Magic Tag in Who? (`EmailLists.MagicTags` in `internal/model/email_lists_magic_tags.go`) sorts the same members by whether the directory holds them: the directory's people are the list's People, and the rest are its Guests under the name the manager typed, keyed `<Group ID>:<address>` - the one `model.Guest` shape a party's ticket holders outside the directory use, keyed by the ticket there.

## Mail

**Why Mailgun.** Loop needs a sender that takes a message as bytes and passes it on as they are: `To` and `Cc` as the sender wrote them, so reply-all reaches the email list, and the sender's `Message-ID`, so every member's copy is one message and replies thread. Resend, which every other app sends through, rebuilds every message on both its API and its SMTP relay - `To` becomes the envelope recipient, `Cc` is dropped, `Message-ID` and `Date` are replaced, and a `List-Unsubscribe` with two forms is corrupted - and has no setting for it. Mailgun's `messages.mime` endpoint delivered a probe byte for byte, adding only its own `Return-Path`, a `Sender`, two `X-Mailgun` headers and a DKIM signature that covers the list headers. Amazon SES, which Resend runs on, keeps `To` and `Cc` on a raw send but documents replacing `Message-ID`.

**Receiving.** `loop.heliosian.com` has to be a domain in Mailgun, verified for sending, with its MX records pointing at Mailgun, so that every message to any address under it lands there, and needs one route matching every recipient under the domain that forwards the message whole, `forward("https://loop.heliosian.com/hooks/mail/mime")` - the address ends in `mime`, which is what makes Mailgun post the raw message rather than its parsed parts; `docs/deploy.md` says how to read what the account holds. On a message, Mailgun calls the route with the recipient it matched, the sender, the From, the subject, and the message itself in `body-mime`, signed with the HTTP webhook signing key (`MAILGUN_WEBHOOK_KEY`; `docs/deploy.md`) - the HMAC-SHA256 of timestamp and token, which `mail.VerifyMailgun` checks, allowing a quarter of an hour of clock drift. The call comes as JSON or as a form and the handler reads either, up to forty megabytes (`mail.MaxNotification`), which holds the largest message Mailgun accepts, twenty-five megabytes, with room for the form's encoding of it. The mail path runs on the data model (`internal/db/loop.go`), writing as the system `loop` (`docs/datamodel.md`). An email list there is a `GROUP` with `mail`, its address its `slug` and its other addresses `ALIAS` rows naming it. A recipient that is an email list's address or alias makes the message a post to that list, once however many of its addresses the message named; `unsubscribe@loop.heliosian.com` is the mail form of unsubscribing (below), a name no email list may take or alias, so the token in its subject reaches no email list; anything else under the domain is logged and ignored. The handler answers 200 for what it took and 406 for a signature that does not check, which Mailgun does not retry; any other answer it retries for eight hours.

**The pipeline.** The handler keeps the message whole as `CONTENT` (`message/rfc822`, in the media bucket under its hash) and records, for each email list it was sent to, an inbound `MESSAGE` of kind `post` in state `received` - its subject, its `From` address and the person who has it, its `Message-ID` as `header_id` - all in one write before it answers, so that once Mailgun has its 200 the post is stored whatever happens next; a failed write is answered 500 and Mailgun brings the message again. A route fires once per matched recipient and a retried call carries the same message again; an email list that already holds a post of the same `CONTENT` takes nothing more. One worker then takes the posts in turn: drops a post (state `dropped`, the reason in `detail`) if it is one Loop already sent, auto-submitted, a mail system's notice, fails authentication (`mail.Authenticated`, over Mailgun's topmost `Authentication-Results`; `loop.md`, Mail) or is from a sender the list's `posting` leaves out, or its `replying` for a reply - one whose `In-Reply-To` or `References` names the `header_id` of a post of the list's that was sent - the last answered with a notice to its `From` through `messages.mime`; rewrites the headers (`loop.md`, Mail; `internal/db/loopmessage.go`), always putting the list's name in brackets before the subject; records an outbound `MESSAGE` under the post; and posts Mailgun one copy per effective member to `messages.mime`, the message as bytes with that member's primary address as the envelope recipient, each copy a `RECIPIENT` with when it was `sent`. The post ends `sent`, any copies that failed named in `detail`, or `failed` when none went or an earlier step failed, with the step and the error. A post that becomes `sent` is filed into Helios Ask's documents by the trigger that files every sent post (`docs/datamodel.md`), sent to its list. A restart, once the first refresh is in, takes up every post still `received`; a `failed` post is a person's to look at, and a message can be resent by posting again.

**Delivery.** The domain's Mailgun webhooks for delivery, permanent failure, temporary failure and complaint have to point at `POST /hooks/events` (public, the same signature, the signature object inside the JSON; `docs/deploy.md`, again, for reading where they point). Each names the copy's From - the email list's address, which says which email list - its recipient, its Message-ID and when it happened. A delivery sets the copy's `RECIPIENT.delivered`, a permanent failure its `failed` and `detail`; a temporary failure, which Mailgun retries for up to eight hours, and a complaint are logged and leave the copy as it stands. Reports gather while a write is in flight and go in the next one, so a burst from a large email list spends a few writes rather than one each. An event for a From that is not an email list's, or for a Message-ID no outbound post of that list has - the volunteer portal's mail and the posting notices, sent from `team@loop.heliosian.com` - is ignored.

**Unsubscribe.** Every copy's `List-Unsubscribe` names both forms: `mailto:unsubscribe@loop.heliosian.com?subject=<token>` and `https://loop.heliosian.com/open/unsubscribe/<token>`, the token the email list's address and the recipient's address signed with the session key, the email list found again by its `slug`, with `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` beside them. `GET` on the link is the page with the button; `POST`, from the button or from a mail app's one-click, makes the person's membership of the list `excluded`, its note saying which way, and answers an empty 200 to a one-click and the page to the button. Mail to the address comes through the same route as a post, and its subject, less any `Re:`, is the token; the sender is recorded but not checked, since the token reaches only the one person's copy. All of it is public (`internal/auth`); the signature is the whole of the check.

**Every other app** sends through Mailgun too, by its JSON endpoint (`mail.Mailgun.Send`), and the calendar's replies come in through a Mailgun route at `reply.heliosian.com` the same way Loop's posts do (`docs/deploy.md`).

`tools/loadcheck` prints every email list with its member count, its unsubscribed count, its subject switch, its visibility, its posting and its replying.
