# Data model

The tabs, columns, and validation rules are in `internal/loop`; this file carries only what reading that code cannot tell you.

The email lists live in one Google Sheet, `Groups`, in the community shared drive, reached through drive membership like the other sheets. `GROUPS_SHEET` names it, and `tools/createtabs` reads that variable to lay it out from an empty spreadsheet titled `Groups`, or to add the tabs and columns an older one lacks. The sheet's tabs and columns call an email list a group.

## Tabs

- `Groups` — Group ID, Name, Title, Description, Created By, Created, Subject Prefix, Visible, Posting, Replying. One row per email list, keyed by Group ID: the email list's ID, minted when the email list is made (`id.New` in `internal/id`), which every other tab's Group column holds and every request to the API names the email list by. Name is the address's local part, fixed once made (What stays keyed by the name, below). Subject Prefix is `off` for an email list whose subjects go out as written; blank, or anything else, means the title goes in front. Visible is `everyone` for an email list everyone in Loop sees, `members` for one the people on it see, and `hidden` or blank for one its managers and the admins alone see (`loop.md`, Visible); any other value refuses the load. Posting is `members` for an email list whose managers and members alone may post, `managers` for one whose managers alone may, and `everyone` or blank for one anyone may post to (`loop.md`, Posting); any other value refuses the load. Replying takes the same values for who may reply to the email list's own mail (`loop.md`, Replying).
- `Managers` — Group, Email. One row per manager per email list; every email list needs at least one.
- `Rules` — Group, Kind, Roles, Search, Classrooms, Grades, Tags, Family. One row per rule, in the order the editor shows them. Kind is `include` or `exclude`. Roles, Classrooms, Grades, Tags and Family are comma-separated lists; Tags holds tags by their ID in Who?'s `Tag List` (`tag:<id>`, whoever wrote the rule; `docs/who/data.md`, Tags are kept by ID) and Magic Tag keys (`party:<id>`, `activity:<id>`, `room:<band>`), each read for the email list's managers (`loop.md`, A rule discloses the tags it names); a tag named any other way refuses the load. A tag with a comma in its name cannot be used in a rule. Every email list needs at least one include rule, and every rule at least one of the facets.
- `Additions` — Group, Email, Name. One row per person added by hand from outside the directory (`loop.md`, Addition). Email is lowercased and must be an address; Name may be blank, the address standing in for it. An email list holds at most two hundred, once each by address. The load never asks the directory about them, so an address that has since joined the directory is not a refused load; the app refuses one at save alone.
- `Excluded` — Group, Email, Note, Timestamp. One row per address kept off an email list (`loop.md`, Excluded): the address, lowercased; a note saying why, which an unsubscribe fills in itself (`Unsubscribed by one-click`, `by the page`, `by mail from <address>`, `by the email list's page in Loop`) and a manager types by hand, up to a hundred and twenty characters; and when it went on. An email list holds at most two hundred, once each by address, listed by when they went on, a blank Timestamp last. The evaluator leaves them off whatever the rules and additions say; a manager's Remove in the editor deletes the row, as does the person's own Resubscribe on the email list's page (`POST /api/loop/subscription`, which adds the row with `subscribed` false and deletes it with `subscribed` true, for someone the rules or additions place on an email list they can see).
- `Aliases` — Alias, ID. One row per other local part an email list answers as (`loop.md`, Email list), ID the email list's Group ID; the shape every sheet's Aliases tab has (`internal/id`), read by `id.ParseAliases`, which lowercases the alias and refuses a blank one, a repeated one or an ID that is not one. Each is held to the same form as a name and unique across every email list's name and aliases: a load with an alias that is another email list's name or alias, or an email list named for another's alias, refuses. At most ten per email list.
- `Messages` — ID, Group, Received, From, Subject, State, Recipients, Object, Detail, Message ID. One row per message per email list it was addressed to, keyed by ID (sixteen hex characters of the SHA-256 of the message as Mailgun handed it over, `idOf` in `internal/loop/mail.go`, so the same message delivered again reads as the same row) and Group: when it came, who from, the subject, where it stands (Mail, below), how many copies went out, the archive object holding it whole, what went wrong or why it was dropped, and the message's own `Message-ID` without its angle brackets. Written by the server alone; read back to resume what a restart cut short and, for the rows `sent`, as the email list's History (`loop.md`, Pages), newest Received first. The archive it indexes is the record; the rows go with the email list when it is deleted, the archive stays. A Source column in the production sheet is unread and can be dropped.
- `Deliveries` — Timestamp, Group, Email, Event, Message, Detail. One row per thing that happened to one copy: which email list's, to whom, what (`sent` when Mailgun took the copy, then `delivered`, `delivery_delayed`, `bounced` or `complained` as Mailgun reports them, at Mailgun's time for the event), the copy's Message-ID, and Mailgun's words for anything but a delivery. Appended by the server, never edited, batched: the rows gather for five seconds and go in one commit, so a burst of deliveries from a large email list spends one commit rather than one each. It is the store's one append-only tab (`docs/storage.md`, Store): the store refuses to edit a row of it and logs none of it in the Change Log, which would otherwise grow by several rows for every copy of every post. History shows each under the `Messages` row whose Message ID is the same, angle brackets aside; the copies Loop sends keep the sender's `Message-ID`, which is what makes the two meet. A copy's standing is read from its rows: delivered once it has a `delivered` row, failed once it has a `bounced` one and no delivery, and pending until then, delays included. A message with no `sent` rows shows only the copies Mailgun reported on.
- `Admins` — Email. Who sees and may change every email list, beyond the platform super admins (`docs/config.md`).
- `Archived` — Group, Email. One row per person per email list they have archived for themselves (`loop.md`, Archived): the address, lowercased. Archive on the email list's page adds the row once and Unarchive deletes it (`POST /api/loop/archive`); a row naming an email list the sheet does not have refuses the load, like every other tab's.
- `Change Log` — the store's, one row per changed cell holding what it held before and one per inserted row (`docs/storage.md`). Never read back. An unsubscribe or resubscribe is made as the person themselves, with Real Actor blank for one by link or by mail, which nobody signed in makes; the mailer's own writes - a message received, its state, its deliveries - are made as `loop mailer`.

**A load either succeeds whole or refuses**, the stance every app takes: an email list with a Group ID missing, malformed or another email list's, an email list with no manager or no include rule, a rule, addition, exclusion or alias naming an email list the sheet does not have, an unknown kind, role, relation, visibility, posting or replying, a name or alias that cannot be an address, or an alias two email lists would share refuses the load, so a hand edit that breaks a rule surfaces as a refused start or a failed refresh rather than an email list quietly matching nobody. The app checks the same rules before it writes, so nothing it saves can refuse the next load. `Messages` and `Deliveries` are records, not rules: a row of either naming an email list the sheet does not have is kept and ignored.

**Saving writes what changed.** A save sets the email list's `Groups` row, adds and deletes `Managers` and `Aliases` rows by address and alias, sets or deletes `Additions` and `Excluded` rows by address, and, when any rule changed, replaces the email list's `Rules` rows in the editor's order, so the sheet's rows for an email list are always what the editor last held and the Change Log holds only what the save changed (`groupOps` in `internal/loop/handlers.go`). Deleting the `Groups` row takes the email list's `Managers`, `Rules`, `Additions`, `Excluded`, `Aliases`, `Archived`, `Messages` and `Deliveries` rows with it in the same commit (`carryGroup` in `internal/loop/cache.go`), and its posts among Helios Ask's documents (`docs/ask/artifacts.md`, What is in it and what is not) are removed on the write queue after; the buckets are written, never emptied, so the archived messages and the documents' objects stay, named by no row and read by nothing. The name goes back into the pool with the email list, and an email list made later under it gets an ID of its own, so none of these records reach it.

## What stays keyed by the name

The name is the email list's address and never changes, so what leaves the sheet - in mail, in links, in another store - carries the name rather than the ID, and finds the email list by it (`Model.Named`, exact, never through an alias):

- the mail's own headers: `List-Id`, `List-Post`, `X-Helios-Loop`, and the email list's address in `From` (`rewrite.go`);
- the unsubscribe token, the name and the address signed together (Unsubscribe, below), so every token already in someone's mailbox keeps working;
- the archive's object names, `loop/<name>/...`, and so the `Messages` rows' Object;
- Helios Ask's documents of an email list's posts, whose Channel is the name (`docs/ask/artifacts.md`); re-keying them would mean embedding every post again;
- the email list's page, `/groups/<name>`, and every link to it: Team's email list link (the name `Tagged` in `internal/loop/lists.go` returns) and the email list's Magic Tag in Who?, keyed `group:<Group ID>` and carrying the name as its `slug` for the link.

## The membership

An email list's members are never stored. They are computed from the directory model, the tags the rules name (`who.Model.Tags`, which a tool reads off a model it loaded itself), each read while one of the email list's managers owns it or is among its managers (`who.Model.SharedTags`), and the managers' Magic Tags (`Lists` on the Celebrate and HCA-Team models, plus the directory's room-parent lists) whenever anything asks: the email list's page, the editor's preview (which also asks it how many people each rule touches, `RuleCounts`), every forward and `tools/loadcheck` all call the one evaluator, `internal/filter` - Heliosian's audiences read through it too - which `internal/loop/eval.go` reads an email list's rules through, reading a person's grade and classroom as every filter does - `who.Model.Facets`, the server's counterpart of Who?'s client-side `personFacets`: a student's own, a parent's children's, none for staff, and the same order: the roles keep their kind after Add family has widened the matches. The rules' result is then unioned with the email list's additions, each on once by address whatever the exclude rules say, with `Added` as its reason, and the excluded addresses taken away last. The tests pin it to the sample community.

The email list's Magic Tag in Who? (`Model.Lists` in `internal/loop/lists.go`) sorts the same members by whether the directory holds them: the directory's people are the list's People, and the rest are its Guests under the name the manager typed, keyed `<Group ID>:<address>` - the one `who.Guest` shape a party's ticket holders outside the directory use, keyed by the ticket there.

## Mail

**Why Mailgun.** Loop needs a sender that takes a message as bytes and passes it on as they are: `To` and `Cc` as the sender wrote them, so reply-all reaches the email list, and the sender's `Message-ID`, so every member's copy is one message and replies thread. Resend, which every other app sends through, rebuilds every message on both its API and its SMTP relay - `To` becomes the envelope recipient, `Cc` is dropped, `Message-ID` and `Date` are replaced, and a `List-Unsubscribe` with two forms is corrupted - and has no setting for it. Mailgun's `messages.mime` endpoint delivered a probe byte for byte, adding only its own `Return-Path`, a `Sender`, two `X-Mailgun` headers and a DKIM signature that covers the list headers. Amazon SES, which Resend runs on, keeps `To` and `Cc` on a raw send but documents replacing `Message-ID`.

**Receiving.** `loop.heliosian.com` has to be a domain in Mailgun, verified for sending, with its MX records pointing at Mailgun, so that every message to any address under it lands there, and needs one route matching every recipient under the domain that forwards the message whole, `forward("https://loop.heliosian.com/hooks/mail/mime")` - the address ends in `mime`, which is what makes Mailgun post the raw message rather than its parsed parts; `docs/deploy.md` says how to read what the account holds. On a message, Mailgun calls the route with the recipient it matched, the sender, the From, the subject, and the message itself in `body-mime`, signed with the HTTP webhook signing key (`MAILGUN_WEBHOOK_KEY`; `docs/deploy.md`) - the HMAC-SHA256 of timestamp and token, which `mail.VerifyMailgun` checks, allowing a quarter of an hour of clock drift. The call comes as JSON or as a form and the handler reads either, up to forty megabytes (`mail.MaxNotification`), which holds the largest message Mailgun accepts, twenty-five megabytes, with room for the form's encoding of it. A recipient that is an email list's name or alias in the sheet becomes a job under the email list's ID, one job however many of its addresses the message named; `unsubscribe@loop.heliosian.com` is the mail form of unsubscribing (below), a name no email list may take or alias, and it is left out of the recipients that become jobs, so the token in its subject reaches no email list; anything else under the domain is logged and ignored. The handler answers 200 for what it took and 406 for a signature that does not check, which Mailgun does not retry; any other answer it retries for eight hours.

**The pipeline.** For each email list a message was sent to, the handler writes the message whole to the mail bucket as `loop/<name>/<time>-<id>.eml` and records a `Messages` row in state `received` naming the object and the message's `Message-ID`, committing it with `CommitAndWait` so the row is in the sheet (`docs/storage.md`, Store), both before it answers, so that once Mailgun has its 200 the message is on disk and in the sheet whatever happens next; a bucket write or a row write that fails is answered 500, nothing of that email list's is kept, and Mailgun brings the message again. A route fires once per matched recipient and a retried call carries the same message again, and the row, with an in-memory record of what this process has handled, is what makes a second delivery do nothing. One worker (`mailer` in `internal/loop/mail.go`) then takes the messages in turn: reads the archived copy back from the bucket; drops it if it is one Loop already sent, auto-submitted, a mail system's notice, fails authentication (`authenticated` in `rewrite.go`, over Mailgun's topmost `Authentication-Results`; `loop.md`, Mail) or is from a sender the email list's Posting leaves out, or its Replying for a reply - one whose `In-Reply-To` or `References` names the Message ID of a `sent` row of the email list's - the `From` address resolved through the directory's aliases, checked by `Group.PostableBy` - the last answered with a notice to its `From` through `messages.mime` (state `dropped`, with why; the archived copy stays, as everything received does); rewrites the headers (`loop.md`, Mail; `rewrite.go`); works out the members as they stand; and posts Mailgun one copy per member to `messages.mime`, the message as bytes with that one member as the envelope recipient. The row ends `sent` with the count of copies that went, and any that failed named in Detail, or `failed` when none did or an earlier step failed, with the step and the error. A post recorded `sent` is filed at once into Helios Ask's documents under its email list's name, for the email list's managers and members to ask about (`docs/ask/artifacts.md`, What is in it and what is not); a filing that fails is logged and the row stands. A restart re-queues every row still `received`, reading its object from the bucket again, so a message the instance died on goes out on the next start; a `failed` row is a person's to look at, and a message can be resent by hand by posting again.

**Delivery.** Each copy Mailgun accepts is recorded in `Deliveries` as `sent`. The domain's Mailgun webhooks for delivery, permanent failure, temporary failure and complaint have to point at `POST /hooks/events` (public, the same signature, the signature object inside the JSON; `docs/deploy.md`, again, for reading where they point). Each names the copy's From - the email list's address, which says which email list - its recipient, its Message-ID and when it happened, and is appended to `Deliveries` as `delivered`, `bounced`, `delivery_delayed` or `complained`. Mailgun reports a temporary failure as failed and retries the copy for up to eight hours, so a `delivery_delayed` copy is still pending, and a later delivery or a permanent failure settles it. An event for a From that is not under Loop's domain, or for a Message-ID the email list's `Messages` rows do not hold - the volunteer portal's mail and the posting notices, sent from `team@loop.heliosian.com` - is not a post of Loop's and is ignored.

**The archive.** Every message forwarded or dropped is in `gs://heliosian-mail` (`docs/deploy.md`), whole, as received, under the email list and the time; nothing here serves it, and what Helios Ask reads of a sent post is its own document, filed as the post is sent. `Messages` is its index; a deleted email list's objects stay, unindexed, since nothing deletes from the bucket. Mailgun's own three-day copy is the working store, not the record.

**Unsubscribe.** Every copy's `List-Unsubscribe` names both forms: `mailto:unsubscribe@loop.heliosian.com?subject=<token>` and `https://loop.heliosian.com/open/unsubscribe/<token>`, the token the email list's name and the recipient's address signed with the session key (`internal/loop/unsubscribe.go`), the email list found again by that name, with `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` beside them. `GET` on the link is the page with the button; `POST`, from the button or from a mail app's one-click, adds the `Excluded` row with a note saying which way and applies it in memory at once, and answers an empty 200 to a one-click and the page to the button. Mail to the address comes through the same route as a post, and its subject, less any `Re:`, is the token; the sender is recorded but not checked, since the token reaches only the one person's copy. All of it is public (`internal/auth`); the signature is the whole of the check.

**Every other app** sends through Mailgun too, by its JSON endpoint (`mail.Mailgun.Send`), and the calendar's replies come in through a Mailgun route at `reply.heliosian.com` the same way Loop's posts do; Resend is gone (`docs/deploy.md`).

`tools/loadcheck` prints every email list with its member count, its unsubscribed count, its subject switch, its visibility, its posting and its replying.
