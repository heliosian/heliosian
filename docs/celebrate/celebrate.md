# Helios Celebrate

Helios Celebrate is the site for the community's fun(d)raiser parties: members of the community host a party - a fondue night, a pickleball mixer, a wine tasting - and families take tickets, which the school invoices later. It serves at celebrate.heliosian.com. Everything it shows and changes is the data model (`docs/datamodel.md`); how a celebration, party, ticket, waitlist place and payment are held there is `data.md`. The pages (`web/celebrate/`) read it in one `/api/q` batch (`loadModel` in `state.js`) and write it through `/api/q` batches (`edit.js`), so who may do what is the policies' alone (`internal/db/policy.go`); the server serves the shell, the redirects ahead of sign-in, the previews and share cards (`PartyPages` in `internal/db/partypages.go`) and the letters (`internal/db/partymail.go`).

## Who may do what

- **Anyone signed in** sees every open party, who is coming and who hosts it. Everyone but a student takes tickets for themselves, their household, anyone else in the directory the party is for, and guests, billed to themselves or another adult in their household; a student browses, and the page says to ask a parent where the button would be. A guest is anyone outside the directory, by name and optionally an address. Nobody is made twice (`knownGuest` in `web/celebrate/edit.js`): a typed address goes to `POST /api/do/person-at`, which answers who already holds it, and that person takes the place; a typed name that matches someone in the buyer's family picks them in Who's coming instead; a name that matches anyone else in the directory asks whether the place is theirs, and makes the guest only if the buyer says no; and a name that matches a guest someone has brought before - to any party, by any family - uses that guest again. Someone in the directory outside the buyer's family holds the place as the buyer's guest (`guest_of`). Once a party is full a family joins its waitlist instead, asking for as many places as it wants and leaving a note; it changes how many places it asks for, or leaves, at any time. A ticket is never given back - it is a fundraiser - but its holder, their household, whoever brought them or the hosts pass it to someone else (Reassign), and its payment stays as it was.
- **Anyone signed in** hosts a party under a celebration that takes parties (Admin Tools › Celebrations, "Takes parties"): it waits as pending, seen by its hosts and Celebrate admins, until an admin approves it. Under a celebration that takes none, only admins add parties.
- **Hosts** - the members of a party's managers group - edit it in place and in Edit Event and Edit Tickets, add and remove hosts, add tickets, paid (billed to whoever the Invoice picker names) or free (no payment, optionally raising the capacity by one so the gift takes no paid place), remove anyone, pass anyone's ticket on, and give waitlisted families their places. Giving a place takes the request off the waitlist and writes a payment and a ticket for each place asked for, billed to whoever asked. A host is never refused for room. A host never approves their own party.
- **Celebrate admins** - the super admins and the members of Celebrate's admins group (Admin Tools › Admins) - approve, hide and delete parties (an empty one only), edit any, move a party to another celebration or category, keep the celebrations, categories and settings, and say where each payment stands, who pays it and which Stripe invoice bills it.

## Pages

One shell, `web/celebrate/index.html`, routes every page on the client: a left rail (Parties with its tabs and counts, My Family's Parties with the household, Hosting, Approval Needed for admins, and Host a Party where the current celebration takes parties) beside the shared top bar (`docs/toolbar.md`), whose search finds the shown celebration's parties by title, subtitle, summary, host, chip, category and place, and people by name - hosts, guests and the waitlisted - one row per party with their role, then under a divider the celebration before.

- **Parties** (`/` for the latest celebration, the open one with the latest `start`; `/celebrations/{slug}` for another year) - the site opens on the latest celebration, and every page names one: a celebration's own address, a party's celebration, and otherwise the one last opened (`celebrationAt` in `web/celebrate/state.js`). A celebration that names no Gather invitation shows its parties under a band: its picture under a wash, theme and title, date and place, and a button for each link in its description, with Add to Calendar while it is still to come. One that names a Gather invitation the viewer can see takes its address for the invitation alone, its parties at `/parties` (`/celebrations/{slug}/parties` for an earlier year) and its guest list at `/guests` (`/celebrations/{slug}/guests`) (`partiesPath`, `guestsPath` and `invitationPath` in `web/celebrate/state.js`); a faint arrow above the invitation's seal in the rail opens every celebration, each with its invitation's seal and year, and picking one opens it (`yearSwitch` in `web/celebrate/chrome.js`). The invitation page (`invitationPage` in `web/celebrate/pages/parties.js`) draws, in the invitation's design, its card with its pages folded above and below it to turn to, and the household's RSVP with the invitation's questions and Add to Calendar, all from the shared modules Gather draws them with (`web/common/invitation.js`, `web/common/invites.js`, `web/public/common/envelope.js`, `sheets.js` and `themes.js`), so an answer here is the same answer as on Gather and When. While it is, the top of the rail is the invitation in place of Celebrate's logo, as Gather's sidebar shows it (`invitationNav` in `web/celebrate/chrome.js`, through `invitationHead`): its seal and title, linking to the invitation page, its day and hours with Add to Google Calendar, its place with a map link and who hosts it, then Invitation and a link to each of its pages, which turns the deck on the invitation page to that page (`?page={id}`), and, where the hosts show the list or the viewer is one of them, Who's coming (Guest list, for the hosts); and the celebration's own site theme, or else the invitation's, dresses every page of the site for that viewer (`siteTheme` in `web/celebrate/app.js`): its backdrop behind the pages, its sidebar colors on the rail, laid see-through over the backdrop as Gather's is, and the top bar (`sidebarIn`, the rules Gather's chrome uses in `web/common/toolbar.css`), its accent on the buttons and highlights, and its title font and rule on the page headings; the invitation's title and subtitle sit at the left of the top bar, as on Gather (`topbarLine`), and the parties list, a party's page, Who's coming, My Family's Parties and Hosting are each drawn as one panel in the theme's card colors (`themed-panel` in `web/celebrate/style.css`), a line above the heading naming the celebration or the invitation, and their tabs, filters, party cards, guest cards, family rows, answer pills and the Students, Parents and Staff chips in the same colors and fonts. A link to any of these previews with the invitation's own card, drawn in the celebration's site theme when it has one (`PartyPages.PreviewHead` and `shareCard` in `internal/db/partypages.go`). The parties list is of the page's celebration in four tabs - Upcoming, Available, Waitlist and Past Parties - with a filter by that celebration's categories; the tab and category are the address's (`?show=`, `&category=`). A card is the picture with its chips, the date stamp, the title, subtitle and summary, the household's own tickets and waitlist places, and a foot with Get Tickets or Join Waitlist and how the room stands.
- **Party** (`/g/{slug}`, or `/g/{id}` for a party with no slug, its generated `path`. Ahead of sign-in, `/g/{key}` naming a party by its id, its slug in any case, or an `ALIAS` - an old Party ID - is sent there with a 301, and an address one of Celebrate's `REDIRECT` rows (app `celebrate`) holds as `old` is sent to its `new` with a 302, matched whole, ignoring case and a trailing slash (`PartyPages.Redirected`); then Celebrate's old prefixes (`Moved` in `internal/app/wiring.go`) are sent to the same rest under `/g/` with a 301) - the wide banner with the shared date card, the chips above the title, the description drawn as markdown (`web/public/common/markdown.js`) with its callouts, the ticket band, the family's own tickets with Reassign, Who's Coming by family or face, the waitlist in order, and for admins an approval band on a pending party; beside it the hosts' invite card (Create Invite on Helios When before an invitation goes out, then RSVPs on Helios When, with each face wearing its answer), Where with a map link, tickets and price, the hosts, the flyer and a mail to the hosts. Hosts open a face's ticket - who is billed, the price, the note, Reassign or Give the places, Remove - and the attendee contact list.
- **Who's coming** (`/guests`, or `/celebrations/{slug}/guests`) - the guest list of the invitation the celebration names, drawn by `comingCard` (`web/common/invites.js`) as Gather's own page draws it; there for the invitation's hosts and, where the hosts show the list, for everyone who sees the invitation.
- **My Family's Parties** (`/my`, `/my/{name}`) - the parties anyone in the household holds a ticket to or waits for, or one member's alone.
- **Hosting** (`/hosting`) - the parties the viewer hosts, each with what sold, who waits and what it raised (the sum of its payments not cancelled); for admins also Approval Needed and All Parties.
- **Admin Tools** (`/admin`) - Celebrations (each named with its year, as every celebration picker in Admin Tools names it; added from its Gather invitation alone - picked from the admin's own, those they host and then those they are invited to, less any a celebration already names, or by a pasted link, `/g/{key}` resolved by ID, slug or alias among the invitations the admin can see - its code made from the invitation's slug and year; each one's code, title, the Gather invitation it is drawn from, its site theme - one of the site's Gather themes, or one the admin uploads there through Gather's own upload and theme kit, in place of the invitation's - or else its theme line, when, where, address, description and picture, whether its tickets are on sale, and whether it takes parties); Categories (each celebration's own, added, renamed, reordered and deleted when empty); Settings (`Parties Intro`, `Ticket Note`); Payments (the shown celebration's payments by payer, filtered by party, status and words, with totals, each row's status and payer changed in place, and Record invoice on a payer with owed rows, which writes a Stripe invoice ID to the owed rows picked and marks them Requested); and Admins.

The toolbar's hourglass counts the pending parties under something the viewer runs, so a Celebrate admin sees every one and a host none of their own (`initAlerts` in `web/common/alerts.js`).

## The ticket window

Get Tickets asks who pays (an adult of the household, by face), who is coming (the household by face, grayed with the reason for anyone already holding a place or outside who the party is for) plus guests added by name, and a note, and tallies the places and the total. It writes, in one batch, a payment at the party's price for each place and then the ticket; when the room runs out mid-purchase, the places past it go on the waitlist in the same batch. Join the Waitlist asks how many places; extra places are unnamed guests of the asker, which a host's Give the places turns into tickets the family passes on later.

## Editing

A party's words are its description, in markdown: its first paragraph, not counting chips and callouts, is the card's summary; a line `> [!chip] Adults` puts a chip above the title and on the card; `> [!note] 📣 Good to know` starts a callout titled by what follows the marker, a leading emoji drawn large in place of the icon. "Hosted by" is the hosts' names. Edit Tickets holds the price and what a ticket covers, the capacity and minimum, whether tickets are on sale, whether a full party takes a waitlist (off only while nobody waits), who may hold a ticket (adults, students or both) and the drop-off and parent-ticket switches. The friendly address is the party's slug; a taken one is refused on save. Pictures go through `/api/do/photo` and flyers through `/api/do/flyer`, uploaded, or found with the image search at `/api/celebrate/images`.

## Sign-in and install

A link to a party previews in chat apps and social feeds even though the site is behind sign-in: the sign-in page served at a party's address (`/g/{slug}`, or `/g/{id}` for a party with no slug; every other way of naming it redirects there first) carries the party's Open Graph tags (title; the day and hours, the place in words and the start of its description, without its chips and callout markers; the address asked for), and `/open/share/{id}.png` is a public 1200x630 card drawn on the server (`PartyPages`, over `internal/sharecard`): on the pale wash, the mark and "Helios Celebrate", the title in deep teal with the yellow swoosh, then the day behind a calendar glyph, the hours behind a clock and the place behind a pin; on the right the party's flyer shown whole, or without one its picture scaled to cover the panel. Every party previews and has a card, whatever its status, as every event and activity does (`docs/dev.md`); any other page gets the upcoming parties, the next one first, and `/open/share/upcoming.png` their card. The tags say what a poster on the wall would say - never the street address, never who is coming.

Everything sits behind Google sign-in restricted to the school domain, sharing a session with the other apps on the same tier. It installs to a home screen with the sun-over-the-hills mark under `web/public/celebrate/brand/` (cut from the app's exports, `docs/brand.md`: the app icon square for the home-screen icons, the bare mark for the favicons, the app switch and the maskables, the vertical lockups for the rail and the login page, and the iOS splash screens under `brand/splash/`).

## Email

The membership mailer sends Celebrate's letters as the data changes, one per write per party, from Celebrate's address with the hosts on Reply-To (`partymail.go`, `docs/datamodel.md`, Groups): places taken bring "Your tickets" to whoever pays - the holder too, and a student's parents, when a place goes to someone else - copying the hosts and the writer, with the places, the total, who is billed and the `Ticket Note` setting, or Free when no payment names them, and a calendar invite when the party has a start; places on the waitlist bring "You're on the waitlist" to the family and "Waitlist for …" to the hosts with Reply-To the family; a ticket passed on brings its new holder and a student's parents "…, you're going!". A write in import mode mails nobody. Invitations and RSVPs are Helios When's guest list for the party (`docs/when/calendar.md`, Invitations), whose mail and calendar invites carry the description without its chip lines and callout markers. The invitation a celebration names sends its own mail from Gather (`docs/gather/gather.md`, Mail), but the button in what a guest from the directory gets - the invitation, its updates and reminders, the calendar invite and a host's reply to their note - opens Celebrate's invitation page (`guestLink` in `internal/db/invitemail.go`); a guest from outside still gets their page on Gather, and the hosts' notices still open Gather.
