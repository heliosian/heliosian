# Helios Celebrate

Helios Celebrate is the site for the community's fun(d)raiser parties: members of the community host a party - a fondue night, a pickleball mixer, a wine tasting - and families take tickets, which the school invoices later. It serves at celebrate.heliosian.com. Everything it shows and changes is the data model (`docs/datamodel.md`); how a celebration, party, ticket, waitlist place and payment are held there is `data.md`. The pages (`web/celebrate/`) read it in one `/api/q` batch (`loadModel` in `state.js`) and write it through `/api/q` batches (`edit.js`), so who may do what is the policies' alone (`internal/db/policy.go`); the server serves the shell, the redirects ahead of sign-in, the previews and share cards (`PartyPages` in `internal/db/partypages.go`) and the letters (`internal/db/partymail.go`).

## Who may do what

- **Anyone signed in** sees every open party, who is coming and who hosts it. Everyone but a student takes tickets for themselves, their household and guests, billed to themselves or another adult in their household; a student browses, and the page says to ask a parent where the button would be. A guest is anyone outside the directory, by name and optionally an address. A guest someone has brought before - to any party, by any family - is the same guest again: a typed address goes to `POST /api/do/person-at`, which answers who already holds it, and a typed name that matches a known guest uses them, so nobody is made twice. An address that belongs to someone in the directory outside the buyer's family is refused with the reason; the party's hosts add them. Once a party is full a family joins its waitlist instead, asking for as many places as it wants and leaving a note; it changes how many places it asks for, or leaves, at any time. A ticket is never given back - it is a fundraiser - but its holder, their household, whoever brought them or the hosts pass it to someone else (Reassign), and its payment stays as it was.
- **Anyone signed in** hosts a party under a celebration that takes parties (Admin Tools › Celebrations, "Takes parties"): it waits as pending, seen by its hosts and Celebrate admins, until an admin approves it. Under a celebration that takes none, only admins add parties.
- **Hosts** - the members of a party's managers group - edit it in place and in Edit Event and Edit Tickets, add and remove hosts, add tickets, paid (billed to whoever the Invoice picker names) or free (no payment, optionally raising the capacity by one so the gift takes no paid place), remove anyone, pass anyone's ticket on, and give waitlisted families their places. Giving a place takes the request off the waitlist and writes a payment and a ticket for each place asked for, billed to whoever asked. A host is never refused for room. A host never approves their own party.
- **Celebrate admins** - the super admins and the members of Celebrate's admins group (Admin Tools › Admins) - approve, hide and delete parties (an empty one only), edit any, move a party to another celebration or category, keep the celebrations, categories and settings, and say where each payment stands and who pays it.

## Pages

One shell, `web/celebrate/index.html`, routes every page on the client: a left rail (Parties with its tabs and counts, My Family's Parties with the household, Hosting, Approval Needed for admins, and Host a Party where the current celebration takes parties) beside the shared top bar (`docs/toolbar.md`), whose search finds the shown celebration's parties by title, subtitle, summary, host, chip, category and place, and people by name - hosts, guests and the waitlisted - one row per party with their role, then under a divider the celebration before.

- **Parties** (`/`, or `/celebrations/{slug}` for another year) - the banner celebration in a band across the top: its picture under a wash, theme and title, date and place, and a button for each link in its description, with Add to Calendar while it is still to come. Then the parties of the current celebration (or the one picked) in four tabs - Upcoming, Available, Waitlist and Past Parties - with a filter by that celebration's categories; the tab and category are the address's (`?show=`, `&category=`). A card is the picture with its chips, the date stamp, the title, subtitle and summary, the household's own tickets and waitlist places, and a foot with Get Tickets or Join Waitlist and how the room stands.
- **Party** (`/p/{slug}`, or `/p/{id}` for a party with no slug, the address `db.Link` gives it. Ahead of sign-in, `/p/{key}` or `/parties/{key}` naming a party by its id, its slug in any case, or an `ALIAS` - an old Party ID - is sent there with a 301, and an address one of Celebrate's `REDIRECT` rows (app `celebrate`) holds as `old` is sent to its `new` with a 302, matched whole, ignoring case and a trailing slash; `PartyPages.Redirected`) - the wide banner with the shared date card, the chips above the title, the description drawn as markdown (`web/common/markdown.js`) with its callouts, the ticket band, the family's own tickets with Reassign, Who's Coming by family or face, the waitlist in order, and for admins an approval band on a pending party; beside it the hosts' invite card (Create Invite on Helios When before an invitation goes out, then RSVPs on Helios When, with each face wearing its answer), Where with a map link, tickets and price, the hosts, the flyer and a mail to the hosts. Hosts open a face's ticket - who is billed, the price, the note, Reassign or Give the places, Remove - and the attendee contact list.
- **My Family's Parties** (`/my`, `/my/{name}`) - the parties anyone in the household holds a ticket to or waits for, or one member's alone.
- **Hosting** (`/hosting`) - the parties the viewer hosts, each with what sold, who waits and what it raised (the sum of its payments not cancelled); for admins also Approval Needed and All Parties.
- **Admin Tools** (`/admin`) - Banner (which celebration the band advertises, the `Banner` setting); Celebrations (each one's code, title, theme, when, where, address, description, picture, whether it is the current one - the `Current` setting - and whether it takes parties); Categories (each celebration's own, added, renamed, reordered and deleted when empty); Settings (`Parties Intro`, `Ticket Note`); Payments (the shown celebration's payments by payer, filtered by party, status and words, with totals, each row's status and payer changed in place); and Admins.

The toolbar's hourglass counts the pending parties under something the viewer runs, so a Celebrate admin sees every one and a host none of their own (`initAlerts` in `web/common/alerts.js`).

## The ticket window

Get Tickets asks who pays (an adult of the household, by face), who is coming (the household by face, greyed with the reason for anyone already holding a place or outside who the party is for) plus guests added by name, and a note, and tallies the places and the total. It writes, in one batch, a payment at the party's price for each place and then the ticket; when the room runs out mid-purchase, the places past it go on the waitlist in the same batch. Join the Waitlist asks how many places; extra places are unnamed guests of the asker, which a host's Give the places turns into tickets the family passes on later.

## Editing

A party's words are its description, in markdown: its first paragraph, not counting chips and callouts, is the card's summary; a line `> [!chip] Adults` puts a chip above the title and on the card; `> [!note] 📣 Good to know` starts a callout titled by what follows the marker, a leading emoji drawn large in place of the icon. "Hosted by" is the hosts' names. Edit Tickets holds the price and what a ticket covers, the capacity and minimum, whether tickets are on sale, whether a full party takes a waitlist (off only while nobody waits), who may hold a ticket (adults, students or both) and the drop-off and parent-ticket switches. The friendly address is the party's slug; a taken one is refused on save. Pictures go through `/api/do/photo`, found or uploaded with the image tools at `/api/celebrate/image`.

## Sign-in and install

A link to a party previews in chat apps and social feeds even though the site is behind sign-in: the sign-in page served at a party's address (`/p/{slug}`, or `/p/{id}` for a party with no slug; every other way of naming it redirects there first) carries the party's Open Graph tags (title; the day and hours, the place in words and the start of its description, without its chips and callout markers; the address asked for), and `/open/share/{id}.png` is a public 1200x630 card drawn on the server (`PartyPages`, over `internal/sharecard`): on the pale wash, the mark and "Helios Celebrate", the title in deep teal with the yellow swoosh, then the day behind a calendar glyph, the hours behind a clock and the place behind a pin; on the right the party's flyer shown whole, or without one its picture scaled to cover the panel. Every party previews and has a card, whatever its status, as every event and activity does (`docs/dev.md`); any other page gets the upcoming parties, the next one first, and `/open/share/upcoming.png` their card. The tags say what a poster on the wall would say - never the street address, never who is coming.

Everything sits behind Google sign-in restricted to the school domain, sharing a session with the other apps on the same tier. It installs to a home screen with the sun-over-the-hills mark under `web/public/celebrate/brand/` (cut from the app's exports, `docs/brand.md`: the app icon square for the home-screen icons, the bare mark for the favicons, the app switch and the maskables, the vertical lockups for the rail and the login page, and the iOS splash screens under `brand/splash/`).

## Email

The membership mailer sends Celebrate's letters as the data changes, one per write per party, from Celebrate's address with the hosts on Reply-To (`partymail.go`, `docs/datamodel.md`, Groups): places taken bring "Your tickets" to whoever pays - the holder too, and a student's parents, when a place goes to someone else - copying the hosts and the writer, with the places, the total, who is billed and the `Ticket Note` setting, or Free when no payment names them, and a calendar invite when the party has a start; places on the waitlist bring "You're on the waitlist" to the family and "Waitlist for …" to the hosts with Reply-To the family; a ticket passed on brings its new holder and a student's parents "…, you're going!". A write in import mode mails nobody. Invitations and RSVPs are Helios When's guest list for the party (`docs/when/calendar.md`, Invitations), whose mail and calendar invites carry the description without its chip lines and callout markers.
