# Home app

Heliosian is the community's front door: one page of links, grouped into categories, to everything the Helios Community Association and the school run elsewhere. It serves at the apex, the first of `HomeApp`'s `Hosts` in `internal/model/home.go`; the others there only send their root to it.

## Entities

- **Category** — an ID, a title, an optional emoji, and a style. Categories display in their Order (`data.md`, Ordering).
- **Link** — an ID, title, optional description, URL, optional image, the category it belongs to, a Visible flag, and who added it when. Links display in their Order within their category. A link with no image of its own shows its category's emoji; with neither, its initial.

Hidden links (Visible = No) are kept but reach only admins, who see them greyed and badged, so a seasonal link can be parked rather than deleted.

Separately, a link into a community app narrowed by the `Visibility` tab (`docs/home/data.md`) to a list the viewer is not on is left out of their `links` altogether, admin or not, the same way the toolbar's switch leaves the app off - the server recognizes such a link by its host, matched when the sheet loads against every one of every app's `Hosts` on each domain in `model.Domains` (`appOfLink` in `internal/model/home.go`), so a link out to anything else is untouched. Every app serves the switch's list, each app with whether the viewer is shown it (`me.listed`), as `GET /api/apps` (`docs/api.md`, Heliosian), which is how the toolbar knows.

The **apps section** (Style `apps`, `docs/home/data.md`) is the community apps themselves, on the front page: the same registry the toolbar's switch lists (`model.Apps` - Helios Who?, HCA-Team, Helios Celebrate, each with its mark from `/brand/apps/` and its tagline), as feature cards that open the app on the page's own tier in the same tab, less any app the `Visibility` tab keeps from the viewer (`me.listed` on each of `/api/apps`, reckoned server-side; an admin sees every app). It holds no links, so an app added to the registry appears on the page by itself, and one narrowed away disappears along with its row in the switch. Like the events section it is filtered by the search box, capped by Max, and off the page when empty; unlike it, it is on the page only while a category carries its style, and can be deleted like any other - a category becomes it through the editor's Style menu once its links are gone.

## The page

A single page. A teal sidebar, dark at the top shading to medium at the foot and 280px wide, carries the white lockup, a link to Home and to each category (its emoji, or without one a school building, a calendar or a chat bubble by the title's wording, else a grid), then Helios When's calendar for this person, and the campsite illustration at its foot; a window too short for all of it scrolls the rail on its own, the campsite last. The calendar (`web/home/month.js`: the month's events are `events?from=&to=&calendar=` for the month under the viewer's default calendar, the first of `/api/calendar-feeds`, and its days' kinds that calendar's `days`, `docs/api.md`, Helios When; a picker at its head over the viewer's calendars re-reads the month under the one picked, until the page loads again, and each page of months is read the same way) is a small month, white on the teal, paged with arrows at its head: today ringed in amber as When rings it, the day open in the card below filled white, a weekend or a day outside the school year faded, a day that is not regular for the viewer's classrooms in its day type's colour (early-dismissal amber, no-school red, no-aftercare violet), and under each day a dot per kind of thing on it - the school's own blue, HCA's purple, Celebrate's pink, as When tints them. Under the month the day picked - Today until one is - as a card: the day and date, which open that day on When; the day types in force for the viewer's classrooms as pills, worded as When's day words are ("Early Dismissal · Hummingbirds" when only some of them); and every event on or under way that day, its title over its hours (or All day, or Through Fri while a whole-day event has days to go), each opening its page on When; with nothing on, it says so, or No school on a day the year calendar leaves out. Paging keeps the day picked at today when the month holds it, else its first. The shared toolbar (`docs/toolbar.md`) runs across the top of the content with the search box - it filters the links and the upcoming events by title as you type, and `/` jumps to it - and the signed-in user's avatar, whose menu holds the email, Admin Tools and Edit Page (the edit panel below) for admins, and Sign Out. What the page offers an admin goes by the viewer holding `home.configure` in `/api/me` (`isAdmin` in `web/home/state.js`), so an admin viewing as someone else sees the page as that person does.

The hero is a welcome - "Tools and resources for the Helios Community", with a kicker and a line under it - over the left of a landscape that runs along the hero's bottom edge and ends in a pale wave. Under the hero - lifted over the foot of its landscape on a wide page, starting just under its words - a row of widgets (`web/home/widgets.js`), each a card with one app's view of what matters now, at least 380px wide so one alone takes half a wide page, and out of the way while a search filters the page. Each widget's heading leads with the mark of the app its things come from, the same the app switch and the page's app cards show (`/brand/apps/{key}.png`). Upcoming lists its things under day bars - a pale rounded bar with the weekday and the date and, at its far end, how many things fall on it - each row a dot in its colour on a faint line running down the day, what it is, a pill, and a chevron, the whole row opening its page.

A click opens an email on its key points, starting under the subject and wrapping around the time and chip (`internal/keypoints`; "Key points on their way." until they are written) and Ask about this, which opens Helios Ask on a new chat asking what to know from it (`?q=`, which Ask asks and takes out of the address); only one is open at a time, the first when the page loads, and opening another folds away the one before. A dropdown at the heading, All until one is picked, lists each whom the two weeks' email went to as the chips say it, each with how many, and narrows the widget to it; the first of those shown opens. The three most recent show, with Show more and Show less at its foot as every widget has them. The sample server's `sampledata/artifacts` holds a week of such mail dated 2026-09-21 to 25.

Who each widget is for is an admin's to set, as a section's is, from Who sees it in the edit panel - the same rules as a section's Visibility, with the same preview of whom they pick out. To an admin every widget shows, with a Hidden badge when the admin is not among its audience. The rules are the Audience tab's rows for `widget:when`, `widget:team`, `widget:celebrate` and `widget:school` (`data.md`); none means everyone, and a widget whose rules leave the viewer out is not drawn for them. The order the widgets run in is an admin's too, for everyone, set by dragging in the edit panel (the widget's `order`, `data.md`, Ordering), kept in the Widgets tab's `Order`; each widget is a `home-widgets` resource, its `me.shown` saying whether its rules take the viewer in (`docs/api.md`, Heliosian).
Then the categories - but for the events section (`data.md`), which is not drawn, the widgets carrying what is coming up.

- **cards** — compact tinted feature cards, four to a row on a wide page and as tall as their words need: the link's picture (or the category's emoji) in a disc, the title, the description, an Open App button in the card's accent, the picture again large and faint behind the right edge when there is one, and a round chevron in the corner that opens the link too.
- **tiles** — white cards three to a row: the picture (or, without one, the category's emoji or glyph on a pale ground) beside the title and the description on one line.

A card opens its link. The foot of the page is the leaves along the bottom edge. On phones the sidebar becomes a drawer behind the toolbar's hamburger - the same column, campsite and all, slid in from the left and closed by a section link, the backdrop or Escape - and the grids go to one column.

The art lives in `web/home/brand/`: `hero-art.png` (the landscape strip, transparent above and to the left), `footer-leaves.png` (the wave and leaves), and `sidebar-camp.png` (the campsite, transparent).

## Editing

Editing is for admins, in one panel over the page, Edit the Page, opened from Edit Page in the account menu; the page itself carries no editing controls. The panel holds the whole page as a hierarchy, each section folded to its heading at first, with a chevron that opens its items and folds them away again, remembered while the page stays loaded. Every row says under its title whom it is shown to ("Shown to everyone", or its audience rules in short, and for an app the people its list names). Widgets comes first, each widget with the pencil into Who sees it. Every category but Events follows in page order, its heading row carrying a pencil into the category's editor and a delete. Beneath each heading sit its items: links with a pencil into the link's editor, a delete, and an Add Link at the end; for the apps section, the apps, each with its pencil. Add Category sits at the panel's foot. Rows are reordered by dragging their grip to a new place among their siblings: widgets among widgets, categories among categories, and items within their category; a drop sends the moved row's new `order` alone (`data.md`, Ordering). The link editor takes an image by upload or by Find an image, the same picture search HCA-Team has (`docs/team/team.md`); the category editor takes an emoji from a grid of school-flavoured picks in groups, from the whole library behind "More emoji…" (every single-character emoji with its Unicode name to search by, generated into `web/home/emoji.json`), or any pasted in. Each opens a modal with the entity's fields, an image picker, and Delete. An image is uploaded the moment it is chosen and named by its content; the save that follows records the name. A category cannot be deleted while links remain in it. Links and categories are named by ID in every edit (`docs/home/data.md`), so a renamed category keeps its links and a renamed link or category keeps who sees it. Every edit is a write on the resource API - `links`, `link-categories`, `apps` and `home-widgets` (`docs/api.md`, Heliosian) - and the page reads everything again after it.

Every change is applied to the in-memory model first, then written to the sheet cell by cell through the shared write queue, and appended to the Change Log tab. A change the sheet rules reject (a bad URL, an unknown category) is refused before anything is written.

Who may edit is the Admins tab of the Apps sheet plus the platform super admins (`docs/config.md`). Admins manage the tab from `/admin` - Admin Tools, drawn inside the front page's own document by the builder every app shares (`docs/toolbar.md`; `web/home/adminpage.js`), with the admin list. The admin list is its `admin-lists` resource (`docs/api.md`, Admin lists). A super admin gets one panel more: Feedback, every app's reports and the queue that turns one into a GitHub issue (`feedback-reports`, `docs/feedback.md`). It is the super admins' alone, since a report carries who wrote it and what page they were on, and an app admin does not see the tab.

## Sign-in

Everything behind Google sign-in restricted to the school domain, like every app here; there is no public view. The login page is Heliosian's own splash. A session is shared with the directory on the same tier, since the cookie is scoped one label up from the app's hostname.

A link to Heliosian pasted into a chat app previews the way the apps' pages do (`internal/model/home_share.go`, on the shared drawer `internal/sharecard`): the sign-in page served at any address on the portal carries Open Graph tags - "Tools and resources for the Helios Community", a line naming each app everyone has with its tagline and address, and a sign-in note - and names a card at `/open/share/apps.png`: the four-petal mark and wordmark over the registry's tagline, the headline over the yellow swoosh, the sign-in note and an Open Heliosian button; down the right, each app everyone has with its mark, its name and its tagline as the Visibility tab has them (the tags carry each app's address too, on the tier the link was to - `who.heliosian.com`, `when.heliosian.com`, a local port carried over). An app narrowed to a list, or one with no row yet, is nobody's to preview and is left off the card and the tags alike. The card is public, as every `/open/share/` path is, and its ETag hashes the apps it names, so a renamed or newly shared app reaches a chat app that cached it.

## Installable

Ships as an installable web app like the directory (`docs/who/pwa.md` describes the mechanics): its own manifest, icons, and iOS splash battery under `web/public/home/`.
