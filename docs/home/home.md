# Home app

Heliosian is the community's front door: one page of links, grouped into categories, to everything the Helios Community Association and the school run elsewhere. It serves at the apex, the first of `HomeApp`'s `Hosts` in `internal/model/home.go`; the others there only send their root to it.

## Entities

- **Category** — an ID, a title, an optional emoji, and a style. Categories display in their Order (`data.md`, Ordering).
- **Link** — an ID, title, optional description, URL, optional image, the category it belongs to, a Visible flag, and who added it when. Links display in their Order within their category. A link with no image of its own shows its category's emoji; with neither, its initial.

Hidden links (Visible = No) are kept but reach only admins, who see them greyed and badged, so a seasonal link can be parked rather than deleted.

Separately, a link into a community app narrowed by the `Visibility` tab (`docs/home/data.md`) to a list the viewer is not on is left out of their `links` altogether, admin or not, the same way the toolbar's switch leaves the app off - the server recognizes such a link by its host, matched when the sheet loads against every one of every app's `Hosts` on each domain in `model.Domains` (`appOfLink` in `internal/model/home.go`), so a link out to anything else is untouched. Every app serves the switch's list, each app with whether the viewer is shown it (`me.listed`), as `GET /api/apps` (`docs/api.md`, Heliosian), which is how the toolbar knows.

The **apps section** (Style `apps-grid` or `apps`, `docs/home/data.md`) is the community apps themselves, on the front page: the same registry the toolbar's switch lists (`model.Apps` - Helios Who?, HCA-Team, Helios Celebrate, each with its mark from `/brand/apps/` and its tagline), drawn as a widget among the others (Widgets, below): as an icon grid of the apps' marks and names, the tagline on hover, or as a row per app, its mark, name and tagline, each opening the app on the page's own tier in the same tab, less any app the `Visibility` tab keeps from the viewer (`me.listed` on each of `/api/apps`, reckoned server-side; an admin sees every app). It holds no links, so an app added to the registry appears on the page by itself, and one narrowed away disappears along with its row in the switch. While the search box holds words it is a section of feature cards instead, as every category is (below). It is on the page only while a category carries its style, and is renamed and moved but not deleted; a category becomes it by its Style cell in the sheet, once its links are gone. The category editor offers every category the same Layout choice, Icon grid or Compact list, and keeps what a category holds as it is: the apps section's editor says it lists the community apps, and every other category holds links.

Every other category but the events section is a widget too, headed by the category's title and mark, its links opening in a new tab, laid out as its Style says (`data.md`): `cards`, called Icon grid in the editor, a grid of the links' pictures - else the title's first letter on a pale tile - with the title under each; `tiles`, called Compact list, a row per link, a small picture beside the title, in as many columns as the card's width holds and every link shown. The editor's Show descriptions checkbox puts each link's description - or each app's tagline - under its title, in two lines in a grid and one in a list; checked or not, it is the title's tooltip. Each is a widget of its own in the Widgets tab, named `category:<Category ID>` (`data.md`): dragged among the other widgets in the edit panel, and seen by whoever the category's own Visibility takes in - Who sees it on the widget and the category's Visibility are the same rules. The rail holds the logo and the calendar, and above the calendar the first few items (`sidebarCount` in `web/home/widgets.js`) of each widget whose Show in sidebar is ticked - in the category's editor for a category, beside Who sees it for the others, unchecked until ticked and kept in the Widgets tab's Sidebar (`data.md`) - under a heading of the widget's name and mark, with a Hidden badge for an admin outside the widget's audience, in widget order (`renderNav` in `web/home/widgets.js`, redrawn when the page loads, a to-do is marked or Upcoming's calendar changes): a category's links or apps, each with its small picture or first letter and opening as the widget's do; Upcoming's next events, the day of the month in the box; Reminders' open to-dos, soonest due, each behind a dot and opening Ask; Team's open sign-ups; Celebrate's upcoming parties; the Inbox's newest emails, each opening Ask about it; and the viewer's own birthdays, or everyone's for a birthday admin with none of their own. With none ticked the rail has no links at all.

A to-do reaches whoever its email reaches, by the Inbox's rules for school mail and Loop's for an email list's post. Three chips, each with how many it holds: **Recent**, everything not marked done; **Saved**; **Done**. The list is a checklist, soonest due first and those whose email names no day last: each row a checkbox, the title, and the day it is due at the right, red once a saved one's day has gone. Ticking the box marks it done - the box filled, the title crossed out - and unticking takes the mark off. The five-to-ten-word summary, the details - a sentence or two - and the email the to-do came from, whom it went to and when, are the title's tooltip. A click on the title opens Helios Ask on a new chat asking about the to-do and its email (`?q=`). Done moves a to-do to the Done chip, crossed out; Save keeps it on the list after it would otherwise go. Otherwise a to-do with a due day stays until that day passes, and one without stays while its email is within `model.ToDoWindow`; a done one stays on the Done chip as long as it would have stayed on the list. The marks are each person's own, kept in the Apps sheet's `To Dos` tab (`data.md`), so one parent's Done leaves the other's list alone. The sample server's `sampledata/artifacts` carries to-dos from that mail and marks every email read under the current `digest.Revision`, so it asks its Claude intercept for none; a sample email added without a `Reads` row is read by the intercept, which fills it with placeholder words.

Who each widget is for is an admin's to set, as a section's is, from Who sees it in the edit panel - the same rules as a section's Visibility, with the same preview of whom they pick out. To an admin every widget shows, with a Hidden badge when the admin is not among its audience. A link whose rules leave the admin out is not drawn for them, in a widget, the rail or a search, though the server sends it (`forMe` false) and the edit panel lists it for editing (`linksForMe` in `web/home/cards.js`). The rules are the Audience tab's rows for `widget:when`, `widget:team`, `widget:celebrate`, `widget:school`, `widget:birthday` and `widget:todo` (`data.md`); none means everyone, and a widget whose rules leave the viewer out is not drawn for them. The order the widgets run in is an admin's too, for everyone, set by dragging in the edit panel (the widget's `order`, `data.md`, Ordering), kept in the Widgets tab's `Order`. So is how each row of widgets is split, set on the edit panel's Layout tab, beside its Widgets tab, and kept in the Layout tab of the sheet (`data.md`): each row is one of the splits `home-settings` lists in `layouts` (`model.RowLayouts`), picked from buttons drawn as the split, beside a menu for each of its places naming the widget that lands there, with an eye on any widget not shown to everyone and its tooltip saying whom it is; picking another widget in a place swaps the two in the order, and picking one for the first empty place moves it to the end, the later empty places offering nothing. The widgets fill the rows' places in their order (`widgetGrid` in `web/home/state.js`), and rows past the Layout tab's last take the first split. A widget a viewer is not shown keeps its place, and the rest of its row shares the row in their own proportions, so one person's missing widget never moves another into a different row; a row with nothing to show is left out. While the page is too narrow for three columns of about 330px each row becomes two columns - a row of one widget one, and the third of a row of three across the whole width under the other two - and on a phone one (container queries on `.page-body`, `web/home/style.css`); each widget is a `home-widgets` resource, its `me.shown` saying whether its rules take the viewer in (`docs/api.md`, Heliosian).
While the search box holds words the widgets step aside and every category but the events section is drawn as a section of everything that matches:

- **cards** — compact tinted feature cards, four to a row on a wide page and as tall as their words need: the link's picture (or the category's emoji) in a disc, the title, the description, an Open App button in the card's accent, the picture again large and faint behind the right edge when there is one, and a round chevron in the corner that opens the link too.
- **tiles** — white cards three to a row: the picture (or, without one, the category's emoji or glyph on a pale ground) beside the title and the description on one line.

A card opens its link. The foot of the page is the leaves along the bottom edge. On phones the sidebar becomes a drawer behind the toolbar's hamburger - the same column, campsite and all, slid in from the left and closed by a section link, the backdrop or Escape - and the grids go to one column.

The art lives in `web/home/brand/`: `hero-art.png` and `hero-art-dark.png` (the landscape strip for each mode, cut from `asset-sources/decorative/heliosian_top` both cropped to one box around the painting so the two modes lay out alike, drawn at the right of the hero across most of its width under 70px of the hero's own sky, its left edge faded into that sky by a mask on `.hero-art`, the welcome laid over the sky), `footer-leaves.png` (the wave and leaves), and `sidebar-camp.png` (the campsite, transparent).

## Editing

Editing is for admins, in one panel over the page, Edit the Page, opened from Edit Page in the account menu; the page itself carries no editing controls. The panel has two tabs, Widgets and Layout (Widgets, above). Widgets holds the whole page as a hierarchy, each section folded to its heading at first, with a chevron that opens its items and folds them away again, remembered while the page stays loaded. Every row says under its title whom it is shown to ("Shown to everyone", or its audience rules in short, and for an app the people its list names). Its rows are the widgets in page order, one list: an app's widget (Upcoming, Reminders, Team and the rest) a row with the pencil into Who sees it, and a category's widget a heading row with a pencil into the category's editor and a delete, beneath which sit its items: links with a pencil into the link's editor, a delete, and an Add Link at the end; for the apps section, the apps, each with its pencil. Add Category sits at the panel's foot while Widgets is open. Rows are reordered by dragging their grip to a new place among their siblings: the widgets among each other, whatever kind, which is their order on the page, and items within their category; a drop sends the moved row's new `order` alone (`data.md`, Ordering). The rail's category links, for the categories shown in the sidebar, and a search's sections follow the same order. The link editor takes an image by upload or by Find an image, the same picture search HCA-Team has (`docs/team/team.md`); the category editor takes an emoji from a grid of school-flavoured picks in groups, from the whole library behind "More emoji…" (every single-character emoji with its Unicode name to search by, generated into `web/home/emoji.json`), or any pasted in. Each opens a modal with the entity's fields, an image picker, and Delete. An image is uploaded the moment it is chosen and named by its content; the save that follows records the name. A category cannot be deleted while links remain in it. Links and categories are named by ID in every edit (`docs/home/data.md`), so a renamed category keeps its links and a renamed link or category keeps who sees it. Every edit is a write on the resource API - `links`, `link-categories`, `apps` and `home-widgets` (`docs/api.md`, Heliosian) - and the page reads everything again after it.

Every change is applied to the in-memory model first, then written to the sheet cell by cell through the shared write queue, and appended to the Change Log tab. A change the sheet rules reject (a bad URL, an unknown category) is refused before anything is written.

Who may edit is the Admins tab of the Apps sheet plus the platform super admins (`docs/config.md`). Admins manage the tab from `/admin` - Admin Tools, drawn inside the front page's own document by the builder every app shares (`docs/toolbar.md`; `web/home/adminpage.js`), with the admin list. The admin list is its `admin-lists` resource (`docs/api.md`, Admin lists). A super admin gets one panel more: Feedback, every app's reports and the queue that turns one into a GitHub issue (`feedback-reports`, `docs/feedback.md`). It is the super admins' alone, since a report carries who wrote it and what page they were on, and an app admin does not see the tab.

## Sign-in

Everything behind Google sign-in restricted to the school domain, like every app here; there is no public view. The login page is Heliosian's own splash. A session is shared with the directory on the same tier, since the cookie is scoped one label up from the app's hostname.

A link to Heliosian pasted into a chat app previews the way the apps' pages do (`internal/model/home_share.go`, on the shared drawer `internal/sharecard`): the sign-in page served at any address on the portal carries Open Graph tags - "Tools and resources for the Helios Community", a line naming each app everyone has with its tagline and address, and a sign-in note - and names a card at `/open/share/apps.png`: the four-petal mark and wordmark over the registry's tagline, the headline over the yellow swoosh, the sign-in note and an Open Heliosian button; down the right, each app everyone has with its mark, its name and its tagline as the Visibility tab has them (the tags carry each app's address too, on the tier the link was to - `who.heliosian.com`, `when.heliosian.com`, a local port carried over). An app narrowed to a list, or one with no row yet, is nobody's to preview and is left off the card and the tags alike. The card is public, as every `/open/share/` path is, and its ETag hashes the apps it names, so a renamed or newly shared app reaches a chat app that cached it.

## Installable

Ships as an installable web app like the directory (`docs/who/pwa.md` describes the mechanics): its own manifest, icons, and iOS splash battery under `web/public/home/`.
