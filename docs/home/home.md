# Home app

Heliosian is the community's front door: one page of links, grouped into sections, to everything the Helios Community Association and the school run elsewhere. It serves at the apex, the first of the `home` entry's `Hosts` in `hosts.Apps` (`internal/hosts/hosts.go`); the others there only send their root to it. Everything on it is the data model's (`docs/home/data.md`): the page reads it in one `/api/q` batch (`loadModel` in `web/home/state.js`) and writes it as `POST /api/q` batches (`web/home/writes.js`). Its server is `HomePages` in `internal/db/homepages.go`, which serves the page (`web/home/index.html`) at `/` and `/admin`, sends `/dl/` home, and draws the share card (Sign-in, below), beside the image search Heliosian's editors use (`/api/home`, registered in `internal/app/wiring.go`).

## Entities

- **Section** — a widget of links (`WIDGET` key `links`): a heading, a mark, a layout, and the group its links sit under (`data.md`). Sections display in widget order.
- **Link** — a group under a section's group: a title, optional description, URL, optional picture, and who sees it. Links display in their order within their section. A link with no picture of its own shows its section's mark.

Hidden links (Visible unticked, `visible_to` blank) are kept but reach only admins, who see them greyed and badged, so a seasonal link can be parked rather than deleted.

The **apps section** (`WIDGET` key `apps`, `data.md`) is the community apps themselves, on the front page: the `APP` rows the toolbar's switch lists (every app but Heliosian, each with its mark from `/brand/apps/` and its subtitle), drawn as a widget among the others (Widgets, below): as an icon grid of the apps' marks and names, the subtitle on hover, or as a row per app, its mark, name and subtitle, each opening the app on the page's own tier in the same tab, less any app whose `visible_to` group the viewer is not in (an admin sees every app). It holds no links, so an app given an `APP` row appears on the page by itself, and one narrowed away disappears along with its row in the switch. While the search box holds words it is a section of feature cards instead, as every section is (below). It is renamed and moved but never added or deleted. The section editor offers every section the same Layout choice, Icon grid or Compact list, and keeps what a section holds as it is: the apps section's editor says it lists the community apps, and every other section holds links.

Every section of links is a widget too, headed by the section's heading and mark, its links opening in a new tab, laid out as its style says (`data.md`): `grid`, called Icon grid in the editor, a grid of the links' pictures - else the section's mark on a pale tile - with the title under each; `list`, called Compact list, a row per link, a small picture beside the title, in as many columns as the card's width holds and every link shown. The editor's Show descriptions checkbox puts each link's description - or each app's subtitle - under its title, in two lines in a grid and one in a list; checked or not, it is the title's tooltip. Each is dragged among the other widgets in the edit panel, and seen by whoever its Who sees it takes in (`data.md`, Who sees what). The rail holds the logo and the calendar, and above the calendar the first few items (`sidebarCount` in `web/home/widgets.js`) of each widget whose Show in sidebar is ticked - in the section's editor for a section, beside Who sees it for the others, unchecked until ticked and kept in the widget's `sidebar` (`data.md`) - under a heading of the widget's name and mark, with a Hidden badge for an admin outside the widget's audience, in widget order (`renderNav` in `web/home/widgets.js`, redrawn when the page loads, a to-do is marked or Upcoming's calendar changes): a section's links or apps, each with its small picture or mark and opening as the widget's do; Upcoming's next events, the day of the month in the box; Reminders' open to-dos, soonest due, each behind a dot and opening Ask; Team's open sign-ups; Celebrate's upcoming parties; the Inbox's newest emails, each opening Ask about it; and the viewer's own birthdays, or everyone's for a birthday admin with none of their own. With none ticked the rail has no links at all.

## The page

A single page. A teal sidebar, dark at the top shading to medium at the foot and 280px wide, carries the white lockup, a link to Home and to each section (its mark), then Helios When's calendar for this person, and the campsite illustration at its foot; a window too short for all of it scrolls the rail on its own, the campsite last. The calendar (`web/home/month.js`) is a small month, white on the teal, paged with arrows at its head, drawn from the calendar the viewer has picked: their calendars are their default saved search - or, with none, My Heliosian's built-in words (`builtinWords` in `web/common/calendar.js`, the classrooms of their household and When's default categories) - and then their other saved searches (`calendarsOf` in `web/home/state.js`); each calendar's events are the events, parties and activities its generated `condition` matches (`matchingQuery`, asked of `/api/q` for every calendar as the page loads; `docs/datamodel.md`, Saved searches), and its days' kinds come from the school days over the classrooms it is for (`roomsOn`). The calendar reading is `web/common/calendar.js`, which Helios When's page shares (`calendarQueries`, `buildCalendar`). A picker at the month's head over the viewer's calendars redraws the month under the one picked, until the page loads again. In the month, today is ringed in amber as When rings it, the day open in the card below filled white, a weekend or a day outside the school year faded, a day that is not regular for the calendar's classrooms in its day type's colour (early-dismissal amber, no-school red, no-aftercare violet), and under each day a dot per kind of thing on it - the school's own blue, HCA's purple, Celebrate's pink, as When tints them. Under the month the day picked - Today until one is - as a card: the day and date, which open that day on When; the day types in force for the calendar's classrooms as pills, worded as When's day words are ("Early Dismissal · Hummingbirds" when only some of them); and every event on or under way that day, its title over its hours (or All day, or Through Fri while a whole-day event has days to go), each opening its page on When; with nothing on, it says so, or No school on a day the year calendar leaves out. Paging keeps the day picked at today when the month holds it, else its first. The shared toolbar (`docs/toolbar.md`) runs across the top of the content with the search box - it filters the links and the upcoming events by title as you type, and `/` jumps to it - and the signed-in user's avatar, whose menu holds the email, Admin Tools and Edit Page (the edit panel below) for admins, and Sign Out. What the page offers an admin goes by the viewer's `home` `APP` row answering `(admin_of "home")` (`isAdmin` in `web/home/state.js`), so an admin viewing as someone else sees the page as that person does.

The hero is a welcome - "Tools and resources for the Helios Community", with a kicker and a line under it - in the open sky at the upper left of a landscape that runs the hero's whole width, its grass meeting the tops of the widgets; on a phone the words sit above it. At the hero's top right a dark glass card gives the next school day's hours - today until its last block ends, then the next school day after it, headed Today, Tomorrow or its weekday (`nextSchoolDay` in `web/home/month.js`): titled Early Dismissal when that day is one of those for the rail's calendar - the classrooms it holds for under the title when only some - and Regular Dismissal otherwise, then Dropoff, School, Pickup and Aftercare, each with its icon and its times - a block the day type leaves blank, Aftercare on a No Aftercare day, left out - from that day type's blocks (a school day of that type's `day_part` groups, `dayTypes` in `buildCalendar`; `docs/datamodel.md`, Calendar import); a day the calendar marks some other kind, No Aftercare say, takes that kind's times. It opens the day in When, and is gone when no school day lies ahead in the calendar (`schedule` in `web/home/month.js`). On a phone it sits under the words. Under the hero - lifted over the foot of its landscape on a wide page, starting just under its words - a row of widgets (`web/home/widgets.js`), each a card with one app's view of what matters now, laid out in rows an admin splits (below), with room around each, and out of the way while a search filters the page. Each widget's heading leads with the mark of the app its things come from, the same the app switch and the page's app cards show (`/brand/apps/{key}.png`). Upcoming lists its things under day bars - a pale rounded bar with the weekday and the date and, at its far end, how many things fall on it - each row a dot in its colour on a faint line running down the day, what it is, a pill, and a chevron, the whole row opening its page.

The fourth, **Inbox**, under Helios Ask's mark: the school's email of the last fourteen days, as far back as the digest reads (`MailWindow` in `internal/db/digest.go`) - every root `mail` document (`DOCUMENT` kind `mail` with no parent) published in that time that the viewer may read (`document_visible`: it was sent to a group whose mail they see, `docs/datamodel.md`, Documents), less Loop's posts (the documents naming a `MESSAGE`). So it holds the lists to every family and the school's chat, the viewer's own classrooms' parent and student lists and the gradebands that hold them, and the mail the school sends through Veracross once the classifier has filed it under the groups it was written to - in Upcoming's rows under its day bars, newest day first and each day's newest first, the bar saying how many, and how many of the viewer's to-dos they hold when any do ("2 emails · 1 reminder"), an email holding the to-dos whose checkboxes its key points carry, each once, each row laid out as Upcoming's but with no dot or line of its own: its subject at the row's left edge, followed in grey by how many of the viewer's to-dos its key points carry when they carry any ("2 reminders"), the same checkboxes the email shows when open, and under it while the email is folded its one-sentence summary in grey (`DOCUMENT.summary`, the digest's, `docs/datamodel.md`, Digest), cut short with an ellipsis and whole on hover, the time it was sent at the right, and under the time a chip saying whom it went to: the names of the groups it was `sent_to`, in order, joined with " · ", cut short with an ellipsis when long, the whole of it on hover. Its chips each carry how many they hold, and a chip holding nobody is left off, so it starts on Mine when that holds anyone and on All otherwise. **Mine** is the birthdays assigned to the viewer in the next two newsletters still waiting on their part - asking, or recording the charity - soonest first: the staff member's photo, else their initial, beside their name over the step and its day ("Ask by Thu, Sep 24", "Charity due Tue, Oct 6"). **All** is a Birthdays admin's alone: everyone the next two newsletters carry, under a day bar for each issue saying how many, each with who has them, or Unassigned. Every row names the charity - the one recorded for the year, else the charity the weekly copy would use after "Default:" - and under it the stage as a chip in the colour the app gives it ("Ready to Contact"), beside a red Late or amber Due today pill when the step is late or due today. A row opens the staff member's page on Staff Birthdays, and See all goes across to the app.

**Reminders**, the widget `todo`, under its own mark (`web/home/brand/reminders.png`, cut from `asset-sources/reminders/icon.png` as `docs/brand.md` cuts a mark): what the school's email and the viewer's Loop email lists ask them to do - a form to return, a fee to pay, something to bring, a sign-up - the `TODO` rows the digest reads out of each email (`docs/datamodel.md`, Digest). A to-do reaches whoever may read its email (`document_visible`), school mail and a list's post alike. Three chips, each with how many it holds: **Recent**, everything not marked done; **Saved**; **Done**. The list is a checklist, soonest due first and those whose email names no day last: each row a checkbox, the title, and the day it is due at the right, red once a saved one's day has gone. Ticking the box marks it done - the box filled, the title crossed out - and unticking takes the mark off. A star saves it, filled gold once saved and a click again taking the mark off, and an open-link icon follows the title when the email gives a link; both show while the row is hovered, and always on a saved one's star and on a touch screen. The five-to-ten-word summary, the details - a sentence or two - and the email the to-do came from, whom it went to and when, are the title's tooltip. A click on the title opens Helios Ask on a new chat asking about the to-do and its email (`?q=`). Done moves a to-do to the Done chip, crossed out; Save keeps it on the list after it would otherwise go. Otherwise a to-do with a due day stays until that day passes, and one without stays while its email is within the fourteen days; a done one stays on the Done chip as long as it would have stayed on the list (`mailModel` in `web/home/state.js`). The marks are each person's own `TODO_MARK` rows (`data.md`), so one parent's Done leaves the other's list alone. The sample's to-dos are `sampledata/datadocuments/TODO.csv`, and its marks `sampledata/datapeople/TODO_MARK.csv`; the digest runs on Cloud Run alone, so a local server asks Claude for none.

Who each widget is for is an admin's to set, as a section's is, from Who sees it in the edit panel - the same rules as a section's, with the same preview of whom they pick out (`data.md`, Who sees what). To an admin every widget shows, with a Hidden badge when the admin is not among its audience; anyone else is never sent a widget whose group leaves them out. A link whose group leaves the admin out is not drawn for them, in a widget, the rail or a search, though they read it and the edit panel lists it for editing (`linksForMe` in `web/home/cards.js`). The order the widgets run in is an admin's too, for everyone, set by dragging in the edit panel (the widget's `order`, `data.md`, Ordering). So is how each row of widgets is split, set on the edit panel's Layout tab, beside its Widgets tab, and kept in the `home` `layout` setting (`data.md`): each row is one of the splits in `layouts` (`web/home/state.js`), picked from buttons drawn as the split, beside a menu for each of its places naming the widget that lands there, with an eye on any widget not shown to everyone and its tooltip saying whom it is; picking another widget in a place swaps the two in the order, and picking one for the first empty place moves it to the end, the later empty places offering nothing. The widgets fill the rows' places in their order (`widgetGrid` in `web/home/state.js`), and rows past the setting's last take the first split. A widget a viewer is not shown keeps its place, and the rest of its row shares the row in their own proportions, so one person's missing widget never moves another into a different row; a row with nothing to show is left out. While the page is too narrow for three columns of about 330px each row becomes two columns - a row of one widget one, and the third of a row of three across the whole width under the other two - and on a phone one (container queries on `.page-body`, `web/home/style.css`).
While the search box holds words the widgets step aside and every section is drawn as a section of everything that matches:

- **grid** — compact tinted feature cards, four to a row on a wide page and as tall as their words need: the link's picture (or the section's mark) in a disc, the title, the description, an Open App button in the card's accent, the picture again large and faint behind the right edge when there is one, and a round chevron in the corner that opens the link too.
- **list** — white cards three to a row: the picture (or, without one, the section's mark on a pale ground) beside the title and the description on one line.

A card opens its link. The foot of the page is the leaves along the bottom edge. On phones the sidebar becomes a drawer behind the toolbar's hamburger - the same column, campsite and all, slid in from the left and closed by a section link, the backdrop or Escape - and the grids go to one column.

The art lives in `web/home/brand/`: `hero-art.png` and `hero-art-dark.png` (the landscape strip for each mode, cut from `asset-sources/decorative/heliosian_top` both cropped to one box around the painting so the two modes lay out alike, drawn at the right of the hero across most of its width under 70px of the hero's own sky, its left edge faded into that sky by a mask on `.hero-art`, the welcome laid over the sky), `footer-leaves.png` (the wave and leaves), and `sidebar-camp.png` (the campsite, transparent).

## Editing

Editing is for admins, in one panel over the page, Edit the Page, opened from Edit Page in the account menu; the page itself carries no editing controls. The panel has two tabs, Widgets and Layout (Widgets, above). Widgets holds the whole page as a hierarchy, each section folded to its heading at first, with a chevron that opens its items and folds them away again, remembered while the page stays loaded. Every row says under its title whom it is shown to ("Shown to everyone", Hidden, or the group's name with how many rules and people it holds; `shownTo` in `web/home/cards.js`). Its rows are the widgets in page order, one list: an app's widget (Upcoming, Reminders, Team and the rest) a row with the pencil into Who sees it, and a section's widget a heading row with a pencil into the section's editor and a delete, beneath which sit its items: links with a pencil into the link's editor, a delete, and an Add Link at the end; for the apps section, the apps, each with its pencil into the app's editor - its mark and address at the top, so the window says which app it is whatever it is called, then its name and subtitle, and Everyone or Only some people, the latter with the rules and the people named, picked from the directory. Add Category sits at the panel's foot while Widgets is open. Rows are reordered by dragging their grip to a new place among their siblings: the widgets among each other, whatever kind, which is their order on the page, and items within their section - the apps' order being the switch's too; a drop sets the moved row's `order` alone (`data.md`, Ordering). The rail's section links, for the sections shown in the sidebar, and a search's sections follow the same order. The link editor takes an image by upload or by Find an image, the same picture search HCA-Team has (`docs/team/team.md`); the section editor takes a mark from a grid of outline marks (`categoryIcons` in `web/home/dom.js`) or a picture of its own. Each opens a modal with the entity's fields, a Visibility tab beside its Details for who sees it, an image picker, and Delete. A section cannot be deleted while links remain in it. Sections, links and apps are named by ID in every edit, so a renamed section keeps its links and a renamed link or section keeps who sees it. Every edit is a `POST /api/q` batch (`web/home/writes.js`; `data.md`), checked against the cells and the policies and committed whole or not at all (`docs/datamodel.md`, The query API), and the page reads everything again after it.

Who may edit is Heliosian's admins: the super admins and the effective members of the `home` `APP` row's admins group (`data.md`, Who may edit). Admins manage that group from `/admin` - Admin Tools, drawn inside the front page's own document by the builder every app shares (`docs/toolbar.md`; `web/home/adminpage.js`), with the admin list: the shared admins card (`adminsCard` in `web/common/admin.js`) over the group's `MEMBER` rows (`groupMembers`). A super admin gets one panel more: Feedback, every app's reports and the queue that turns one into a GitHub issue (`docs/feedback.md`). It is the super admins' alone, since a report carries who wrote it and what page they were on, and an app admin does not see the tab.

## Sign-in

Everything behind Google sign-in restricted to the school domain, like every app here; there is no public view. The login page is Heliosian's own splash. A session is shared with the directory on the same tier, since the cookie is scoped one label up from the app's hostname.

A link to Heliosian pasted into a chat app previews the way the apps' pages do (`HomePages` in `internal/db/homepages.go`, on the shared drawer `internal/sharecard`): the sign-in page served at any address on the portal carries Open Graph tags - "Tools and resources for the Helios Community", a line naming each app everyone has with its subtitle and address, and a sign-in note - and names a card at `/open/share/apps.png`: the four-petal mark and wordmark over Heliosian's own subtitle (the `home` `APP` row's), the headline over the yellow swoosh, the sign-in note and an Open Heliosian button; down the right, each app everyone has with its mark, its name and its subtitle as its `APP` row has them, in their order (the tags carry each app's address too, on the tier the link was to - `who.heliosian.com`, `when.heliosian.com`, a local port carried over). An app whose `visible_to` is anything but the Everyone group is nobody's to preview and is left off the card and the tags alike. The card is public, as every `/open/share/` path is, and its ETag hashes the keys, names and subtitles of the apps it names, so a renamed or newly shared app reaches a chat app that cached it.

## Installable

Ships as an installable web app like the directory (`docs/who/pwa.md` describes the mechanics): its own manifest, icons, and iOS splash battery under `web/public/home/`.
