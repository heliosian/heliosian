# Home app

Heliosian is the community's front door: one page of links, grouped into categories, to everything the Helios Community Association and the school run elsewhere. It serves at heliosian.com, www.heliosian.com, and home.heliosian.com.

## Entities

- **Category** — a title, an optional emoji, and a style. Categories display in the order their rows sit in the sheet, which is also how they are reordered.
- **Link** — title, optional description, URL, optional image, the category it belongs to, a Visible flag, and who added it when. Links display in sheet row order within their category. A link with no image of its own shows its category's emoji; with neither, its initial.

Hidden links (Visible = No) are kept but reach only admins, who see them greyed and badged while **Super Admin Mode** - the pencil in the toolbar - is on (off by default, remembered per browser; the same switch brings out the add cards), so a seasonal link can be parked rather than deleted and an admin still sees the page as everyone else does.

Separately, a link into a community app narrowed by the `Visibility` tab (`docs/home/data.md`) to a list the viewer is not on is left out of their model altogether, admin or not, the same way the toolbar's switch leaves the app off - the server recognizes such a link by its host on the page's own tier (`hiddenHosts` in `internal/home/home.go`: from `heliosian.com` the directory's links are `who.heliosian.com`'s, from `heliosiandev.com` they are `who.heliosiandev.com`'s, and `hca.` counts as `team`'s), so a link out to anything else is untouched. Every app serves the switch's list and the person's own list of what to leave off at `GET /api/apps/switch` (`home.RegisterSwitch`, on every mux), which is how the toolbar knows.

The **apps section** (Style `apps`, `docs/home/data.md`) is the community apps themselves, on the front page: the same registry the toolbar's switch lists (`internal/home.Apps` - Helios Who?, HCA-Team, Helios Celebrate, each with its mark from `/brand/apps/` and its tagline), as feature cards that open the app on the page's own tier in the same tab, less any app the `Visibility` tab keeps from the viewer (`Model.Apps`, reckoned server-side). It holds no links, so an app added to the registry appears on the page by itself, and one narrowed away disappears along with its row in the switch. Like the events section it is filtered by the search box, capped by Max, and off the page when empty; unlike it, it is on the page only while a category carries its style, and can be deleted like any other - a category becomes it through the editor's Style menu once its links are gone.

## The page

A single page. A teal sidebar, dark at the top shading to medium at the foot and 280px wide, carries the white lockup, a link to Home and to each category (its emoji, or without one a school building, a calendar or a chat bubble by the title's wording, else a grid), then Helios When's calendar for this person, and the campsite illustration at its foot; a window too short for all of it scrolls the rail on its own, the campsite last. The calendar (`web/home/month.js`, drawn from `calendar` in the model - `Model.Month` in `internal/calendar/month.go`, read for the signed-in viewer under the filter When first shows them, the same one the events section is read under, and paged through `GET /api/apps/calendar?month=YYYY-MM`) is a small month, white on the teal, paged with arrows at its head: today ringed in amber as When rings it, the day open in the card below filled white, a weekend or a day outside the school year faded, a day that is not regular for the viewer's classrooms in its day type's colour (early-dismissal amber, no-school red, no-aftercare violet), and under each day a dot per kind of thing on it - the school's own blue, HCA's purple, Celebrate's pink, as When tints them. Under the month the day picked - Today until one is - as a card: the day and date, which open that day on When; the day types in force for the viewer's classrooms as pills, worded as When's day words are ("Early Dismissal · Hummingbirds" when only some of them); and every event on or under way that day, its title over its hours (or All day, or Through Fri while a whole-day event has days to go), each opening its page on When; with nothing on, it says so, or No school on a day the year calendar leaves out. Paging keeps the day picked at today when the month holds it, else its first. The shared toolbar (`docs/toolbar.md`) runs across the top of the content with the search box - it filters the links and the upcoming events by title as you type, and `/` jumps to it - and the signed-in user's avatar, whose menu holds the email, Admin Tools for admins, Edit Categories for an admin in Super Admin Mode, and Sign Out; for admins, Super Admin Mode's pencil sits before it. The switch is kept per browser, so the page counts it only while the model says the viewer is an admin (`superOn` in `web/home/state.js`): someone who has lost admin, an admin viewing as someone else, or the next person on a shared device sees the page as anyone does, whatever the browser remembers.

The hero is a welcome - "Tools and resources for the Helios Community", with a kicker and a line under it - over the left of a landscape that runs along the hero's bottom edge and ends in a pale wave. Then the categories, one of which is the events section - Helios When's next six events for this person, as the calendar page first shows them to someone who has not touched its filters: from today on, for their own classrooms (their children's, their own as a teacher, every classroom for someone with none), under the categories the Tags tab marks on by default, with HCA-Team's events and Celebrate's parties folded in the way the calendar lists them (`Model.Upcoming` in `internal/calendar/upcoming.go`, read for the signed-in viewer and handed to the page in its model; `docs/calendar/calendar.md`). An event arrives as the calendar's own card shape, `calendar.Card`, and the rail's month as `calendar.Month`'s own days and cards: Heliosian keeps no copy of either, so what the calendar adds to a card reaches this page without a second struct learning about it, and the words on the way in - **Join**, **Get tickets**, You're going - are written once, in the calendar, rather than by each page that says them. The section sits among the others and is renamed, re-marked and moved like them (`docs/home/data.md`), though it takes no links and cannot be deleted. Its cards each carry the picture the event's page on When wears (its own, its first category's, or the calendar's header, fetched from whichever app serves it), a month-and-day stamp, the title, the day and time beside a calendar, and one row of buttons - a calendar-with-plus icon that adds it to Google Calendar (an entry built from the sheet's start, end, location and description: all-day for a bare day, two hours long for a timed start with no end, in the school's time zone), and for an event another app runs, the way in there, saying what When's own pill says - **Join** for an HCA event, **Get tickets** or **Join the waitlist** for a party, or where the household already stands (You're going, Signed up, Waitlisted) with a check, teal while it is open or the household is in and plain once it is full or sold out - leading to the event's page on that app; a school event has the one button. The card itself opens the event's page on When, and "See all in Helios When" beside the heading leads to the calendar. With nothing ahead the section stays off the page (it says so in Super Admin Mode). A section with nothing to show stays off the page - a category whose links are all hidden, or the events section with nothing ahead - except in Super Admin Mode, where it appears empty so it can be filled or edited. A section whose Max is set shows that many and a "See more (n)" button for the rest. The link categories, each headed by its title in the Montserrat-over-swoosh treatment Who? and HCA-Team use, come in two styles:

- **cards** — tinted feature cards three to a row: the link's picture (or the category's emoji) in a disc, the title, the description, an Open App button in the card's accent, the picture again large and faint behind the right edge when there is one, and a round chevron in the corner that opens the link too.
- **tiles** — white cards three to a row: the picture (or, without one, the category's emoji or glyph on a pale ground) beside the title and the description on one line.

A card opens its link; in Super Admin Mode it wears a pencil in its corner, the way into its editor. The foot of the page is the leaves along the bottom edge. On phones the sidebar becomes a drawer behind the toolbar's hamburger - the same column, campsite and all, slid in from the left and closed by a section link, the backdrop or Escape - and the grids go to one column.

The art lives in `web/home/brand/`: `hero-art.png` (the landscape strip, transparent above and to the left), `footer-leaves.png` (the wave and leaves), and `sidebar-camp.png` (the campsite, transparent).

## Editing

Editing behaves as the directory's does: in place, for admins, with no separate editing pages. With Super Admin Mode on, admins see a pencil beside every category title and on every card, and a dashed add card at the end of every category ("Add Event" under Events, "Add Chat" under Chats, "Add Link" where the title does not read that way) that opens the link editor with the category chosen. The link editor takes an image by upload or by Find an image, the same picture search HCA-Team has (`docs/team/team.md`); the category editor takes an emoji from a grid of school-flavoured picks in groups, from the whole library behind "More emoji…" (every single-character emoji with its Unicode name to search by, generated into `web/home/emoji.json`), or any pasted in. Each opens a modal with the entity's fields, an image picker, and Delete. An image is uploaded the moment it is chosen and named by its content; the save that follows records the name. A category cannot be deleted while links remain in it. Renaming a category carries its links along.

Every change is applied to the in-memory model first, then written to the sheet cell by cell through the shared write queue, and appended to the Change Log tab. A change the sheet rules reject (a duplicate title, a bad URL, an unknown category) is refused before anything is written.

Who may edit is the Admins tab of the Apps sheet plus the platform super admins (`docs/config.md`). Admins manage the tab from `/admin` - Admin Tools in the shared admin chrome (`docs/toolbar.md`), with a Categories overview and the admin list. A super admin gets one panel more: Feedback, every app's reports and the queue that turns one into a GitHub issue (`docs/feedback.md`). It is the super admins' alone, since a report carries who wrote it and what page they were on, and an app admin does not see the tab.

## Sign-in

Everything behind Google sign-in restricted to the school domain, like every app here; there is no public view. The login page is Heliosian's own splash. A session is shared with the directory on the same tier, since the cookie is scoped one label up from the app's hostname.

A link to Heliosian pasted into a chat app previews the way the apps' pages do (`internal/home/share.go`, on the shared drawer `internal/sharecard`): the sign-in page served at any address on the portal carries Open Graph tags - "Tools and resources for the Helios Community", a line naming each app everyone has with its tagline and address, and a sign-in note - and names a card at `/open/share/apps.png`: the four-petal mark and wordmark over the registry's tagline, the headline over the yellow swoosh, the sign-in note and an Open Heliosian button; down the right, each app everyone has with its mark, its name and its tagline as the Visibility tab has them (the tags carry each app's address too, on the tier the link was to - `who.heliosian.com`, `when.heliosian.com`, a local port carried over). An app narrowed to a list, or one with no row yet, is nobody's to preview and is left off the card and the tags alike. The card is public, as every `/open/share/` path is, and its ETag hashes the apps it names, so a renamed or newly shared app reaches a chat app that cached it.

## Installable

Ships as an installable web app like the directory (`docs/who/pwa.md` describes the mechanics): its own manifest, icons, and iOS splash battery under `web/public/home/`.
