# Data model

The tabs, columns, and validation rules are in `internal/home`; this file carries only what reading that code cannot tell you.

Home's data lives in one Google Sheet, `Apps`, in the community shared drive, reached through drive membership like the directory's sheets. `APPS_SHEET` names it, and `cmd/createtabs` reads that variable to lay it out from an empty spreadsheet titled `Apps`, alongside the directory and config sheets it lays out in the same run.

## Tabs

- `Categories` — Title, Emoji, Style, Max. Row order is display order.
- `Links` — Title, Description, URL, Image, Category, Visible, Added By, Added. Row order is display order within a category.
- `Admins` — Email. Who may edit, beyond the platform super admins (`docs/config.md`).
- `Visibility` — App, Visibility, Emails, Tagline, Name, Order. One row per community app in the registry (`internal/home.Apps`): whether everyone sees it or only its list - the people in its Emails cell and whoever its rules on `Audience` pick out - in the app switch of every app's toolbar, and on the front page's apps section - the name it goes by there and the line under it, and its place in the order. The server adds the row for an app that has none.
- `Audience` — Thing, Kind, Roles, Search, Classrooms, Grades, Tags, Family. The rules that keep an app, a category or a link to some people (Rules, below): Thing says which - `app:<key>`, `category:<title>`, `link:<title>` - and the rest are one filter rule, the columns Helios Loop's `Rules` tab has after its Group. A thing with no rows has none. `cmd/createtabs` makes the tab; the load needs it to exist.
- `Change Log` — the store's, one row per changed cell holding what it held before (`docs/storage.md`). Never read back.

## Rules

**Title is the key.** Links and categories are edited by title, so a title must be unique within its tab. An edit that renames carries the new title in the same write, so the row keeps its place; a category rename rewrites the Category cell of every link in it, and a rename or delete of either carries its `Audience` rows along - cascades on the store (`carryCategory` and `carryLink` in `internal/home/cache.go`).

**Visible is Yes or No**, spelled exactly so. Any other value refuses the load.

**Style is `cards`, `tiles`, `events` or `apps`**, spelled exactly so, and every category needs one. `cards` renders the category as large feature cards, each with its image, description, and its own button; `tiles` renders it as a row of compact tiles with a picture, the title and a line; `apps` is the community apps section, described with `events` below. A blank or misspelled Style refuses the load rather than guessing a presentation, the same stance Visible takes — the front page's shape is a property of the sheet, not of what the renderer happens to fall back to.

**`events` is the one section that holds no links**: Helios When's upcoming events for the viewer, which the calendar supplies (`home.md`). The Style value is what marks the row as the app's own - no other column says so, and the row has no id: the sheet is keyed by Title throughout, and at most one row may carry this style, so it is found by that. `cmd/createtabs` seeds the row ("Upcoming Events" 📅) when it makes the tab, and the sample data carries it, so normally the sheet holds it like any other category and it is renamed, re-marked and moved like one. A sheet without the row (one laid out before the section existed) still gets the section, synthesized at the top under that standing name; the app writes the row the first time an admin renames it, changes its emoji or moves it (`saveCategory` and `reorderCategories` in `internal/home/home.go` append it, then order it where the page had it). No link may name it as its category, and it cannot be deleted - only renamed and moved.

**`apps` is the other section that holds no links**: the community apps themselves - the toolbar's switch as feature cards, each with its mark and tagline, less any app the Visibility tab keeps from the viewer (`docs/home/home.md`). At most one category carries the style, no link may name it, and a category becomes it (through the editor's Style menu, or the sheet) only once its links are gone. Unlike the events section it is not synthesized: with no row, the apps are in the toolbar alone; and it can be deleted like any other category.

**Max caps what a section shows.** Blank shows everything; a whole number of one or more shows that many links (or, for the events section, events) and puts the rest behind a See More button, which opens the section for the rest of the visit. A search shows every match regardless. Anything else in the cell refuses the load. The category editor's "Show at most" field sets it.

**Visibility is `everyone` or `list`**, spelled so, and App is one of the registry's keys (`internal/home.Apps`, the keys `web/common/toolbar.js` lists the switch by; Heliosian itself is not among them, being the front page and the way home); a second row for one app, or any other value in the Visibility column, refuses the load - a misspelled mode would narrow nothing, silently. A row whose App the running build does not know is logged and skipped rather than refused: a newer build writes the row for an app it carries the moment it starts, and the older build still serving beside it during a deploy must not die over an app it has never heard of. Emails is the list, separated by commas, semicolons, spaces or line breaks, forgiven case and duplicates, read only while Visibility is `list` but kept either way, so the list drawn up for an app is still there after it has been everyone's for a while; the app's rules on `Audience` are the list's other half, whose people are on it beside those named. `list` with an empty cell and no rules shows the app to nobody. Name is what the switch and the front page call the app, and Tagline the line under it; blank, the registry's own stands in for either. Order is the app's place in the switch and on the front page, counted from one; rows with a number come first, by it, then the rest in the registry's order, so a tab with no numbers lists the apps as the code does. A blank Name changes nothing in the app itself: each app's own tab title, drawer and login page carry the name its code gives it. This is visibility, never access: a direct link, a bookmark or a shared event page still opens the app to anyone signed in.

**A new app starts out of sight.** The registry is the code's: an app is added there when it is built, and the first server to start with it finds the tab has no row for it and writes one (`discoverApps` in `internal/home/home.go`) - `list`, nobody on it, the registry's tagline - so a new app shows to nobody until an admin lets people in or switches it to everyone. Until that write lands, and for any row deleted by hand, the page reads a missing row the same way. The front page edits the tab in Super Admin Mode, on each app's card in the apps section: a pencil opens the app's editor - its mark and address at the top, which are the code's and not for editing, so the window says which app it is whatever it is called; then the name, the tagline, Everyone or Only some people, and under the latter the rules and the people named, picked from the directory - saving through `/api/admin/visibility`; the two arrows beside the pencil move the app in the switch's order, every row's Order rewritten at once (`/api/admin/visibility/order`). A long list can be pasted into the Emails cell by hand, live within the five-minute reload. The tagline reaches HCA-Team's social share cards too: the card drawer asks the registry for it at each draw (`ShareTagline`, wired in `internal/app`), and the card's ETag carries it, so a chat app that cached a card fetches the new one. Helios Celebrate's card wears its logo as drawn (`web/public/celebrate/brand/logo-lockup.png`, from the designer's `Celebrate Logo.png`), tagline and all, so the registry's does not reach it.

**An audience is a list of filter rules**, the same rules a Helios Loop group is made of, read by the same code (`internal/filter`, which Loop's `eval.go` reads its groups through) against the same sources - the directory, and the tags the rules name, read for the admins (`docs/loop/loop.md`, A rule discloses the tags it names). A link, a whole category and an app's list each have their rows on `Audience`: include rules, whose matches are unioned, and exclude rules, whose matches are taken away; within a rule every choice must hold (`Parent` with `Jays` is the parents of Jays students; a student by their own classroom, a parent by their children's, staff by none, a teacher not being in the room they teach - `who.Model.Facets`); Add family widens a rule's matches by the relatives asked for and the roles then keep their kind, as Loop reads them (`docs/loop/loop.md`, Rule). A rule that says nothing, names a role or relation the filter has no such thing as, or names a tag any way but by its owner's address and name refuses the load, as Loop's would; a classroom or grade the directory has since renamed matches nobody. No rows at all keeps nothing from anyone - or, on an app's list, adds nobody to the people named. A rule's tags are the saving admin's own, or shared with them, to name (`filter.Writable`, the admins as the editors), and read while one of the admins may read them; a rule another admin wrote naming a tag of theirs is read out in the editor and can be removed but not changed, as in Loop. A category kept to some people goes to them whole, links and all, and to nobody else - a section students may not see is one row's Roles cell, `Parents, Staff` - and a link inside it may narrow further but not widen. The link, category and app editors' **Visibility** - the link's and the app's a tab of its own, beside Details - is the rule editor Loop's group editor is (`web/common/rules.js`, with `rules.css`): each rule a band reading out its sentence - a Magic Tag's name in it a link to the thing itself, the group in Loop, the activity in HCA-Team or the party in Celebrate, so it is a click from being changed - opened to its controls - role chips, a search box, Classroom, Grade, Tags and Add family dropdowns, the choices from `/api/apps/audience/options` (`filter.OptionsFor`, the viewer's own tags, the tags shared with them and their Magic Tags among them, each by the key a rule holds) - with Add include rule and Add exclude rule, how many people each rule touches and who they pick out together (`/api/apps/audience/preview`, sent the thing's Audience key with the rules so a rule already on it passes as a save would) asked of the server as they change; the app's editor is the one above. A save replaces a thing's rows whole, refuses a grade or classroom the directory does not have, and a rename or delete of a link or category carries its rows along. An admin is sent every link, every category and every app, the ones not theirs marked `forMe: false` (an app's carrying its row too, for the editor), and the page shows those - and the hidden links - only in Super Admin Mode, with a badge after the title saying who it is kept to; what an admin looks at by default is what they would see as anyone else. This is visibility, never access: the link's address is whatever it is.

**A category must exist before a link names it**, and cannot be deleted while a link still does.

**A category goes by an outline mark, not a picture.** Its Emoji cell (the column keeps its old name) is blank or `icon:<name>` - one of the marks the category editor offers (`categoryIcons` in `web/home/dom.js`: heliosian and when - the apps' own symbol_white exports, masked - pin, link, calendar, chat, school, volunteer, family, star, heart, book, music, ball, ticket, gift, camera, sun, map, bell, cart, bulb, megaphone, hand, home, section), drawn in the rail's text colour so it reads on any rail. Blank, or an emoji left from before the marks (still accepted, no longer shown), gets a mark read off the title: school, calendar, chat, or a grid. The editor stores the choice; the sheet can carry it by hand. A link without a picture of its own shows its category's mark.

**Link images are named, and the name must resolve.** An Image cell is either an object under `link-images/` in the media bucket, content-addressed like photos and written by the image picker (an upload, or a picture found through the search - `internal/imagesearch`, shared with HCA-Team - which fetches and stores it the same way), or a path to a bundled file under `web/home/` or `web/public/home/` (the way the sample data ships). A name that resolves to neither refuses the load, because a recorded image with nothing behind it is a bug, not a missing picture.

**A load either succeeds whole or refuses**, the same stance as the directory: the server does not start, or does not refresh, on a sheet that breaks a rule, and never serves a page quietly missing a link.

## Ordering

Categories and links have no order column. The sheet's row order is the display order: the Edit Categories window's arrows rewrite the `Categories` tab in the new order, and in Super Admin Mode the arrows on a link's card or tile move it one place among its category's links (`/api/apps/link/move`: its row and its nearest neighbour's of the same category trade places, and the `Links` tab is rewritten in the new order); moving a row in the sheet by hand does the same. Adding through the app appends, so a new link lands last in its category until it is moved.
