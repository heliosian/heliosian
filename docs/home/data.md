# Data model

Heliosian keeps no sheet of its own: the front page is rows of the data model (`docs/datamodel.md`), read in the page's first `/api/q` batch (`homeQueries` and `loadModel` in `web/home/state.js`) and written as `POST /api/q` batches (`web/home/writes.js`). What each table and column means is declared beside it in `internal/db/schema.go`; this file carries what spans them.

## Rows

- **`WIDGET`** (`Data: Config`) — one row per widget on the page, in `order`. `when`, `team`, `celebrate`, `school` (the Inbox), `birthday` and `todo` (Reminders) are the apps' own widgets, one row each; `apps` is the apps section, the community apps themselves; `links` is a section of links, its `group` naming the `GROUP` its links sit under, and no two rows share a `key` and `group`. A section's `name` is its heading, `icon` its mark (`icon:<name>`, one of `categoryIcons` in `web/home/dom.js`, drawn in the text's colour, or `image:<name>`, a picture the editor uploaded, served at `/<name>` in its own colours; blank or anything else gets a mark read off the heading - school, calendar, chat, or a grid - by `iconOf`), `style` `grid` (the editor's Icon grid) or `list` (Compact list), and `descriptions` whether each link's description, or each app's subtitle, shows under its name. `sidebar` puts a widget's first few items in the rail. `visible_to` is who sees it (Who sees what, below).
- **`APP`** (`Data: Config`) — one row per app key: `name` and `subtitle`, what the app switch, the apps section and every app's share and about cards call it (`Model.App` in `internal/db/homepages.go`, which `internal/app/wiring.go` hands each card style); `admins`, its admins group; `visible_to`, who is shown it in the switch and the apps section; and `order`, its place in both. The `home` row is Heliosian's own: its `admins` group is who edits the page, and the switch and the apps section list every row but it. This is visibility, never access: a direct link still opens an app to anyone signed in.
- **Sections and links** (`GROUP`, `Data: Groups`) — a section's group is a plain `group`, no slug, not `listed`, posting and replying `none`, open, visible to the Everyone group, made in the same batch as its `WIDGET`, and renamed with it. A link is a plain group whose `parent` is a section's group, carrying `name`, `description`, `url`, `order` (its place in its section) and `visible_to`; moving it to another section sets its `parent` and an `order` after that section's last. Its picture is its group's first ready `PHOTO`, added through `POST /api/do/photo` (the editor's upload or Find an image, stored first through the image routes Heliosian registers at `/api/home`, then posted as the photo, `setPicture` in `web/home/writes.js`), served at `/api/blob/<photo id>/image`; a link without one shows its section's mark.
- **`SETTING`** app `home`, key `layout` (`Data: Config`) — how each row of widgets is split: the rows' splits in order, `;`-separated, each one of `layouts` in `web/home/state.js`. Rows past the last take the first split. `go run ./tools/q read '(from SETTING (where (= app "home") (= key "layout")))'` prints it. The edit panel's Layout tab writes the whole value, inserting the row when there is none.
- **`TODO_MARK`** (`Data: People`) — a person's mark on a to-do in Reminders: `state` `done` or `saved`, and `changed`, one row per person and to-do, deleted when they clear it. Each person reads and writes only their own, so one parent's Done leaves the other's list alone. The to-dos themselves, and the summaries and key points the Inbox shows, are the digest's (`docs/datamodel.md`, Digest).

## Who sees what

Who sees a widget, an app, a section or a link is its `visible_to` group's effective members. Everyone is the `everyone` group. Anything narrower is a dedicated plain group named `<thing> Viewers` - not `listed`, posting and replying `none`, `added_by` the admin who made it - holding the rules from the editor as `RULE` rows and, for an app, the people named as `MEMBER` rows. `audienceEdits` in `web/home/writes.js` decides what a save writes, by what the thing's group is now:

- **Rules where it is Everyone or a shared group** (one any other widget, app or link names, a group with a slug, or one of another kind): a new `<thing> Viewers` group with the rules and people, and the thing's `visible_to` set to it, in one batch.
- **Rules where it is already dedicated** (a plain group with no slug that this thing alone names): its rules are replaced and its members changed to the people named, in place.
- **No rules and nobody named**: `visible_to` back to Everyone; a dedicated group's rules and members are deleted, and the group itself unless it is `listed`.
- **A link unticked Visible**: `visible_to` blank, which leaves it to Heliosian's admins, drawn with a Hidden badge; a dedicated group is cleared the same way.

The rules are Loop's (`docs/datamodel.md`, Effective members), edited with the same card (`rulesCard` in `web/common/listrules.js`), and the editor's preview of whom they pick out is `POST /api/do/draft-members`, which Heliosian's host registers as Loop's does. The read policies do the hiding: a `WIDGET`, an `APP` or a link group is read only by its `visible_to` group's effective members, so nobody is sent what is not theirs, and Heliosian's admins read every one, their page marking what is not shown to them.

## Who may edit

Heliosian's admins are the super admins and the effective members of the `home` `APP` row's `admins` group (`admin_of "home"`). Their grants are the Home admins section of the policies in `internal/db/policy.go`, over three definitions: `home_section` (a group a `WIDGET` names as its `group`), `home_thing` (a section's group or a link in one) and `home_audience` (a plain group with no slug that a widget, an app or a link names in `visible_to`). They read every `APP` and `WIDGET` and every plain and admins group with its members and rules; set an app's `name`, `subtitle`, `order` and `visible_to`; add and delete `links` widgets and set any widget's `name`, `icon`, `style`, `descriptions`, `sidebar`, `visible_to` and `order`; insert and set the `home` `layout` setting; add plain groups with no slug at the top or under a section, set a section's or link's `name`, `description`, `url`, `visible_to` and `order`, move a link between sections, and delete a link, or a top-level unlisted plain group with no slug and no managers (a section, or an audience group); add and remove the rules and members of an audience group; and add and remove a link's photos. `internal/db/homewrites_test.go` pins them. The apps section, its `apps` widget, is never added or deleted, only edited and moved; the editor refuses to delete a section that still holds links.

## Ordering

Widgets, apps and links are ordered by their `order` cells, sort keys (`docs/storage.md`, Order), never by where their rows sit. Dragging one in the edit panel sets the moved row's `order` alone, the browser working out a key between its new neighbours (`movedKey` in `web/common/order.js`); a new section or link gets a key after the last of its list. Placing a widget from the Layout tab's menus sets the keys of the widget picked and of the one it swaps with.
