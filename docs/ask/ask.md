# Helios Ask

Helios Ask is a chat with Claude for the Helios community: one signed-in member asks about the school, their family, the calendar, the people in the directory, the volunteer work, the parties or the email groups, and the answer streams in, drawn from the community's own apps and linking to them. It serves at ask.heliosian.com. The package is `internal/ask`, the API is `/api/ask/`, and it holds no sheet of its own: everything it knows it reads from the other apps' models as they stand, and from the community's documents - every newsletter and every announcement to all the families since 2018, what each classroom's parent list carried, the school website's pages, and the parent portal's pages and the handbook, curriculum decks and other documents it links (`artifacts.md`).

## The page

One page. A rail with the brand, New chat, the person's chats newest first with the open one lit and a remove on each, and a word on what the chat is for; the shared toolbar (`docs/toolbar.md`) without a search box, since the composer is the box, and `/` puts the cursor in it; the thread; and the composer pinned under it, a growing box and a send button, Enter sending and Shift-Enter breaking a line. While an answer is arriving the send button is a stop button: stopping keeps the question and the words so far as a turn of the chat, or, when no words had come yet, takes the question back into the box as though it had not been sent.

An empty thread shows the headline over the swoosh, a line on what to ask, and a few starters - questions the server draws from the person's own circumstances ("Who teaches Sam in Jays?") that send as they are. Each question sits on the right in a teal bubble; each answer on the left, drawn from the light markdown Claude writes (paragraphs, lists, tables, bold, italics, links), in the order it happened: the words written before a lookup, a chip naming the lookup ("Looking at the calendar", "Reading a directory page") as it starts, then the words after it; a lookup is named once however many times it ran, and lookups with no words between them share a line, wrapping as it fills. Until the answer has arrived whole, a braille spinner turns at its very end - after its last word, inside its last paragraph or list item, or beside its last chip - whether words are coming, a lookup is running or the stream has stalled. A link still being written shows as its words and becomes a link when it closes. A link to a person, a family, a classroom, an event on Helios When, a volunteer thing on HCA-Team, a party on Helios Celebrate or a group on Helios Loop is drawn as a chip: the words Claude wrote beside the person's or the family's photo from the directory, the classroom's bird picture (the chip tinted in the classroom's color from the Classroom Colors tab, as Who?'s own classroom chips are), the thing's or the party's picture, an event's day as a badge ("Fri Oct 3"), a group's people icon, or an initial when there is none. The server sends a card for each such link, once per answer, just before the words that hold it - what it points at and its picture or day, read for the person asking under the same rules the tools keep, so a link to a hidden thing, a closed party they do not host, an event the calendar does not show them or a group Loop does not show them stays a plain link - and the browser keeps the cards with the answer, so a chip is still a chip after a reload. Links reach the page as production addresses (`data.md`, Links), and the page lands them on its own tier, so a link to Who? from ask.heliosiandev.com opens who.heliosiandev.com.

The chats live in the browser's local storage, encrypted - the most recent of them, each titled by its first question - so they survive a reload, a closed tab, a browser restart and a deploy, and are this browser's and this device's alone. They are readable only while signed in: the key comes from the server on each load (`data.md`, The conversation), so after sign-out, from any app, what is left in the browser is ciphertext nothing in it can open. Each holds the whole conversation as the model saw it, everything it looked up included, and sends it along with the next question; the server keeps nothing (`data.md`, The conversation), so a chat picked up a week later goes on exactly where it stood. New chat starts another without touching the old ones; opening one from the rail (on a phone, the drawer behind the menu button) picks it up where it stood; the remove forgets it. A chat that has taken its fill of questions, or run long enough in all, asks for a new one, and someone who sends too many messages in an hour is asked to wait; how many each of those is, and how many chats a browser keeps, are the constants `data.md` names.

## What Claude knows

The prompt is two cached pieces. The first is the school: `internal/ask/prompt.md`, hand-written and built into the binary - what Helios is, the lingo (classrooms named for birds, bands, crews, room parents, the HCA, the Spring Celebration and the fun(d)raiser parties, Magic Tags, the school year turning over in July), what each app is for and its address, and how to answer - followed by what the models say right now: the grades and their bands, each band with its grades and classrooms, every classroom with its link, band, grades, teachers and crews, so any classroom Claude names can be a chip without a lookup, the staff departments, the calendar's categories with the descriptions the Tags tab gives them, the day types with their hours, and the school years. The second is the person asking: their record, their pronouns, each family with every student's grade, classroom, crew and teachers, the bands they are room parent for, the roles the other apps give them (the parties they host, the things they co-chair, the groups they manage) and their own tags by name, and the day and the school year; then the documents the chat knows - for a new chat the twenty newest of the last fourteen days, less the portal's, which are dated by when they were fetched, and from then on those plus each one the chat has since been told of - each with its day, its age, its title and the key `read_document` takes; and How links look, a few of the person's own links - their family, a child and the child's classroom, their next event, a volunteer thing, a party, a group - each with what its chip shows, drawn through the same `linkCard` the chips come from, so what the prompt says a chip shows is what the page shows. The prompt tells Claude to write a chip's words as the thing's name alone, to repeat nothing the chip already shows (an event's day), and to put nothing but the key inside a link's parentheses. Both are built afresh for every question from the same inputs, so the cached prefix holds turn after turn until those inputs change. A document that comes in while a conversation is under way, by the mail hook or a refresh (`artifacts.md`), is not yet in the prompt, so the next question carries a system message after it naming each new one in the same form; each is named once per conversation, and the prompt lists it from the question after.

The prompt tells Claude that a recent document can override the calendar and the other apps: a last-minute reminder, a changed time or place, a cancellation or a closure often reaches families by mail before the calendar catches up, or instead of it. For a question about something coming up it looks over the recent documents, reads any that bear on it, and says what a newer one says beside what the calendar says.

## What Claude can read

Fifteen tools, every one read-only, every one reading as the person signed in and never as anyone else (`data.md`, The tools). The directory: find people by words and filters, one person in full with their families, a family, the families living nearest a family by the addresses shared on Who?'s map, a classroom with its crews and room parents. The calendar: events in a date range with the other apps' folded in and the viewer's own standing on each, and the day plan for a date. HCA-Team: this year's things and who signed up, and one thing in full. Celebrate: the parties with their tickets and the household's own. Loop: the groups the viewer can see, with members for the ones they manage. Who?'s lists: the viewer's tags and Magic Tags. Heliosian: the front page's links. The community's documents: the passages that answer a question, and one document whole, each with a link when it is a page of the website or of the portal, and among them the mail of the Helios Loop groups the person manages or is on and can see (`artifacts.md`).

The mail among the documents is what went to everyone or to a whole class, and the website's pages are the school's public ones less the staff directory, the calendar and the site's own machinery. What a committee or a role kept to itself - the board and its committees, the HCA's teams, the room parents, the staff - is not on file at all, since Ask answers whoever asks it (`artifacts.md`, What is in it and what is not).

What the apps keep from a reader, the tools keep too: a masked phone, address or email stays blank, an opted-out person is not there, another person's tags are never read, a hidden volunteer list shows only its co-chairs and the household, a party's attendees reach its hosts alone, a pending or hidden thing reaches whoever runs it. Nothing an admin sees - the super admin list, the admin tabs, provenance, overrides, invoicing - reaches a tool. Staff Birthdays is not read at all.

Claude cannot act: no sign-ups, RSVPs, tickets, mail or edits. The prompt says so, and every answer that wants an action links to the page where the person can take it.

## Sample data

Sample mode signs in as the sample parent, who has two students in two classrooms, co-chairs International Night, manages the soccer team's group and is a room parent, so every tool has something to find. With an Anthropic key in `local/creds/anthropic.key` the sample server talks to Claude over the fictional community; without one it uses a fake that looks up today's day plan, so the page shows a tool at work, and streams a canned answer a word at a time.

## Brand

The app wears Heliosian's marks as stand-ins under `web/public/ask/brand/` and as its mark in the app switch (`web/public/common/brand/apps/ask.png`), until it has art of its own.
