# Helios Ask

Helios Ask is a chat with Claude for the Helios community: one signed-in member asks about the school, their family, the calendar, the people in the directory, the volunteer work, the parties or the email lists, and the answer streams in, drawn from the community's own apps and linking to them. It serves at ask.heliosian.com. The package is `internal/ask`, the API is `/api/ask/`, and it holds no sheet of its own: everything it knows it reads from the data model (`docs/datamodel.md`) as the person asking, through the same tools Helios MCP serves (`docs/tools.md`) - the people, groups and calendar, and the community's documents in `DOCUMENT`: the school mail the mail hook files (`artifacts.md`), the Helios Loop email lists' posts, the school website's and the parent portal's pages and the files they shared, and the Helios Wiki.

## The page

One page. A rail with the brand, New chat, the person's chats newest first with the open one lit and a remove on each, and a word on what the chat is for; the shared toolbar (`docs/toolbar.md`) without a search box, since the composer is the box, and `/` puts the cursor in it; the thread; and the composer pinned under it, a growing box and a send button, Enter sending and Shift-Enter breaking a line. While an answer is arriving the send button is a stop button: stopping keeps the question and the words so far as a turn of the chat, or, when no words had come yet, takes the question back into the box as though it had not been sent.

An empty thread shows the headline over the swoosh, a line on what to ask, and a few starters - questions the server draws from the person's own circumstances ("Who teaches Sam in Jays?") that send as they are. Each question sits on the right in a teal bubble beside the person's avatar - their photo, or their initial without one; each answer on the left beside the bot's face (`web/public/ask/brand/bot-face-1.png`), which, while the answer is arriving, alternates with its open-mouthed `bot-face-2.png` and settles back on the first when the answer ends, drawn from the light markdown Claude writes (paragraphs, lists, tables, bold, italics, links), in the order it happened: the words written before a lookup, a chip naming the lookup ("Looking at the calendar", "Looking at a classroom") as it starts, then the words after it; a lookup is named once however many times it ran, and lookups with no words between them share a line, wrapping as it fills. Until the answer has arrived whole, a braille spinner turns at its very end - after its last word, inside its last paragraph or list item, or beside its last chip - whether words are coming, a lookup is running or the stream has stalled. A link still being written shows as its words and becomes a link when it closes. A link to a person, a family, a classroom or grade, an event on Helios When, a volunteer thing on HCA-Team, a party on Helios Celebrate or an email list on Helios Loop is drawn as a chip: the words Claude wrote beside the first `PHOTO` of the person or group (its thumbnail, `/api/blob/<photo>/thumbnail` on Ask's own host), the chip tinted in a classroom's or grade's `color`, an event's day as a badge ("Fri Oct 3"), an email list's people icon, or an initial when there is no picture. The server sends a card for each such link, once per answer, just before the words that hold it - what it points at and its picture or day, the row read again as the person asking, so a link to anything they may not read stays a plain link, as does one no tool or prompt of theirs produced - and the browser keeps the cards with the answer, so a chip is still a chip after a reload. Links reach the page as production addresses (`data.md`, Links), and the page lands them on its own tier, so a link to Who? from ask.heliosiandev.com opens who.heliosiandev.com.

The chats live in the browser's local storage, encrypted - the most recent of them, each titled by its first question - so they survive a reload, a closed tab, a browser restart and a deploy, and are this browser's and this device's alone. They are readable only while signed in: the key comes from the server on each load (`data.md`, The conversation), so after sign-out, from any app, what is left in the browser is ciphertext nothing in it can open. Each holds the whole conversation as the model saw it, everything it looked up included, and sends it along with the next question; the server keeps nothing (`data.md`, The conversation), so a chat picked up a week later goes on exactly where it stood. New chat starts another without touching the old ones; opening one from the rail (on a phone, the drawer behind the menu button) picks it up where it stood; the remove forgets it. A chat that has taken its fill of questions, or run long enough in all, asks for a new one, and someone who sends too many messages in an hour is asked to wait; how many each of those is, and how many chats a browser keeps, are the constants `data.md` names.

## What Claude knows

The prompt is two cached pieces. The first is the school: `internal/ask/prompt.md`, hand-written and built into the binary - what Helios is, the lingo (classrooms named for birds, bands, crews, room parents, the HCA, the Spring Celebration and the fun(d)raiser parties, the school year turning over in July), what each app is for and its address, and how to answer - followed by what the data model says right now: the grades and their bands, each band with its grades and classrooms, every classroom with its link, band, the grades of its students, its teachers (staff whose classroom it is) and its crews with theirs, so any classroom Claude names can be a chip without a lookup, the staff departments, the calendar's categories with their descriptions, the day types (the categories holding `day` groups) with theirs, the school years from the year calendar's first and last days, and the current celebration (Celebrate's `Current` setting). The second is the person asking, from `helios_whoami`: their name, roles and pronouns, each family with its parents' emails and every student's grade, classroom, crew and teachers, the groups they are in by name (a lead marked), the groups they manage and the apps they are an admin of, and the day and the school year; then the mail the chat knows - for a new chat the twenty newest `mail` documents of the last fourteen days the person may read, and from then on those plus each one the chat has since been told of - each with its day, its age, its name, the id `helios_read_document` takes and the email lists it went to; and How links look, a few of the person's own links - their family, a child and the child's classroom, the next event, activity and party, an email list - each with what its chip shows, drawn through the same `linkCard` the chips come from, so what the prompt says a chip shows is what the page shows. The prompt tells Claude to write a chip's words as the thing's name alone, to repeat nothing the chip already shows (an event's day), and to put nothing but the key inside a link's parentheses. Both are read from the data model as the person asking on a chat's first question, and the chat keeps that exact prompt to the end (`data.md`, The conversation), so the cached prefix and Claude's own earlier reasoning hold however the data or the day moves on. What changes meanwhile reaches Claude as a system message after the question that first meets it: mail that has come in by the mail hook or a Loop post (`artifacts.md`), each named once per conversation in the same form, and a new day, said once, so dates are reckoned from today rather than the day the chat began.

The prompt tells Claude that a recent document can override the calendar and the other apps: a last-minute reminder, a changed time or place, a cancellation or a closure often reaches families by mail before the calendar catches up, or instead of it. For a question about something coming up it looks over the recent documents, reads any that bear on it, and says what a newer one says beside what the calendar says.

## What Claude can read

Every tool Helios MCP serves, the shared set in `internal/tools` (`docs/tools.md`), every one read-only and run as the person signed in, never as anyone else. The directory: people by words and filters, a person with their families, a classroom with its teachers, crews, students and room parents, any group in depth with its members marked student, parent or staff, and the families living nearest a family by the addresses shared on Who?'s map. The calendar: events, parties and HCA-Team activities in a date range with the viewer's own standing on each, and the school day plan for a date. HCA-Team: a year's activities and who signed up. Celebrate: the parties with their tickets. Loop: the email lists the viewer can see, with their members. Heliosian: the front page's links. The community's documents: search by name and meaning, each email's or file's near-copies, and one document whole. Beyond those, any row by ID, and the query language itself with the schema and the policies, for whatever the shortcuts do not answer.

What the policies keep from a reader, the tools keep too, because every read is `Model.Run` as the person asking: a withheld phone or address stays blank, an opted-out person is not there, a volunteer list shows only what its `members_visible_to` lets them see, a pending group reaches only whoever runs it, and an email list's mail reaches only whoever is on it or manages it (`docs/datamodel.md`, Documents). An admin's tools see what that admin's pages see.

Claude cannot act: no sign-ups, RSVPs, tickets, mail or edits. The prompt says so, and every answer that wants an action links to the page where the person can take it.

## Sample data

Sample mode reads the sample data model (`sampledata/data*/`): Rowan Ashdown, whose child is in the Hummingbirds with a crew and a teacher, co-chairs International Night, holds a Taco Night ticket, is one of the Jayvens room parents and has a neighbouring family on the map, so most tools have something to find. The sample server never reaches Claude: its intercept of `api.anthropic.com` (`intercept.Claude` in `internal/intercept`) calls the first tool offered, `helios_whoami`, so the page shows a tool at work, then streams a canned answer a word at a time with the tool's result after it.

## Brand

The app wears Heliosian's marks as stand-ins under `web/public/ask/brand/` and as its mark in the app switch (`web/public/common/brand/apps/ask.png`), until it has art of its own.
