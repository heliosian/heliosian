# The toolbar

Every app puts the same bar across the top of its content: 48px tall in a pale wash of the brand teal (`#ecf3f2`) under a hairline, the app's own search on the left, and on the right the signed-in person's face and then, at the far edge, the switch to the other apps. Helios Staff Birthdays joined the shell on 2026-09-12, on Celebrate's pattern. It is one stylesheet and one script, `web/common/toolbar.css` and `web/common/toolbar.js`, served to every app behind sign-in (`app.Files` reads `web/common/` after the app's own directory); each app's `index.html` links the stylesheet ahead of its own, and its `style.css` supplies the palette the bar's rules read (`--brand`, `--line`, `--ink`, `--muted`, `--input`).

## Search

The pill holds whatever the app searches - Who? finds people, grades and gradebands as you type; HCA-Team filters the page that is open, or jumps to the opportunities list with the words when the open page has no filter of its own; Heliosian filters its links; Helios Calendar filters the whole year, and wears amber while it does (`docs/calendar/calendar.md`). A `/` badge at the pill's right edge names the shortcut: a bare `/` anywhere on the page (no modifier, nothing else focused) puts the cursor in the box, the way GitHub and Slack do, and `toolbar.js`'s `onSlash` is that binding. On a phone Who? and HCA-Team fold the box behind a magnifier in their compact bar, and `/` opens that overlay instead.

## The alerts

Before the avatar, the same two badges in every app: a red count of the things the directory wants updated for the new year (a student's photo or facts past the config sheet's thresholds, a family photo missing or old), and a warning triangle when the family's Helios Who? privacy settings disagree with Veracross. Who? reckons them client-side from its own model (`web/who/stale.js`, `pages/privacy.js`) and hangs its to-do dropdown off the count; the server reckons the same numbers in `internal/who/alerts.go` (`Model.Alerts`, with a test pinning it to the client's answer for the sample parent) and hands them to HCA-Team and Heliosian in their models as `alerts`, whose toolbars link the badges across to Who?'s My Family and My Privacy pages (`renderAlerts` in `toolbar.js`). Each badge drops a card on hover, on the same grace as the bar's menus (`alertMenu` and `alertCard` in `toolbar.js`, the pink card of Who?'s own checklist, in each badge's `.topbar-alert-wrap` and kept inside a narrow viewport): under the count, Who? shows the new-year checklist itself - the one a click used to open, and a tap still toggles - and the other apps its title over a line to My Family in Who?; under the triangle, Who? says which detail is visible on Veracross but hidden here (the banner's sentence, `privacyMismatchText`) and the other apps that something is, each over the link to My Privacy. The badges stay links, so a click or a tap follows one.

## The account

The avatar is the hero photo the directory leads with for the viewer - their own, else their family's - or their initial when there is none, filled by `renderAvatars`; each app's model carries `user.photoUrl` and `user.initial` for it. While Super Admin Mode is on - the same switch, under the same name, in every app's account menu - the avatar wears a red dotted ring (`body.is-super`, set by each app through `markSuper`), so the mode is never on unnoticed. There is no name label: the avatar's only job is opening the account menu, whose contents are the app's own (My Privacy in Who?, the admin switches in HCA-Team, Edit Categories in Heliosian, Sign Out in all) under two rows every app has: the signed-in address, and View Profile, the person's own page in Who? - `/people/<local part of the email>` on Who?'s origin, which `renderProfileLink` in `toolbar.js` points the row at in every app but Who?, which fills its own from its model. The menu opens on hover - it shows while the mouse is over the avatar or the menu itself, and goes once the mouse has left both, after a 150ms grace so crossing the gap between them does not shut it - and a click with the mouse leaves it open (or opens it again after Escape); on a phone, where nothing hovers, a tap toggles it. `initUserMenu` in `toolbar.js` wires both, and since 2026-09-13 each app calls it in place of its own click handler, keeping only its document-click and Escape closers.

## The app switch

The Heliosian mark on its white tile with a chevron, at the bar's far right. The tile is a link home - Heliosian on the page's own tier, its `href` set by `initAppSwitch` - so a click goes there, and while the mouse is over it (or over the list) it opens a list of every community app, on the same hover-with-grace as the account menu; on a phone a tap toggles the list instead, that being the only way to it there, and a tap elsewhere or Escape closes it. A phone is told by `(hover: none)` - a device whose primary pointer cannot hover - which `hoverClick` in `toolbar.js` asks before it trusts the click's own pointer type, since some mobile browsers report a tap as a mouse click; on a laptop with a touchscreen the pointer type decides, a finger toggling and the mouse going home. The list holds every community app - the registry's, `internal/home.Apps`, less any that Heliosian's Visibility tab narrows to people other than the reader - each with its transparent-background mark, name and tagline (the tagline the tab's, editable in Heliosian's admin), the current one tinted, and under them two lines: "Report a problem or idea", which opens the feedback form (`docs/feedback.md`), and "Built for the community, by the community" behind the GitHub mark, linking to the repository. The list is Heliosian's (`internal/home.Home` and `Apps`, the one registry of the apps, which the front page's apps section and the Visibility tab share): `initAppSwitch` in `toolbar.js` fills and wires every `.app-switch` in the page after asking the page's own origin for `/api/apps/switch` (`home.RegisterSwitch`, on every mux) - the apps in order, and which of them the `Visibility` tab narrows to a list this person is not on (`docs/home/data.md`) - and leaves those rows out, except the app being viewed, which stays listed as the current one since the reader is already there; if the ask fails the list is Heliosian alone, always a way home, this being visibility and not access. The links go to each app's host label - its registry key, except where the registry says otherwise (the calendar's is `when`) - and follow the tier of the page's own hostname: from `who.lab.heliosian.com` the portal is `team.lab.heliosian.com`, from `who.local.heliosian.com:8080` it is `team.local.heliosian.com:8080`, and Heliosian is the apex (`heliosian.com`) only in production, `home.<tier>` elsewhere; an alias hostname counts as its app's, as `appFor` in `internal/app` routes it. The tile and the marks live in `web/public/common/brand/` (`heliosian-tile.png`, and `apps/<key>.png` for each registry key; an app without transparent art yet serves a stand-in there, noted in its own docs), which every app serves under `/brand/`.

## Admin Tools

Every app's Admin Tools wears the same chrome, from `web/common/admin.css` (scoped under `.admin`): a teal header with the app's mark, "Admin", the signed-in address and a close button back to the app; a white rail of tabs in groups (Display, Editing & Control, and in Who? Data Overrides) on the left; cards on the right, one tab's panel at a time. Who? and Heliosian serve it as a page of their own (`admin.html`); HCA-Team draws it inside its shell at `/admin`, whose rail, toolbar and tab bar step aside (`body.is-admin`). On a phone the rail becomes a strip of pills (`.admin-strip`), except in Who?, which keeps its fold-out menu.

## On phones

The bar is the topmost thing in every app, pinned under the status bar, at 48px: Who? and HCA-Team add a hamburger at its left for their drawer, the search box stays a box (no magnifier-and-overlay), and the keyboard hint and the switch's chevron drop. Under it Who? and HCA-Team keep a slim teal strip for the page title (or a back arrow and a record's name); Heliosian's rail is a drawer behind the hamburger, the same column slid in from the left.
