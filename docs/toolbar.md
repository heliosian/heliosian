# The toolbar

Every app puts the same bar across the top of its content: 48px tall in a pale wash of the brand teal (`#ecf3f2`) under a hairline, the app's own search on the left, and on the right the signed-in person's face and then, at the far edge, the switch to the other apps. It is one stylesheet and one script, `web/common/toolbar.css` and `web/common/toolbar.js`, served to every app behind sign-in (`app.Files` reads `web/common/` after the app's own directory); each app's `index.html` links the stylesheet ahead of its own, and its `style.css` supplies the palette the bar's rules read (`--brand`, `--line`, `--ink`, `--muted`, `--input`).

## Search

The pill holds whatever the app searches - Who? finds people, grades and gradebands as you type; HCA-Team filters the page that is open, or jumps to the opportunities list with the words when the open page has no filter of its own; Heliosian filters its links; Helios When filters the whole year, and wears amber while it does (`docs/calendar/calendar.md`). A `/` badge at the pill's right edge names the shortcut: a bare `/` anywhere on the page (no modifier, nothing else focused) puts the cursor in the box, the way GitHub and Slack do, and `toolbar.js`'s `onSlash` is that binding. On a phone Who? and HCA-Team fold the box behind a magnifier in their compact bar, and `/` opens that overlay instead.

## The alerts

Before the avatar, the same two badges in every app: a red count of the things the directory wants updated for the new year (a student's photo or facts past the config sheet's thresholds, a family photo missing or old), and a warning triangle when the family's Helios Who? privacy settings disagree with Veracross. Who? reckons them client-side from its own model (`web/who/stale.js`, `pages/privacy.js`) and hangs its to-do dropdown off the count; the server reckons the same numbers in `internal/who/alerts.go` (`Model.Alerts`, with a test pinning it to the client's answer for the sample parent) and hands them to HCA-Team and Heliosian in their models as `alerts`, whose toolbars link the badges across to Who?'s My Family and My Privacy pages (`renderAlerts` in `toolbar.js`). Each badge drops a card on hover, on the same grace as the bar's menus (`alertMenu` and `alertCard` in `toolbar.js`, the pink card of Who?'s own checklist, in each badge's `.topbar-alert-wrap` and kept inside a narrow viewport): under the count, Who? shows the new-year checklist itself - the one a click used to open, and a tap still toggles - and the other apps its title over a line to My Family in Who?; under the triangle, Who? says which detail is visible on Veracross but hidden here (the banner's sentence, `privacyMismatchText`) and the other apps that something is, each over the link to My Privacy. The badges stay links, so a click or a tap follows one.

## The account

The avatar is the hero photo the directory leads with for the viewer - their own, else their family's - or their initial when there is none, filled by `renderAvatars`; each app's model carries `user.photoUrl` and `user.initial` for it. While Super Admin Mode is on - the same switch, under the same name, in every app's account menu - the avatar wears a red dotted ring (`body.is-super`, set by each app through `markSuper`), so the mode is never on unnoticed. There is no name label: the avatar's only job is opening the account menu, whose contents are the app's own (My Privacy in Who?, the admin switches in HCA-Team, Edit Categories in Heliosian, Sign Out in all) under two rows every app has: the signed-in address, and View Profile, the person's own page in Who? - `/people/<local part of the email>` on Who?'s origin, which `renderProfileLink` in `toolbar.js` points the row at in every app but Who?, which fills its own from its model. The menu opens on hover - it shows while the mouse is over the avatar or the menu itself, and goes once the mouse has left both, after a 150ms grace so crossing the gap between them does not shut it - and a click with the mouse leaves it open (or opens it again after Escape); on a phone, where nothing hovers, a tap toggles it. `initUserMenu` in `toolbar.js` wires both, and each app calls it, keeping only its document-click and Escape closers.

## Spoof Mode

Before the avatar, for the platform's super admins alone (`docs/config.md`), an eye in the brand teal: the switch to viewing every app as someone else. It drops down on hover like the bar's other menus (`initSpoof` in `toolbar.js`): the last five people viewed as, most recent first, each with the word that places them, then a search of the whole directory by name, address or that word, eight rows at a time, Enter taking the first or the one arrowed to - and, while a spoof is on, a head naming whom with a Stop. The mouse leaving the menu does not close it while the search box has focus. Picking someone starts the spoof and lands on the app's front page, since the person viewed as may not be allowed where the admin was; while it is on the eye becomes a red pill, "Viewing as *Name*" with a cross that stops it and reloads in place (on a phone the pill is the red eye and the cross alone, the name in the menu under it). Everything else in the bar - the avatar, the badges, the account menu, and every page behind them - is then that person's, since the page is drawn for them.

The spoof is sign-in's, not any app's (`internal/auth/spoof.go`), which is what makes it hold everywhere: it is a second signed cookie beside the session's, on the same tier-wide domain, naming who is really signed in and whom they are viewing as, good for a day at most; `Wrap` (and the sample server's `Fixed`) resolves it once per request, so `auth.Email` - what every app already reads - answers with the person viewed as, reads and writes alike, and `auth.RealEmail` keeps the admin. A cookie that does not verify, names another admin, has run out, names someone the directory no longer lists, or belongs to someone since taken off the super admin list is simply ignored. The switch's own routes - `GET /auth/spoof` (whether to show it, who is viewing as whom, the recent five), `GET /auth/spoof/people` (the directory, for the search) and `POST /auth/spoof` (an address to start, blank to stop) - answer for the admin really signed in, so the way back is never locked out by the spoof it ends; the recent five ride in a third cookie, `spoofed`, a year long. `internal/app` builds the one `auth.Spoof` every app's sign-in shares from the super admin list and the directory. Signing out ends the spoof too. The request log records the admin as `user` and the person viewed as as `as` (`docs/deploy.md`).

## The app switch

The Heliosian mark on its white tile with a chevron, at the bar's far right. The tile is a link home - Heliosian on the page's own tier, its `href` set by `initAppSwitch` - so a click goes there, and while the mouse is over it (or over the list) it opens a list of every community app, on the same hover-with-grace as the account menu; on a phone a tap toggles the list instead, that being the only way to it there, and a tap elsewhere or Escape closes it. A phone is told by `(hover: none)` - a device whose primary pointer cannot hover - which `hoverClick` in `toolbar.js` asks before it trusts the click's own pointer type, since some mobile browsers report a tap as a mouse click; on a laptop with a touchscreen the pointer type decides, a finger toggling and the mouse going home. The list holds every community app - the registry's, `internal/home.Apps`, less any that Heliosian's Visibility tab narrows to people other than the reader - each with its transparent-background mark, name and tagline (the tagline the tab's, editable in Heliosian's admin), the current one tinted, and under them two lines: "Report a problem or idea", which opens the feedback form (`docs/feedback.md`), and "Built for the community, by the community" behind the GitHub mark, linking to the repository. The list is Heliosian's (`internal/home.Home` and `Apps`, the one registry of the apps, which the front page's apps section and the Visibility tab share): `initAppSwitch` in `toolbar.js` fills and wires every `.app-switch` in the page after asking the page's own origin for `/api/apps/switch` (`home.RegisterSwitch`, on every mux) - the apps in order, and which of them the `Visibility` tab narrows to a list this person is not on (`docs/home/data.md`) - and leaves those rows out, except the app being viewed, which stays listed as the current one since the reader is already there; if the ask fails the list is Heliosian alone, always a way home, this being visibility and not access. The links go to each app's host label - its registry key, except where the registry says otherwise (the calendar's is `when`) - and follow the tier of the page's own hostname: from `who.heliosian.com` the portal is `team.heliosian.com`, from `who.local.heliosian.com:8080` it is `team.local.heliosian.com:8080`, and Heliosian is the apex (`heliosian.com`) only in production, `home.<tier>` elsewhere; an alias hostname counts as its app's, as `appFor` in `internal/app` routes it. The tile and the marks live in `web/public/common/brand/` (`heliosian-tile.png`, and `apps/<key>.png` for each registry key; an app without transparent art yet serves a stand-in there, noted in its own docs), which every app serves under `/brand/`.

## Admin Tools

Every app's Admin Tools wears the same chrome, from `web/common/admin.css` (scoped under `.admin`): a teal header with the app's mark, "Admin", the signed-in address and a close button back to the app; a white rail of tabs in groups (Display, Editing & Control, and in Who? Data Overrides) on the left; cards on the right, one tab's panel at a time. Who? and Heliosian serve it as a page of their own (`admin.html`); HCA-Team draws it inside its shell at `/admin`, whose rail, toolbar and tab bar step aside (`body.is-admin`). On a phone the rail becomes a strip of pills (`.admin-strip`), except in Who?, which keeps its fold-out menu.

## On phones

The bar is the topmost thing in every app, pinned under the status bar, at 48px: Who? and HCA-Team add a hamburger at its left for their drawer, the search box stays a box (no magnifier-and-overlay), and the keyboard hint and the switch's chevron drop. Under it Who? and HCA-Team keep a slim teal strip for the page title (or a back arrow and a record's name); Heliosian's rail is a drawer behind the hamburger, the same column slid in from the left.
