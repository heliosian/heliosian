# The toolbar

Every app puts the same bar across the top of its content: 48px tall in a pale wash of the brand teal (`#ecf3f2`) under a hairline, the app's own search on the left, and on the right the signed-in person's face and then, at the far edge, the switch to the other apps. It is one stylesheet, `web/common/toolbar.css`, and the scripts beside it in `web/common/`: `shell.js`, which builds the bar, and the pieces it wires - `appswitch.js` (the app switch, the hover and tap behaviour every menu in the bar shares, and `appOrigin` and `whoLink`, the addresses of the other apps on the page's own tier), `usermenu.js` (the avatar and the account menu), `alerts.js` (the badges and their cards), `spoof.js` (Spoof Mode) and `feedback.js` (the Report a problem form and the page's recent errors it sends) - all served to every app behind sign-in (`app.Files` reads `web/common/` after the app's own directory); each app's `index.html` links the stylesheet ahead of its own and leaves an empty `<header class="topbar" id="topbar">`, and the shared palette (`docs/who/design.md`, Palette) supplies the colours the bar's rules read. `shell.js` builds the bar - the menu button, the search box, the two alert badges, the avatar and account menu (email, View Profile, the app's own rows, Admin Tools for its admins, Sign Out) and the app switch - and draws the app's shell around it: the rail's nav, the phone's tab bar and drawer (the drawer always ending with the person, Admin Tools for an admin, and Sign Out), the page title, search with its carried words, and the phone's viewport height. Each app hands `initShell` its name, how to draw its nav and tab bar, what its search is for, and any rows of its own (HCA-Team's Show Hidden Things, Who?'s My Privacy); Heliosian and Helios Ask keep rails of their own and take only the bar and account menu, through `initTopbar`. Helios When and Who? drive their own search results under the box.

Every request the bar makes - the badges, the app switch's list, Spoof Mode's state and its directory - goes through `api` (`web/common/api.js`), directly or through the resource API's client (`web/common/data.js`, `docs/api.md`), so a signed-out page reloads to sign-in as any other request would. A request that fails is kept by `noteError` in `feedback.js` among the page's recent errors, which a report from Report a problem carries (`docs/feedback.md`), and what it would have drawn says so where the bar has somewhere to say it: the bell's `?`, Spoof Mode's search. A badge whose request fails is simply not shown.

## Search

The pill holds whatever the app searches - Who? finds people, grades and gradebands as you type; HCA-Team filters the page that is open, or jumps to the opportunities list with the words when the open page has no filter of its own; Heliosian filters its links; Helios When filters the whole year, and wears amber while it does (`docs/when/calendar.md`). A `/` badge at the pill's right edge names the shortcut: a bare `/` anywhere on the page (no modifier, nothing else focused) puts the cursor in the box, the way GitHub and Slack do, and `shell.js`'s `onSlash` is that binding. On a phone Who? and HCA-Team fold the box behind a magnifier in their compact bar, and `/` opens that overlay instead.

## The alerts

Before the avatar, the same two badges in every app: a red count of the things the directory wants updated for the new year (a student's photo or facts past the config sheet's thresholds, a family photo missing or old), and a warning triangle when the family's Helios Who? privacy settings disagree with Veracross, its count on a dot - one for each detail hidden in Who? but visible on Veracross, the address and the phone, so 1 or 2. The server hands every app both as lists (`model.Alerts`: `stale`, each thing to update by what it is and whose - "Sam's photo", "Your facts", "Family photo" - and `privacy`, "address" and "phone"), and each badge's card lists them: a white card with a bar of the badge's colour down its left and a point up to the badge, the count large beside what they are and a close, a row for each - its icon in a pale disc beside a chip naming it, to where it is set - and a button across the foot to the whole list (`alertList` in `alerts.js`; RSVP's and the approvals' cards are the same). Every badge is a dark teal disc with its icon in white - a bell for the count of things to update, its number on a red dot, the privacy triangle's on a yellow one - evenly spaced by the bar's own gap. The badges are the same in every app, Who? included, all drawn by `renderAlerts` in `alerts.js`: the server reckons the lists in `internal/model/alerts.go` (`Directory.Alerts`, with a test pinning it to the client's answer for the sample parent) and serves them as the viewer's one `alerts` resource (`GET /api/alerts`, `docs/api.md`, Heliosian), which the shell reads once for every app but Who? (`initTopbar` in `shell.js`) - when that fetch fails the bell shows with a `?` where the count goes, its card saying the check could not be made - and Who? reckons the same lists client-side from its own model (`web/who/stale.js`, `pages/privacy.js`) and hands those to `renderAlerts` itself. Each badge links across to Who?'s My Family or My Privacy page, and drops a card on hover, on the same grace as the bar's menus (`alertMenu` in `alerts.js`, in each badge's `.topbar-alert-wrap` and kept inside a narrow viewport), listing each thing over a link to the page where it is done. The badges stay links, so a click or a tap follows one.

## RSVP

First among the badges, while any invitation waits for the viewer's reply: a calendar in the brand teal with their count on a red dot, going to the RSVP list on Helios When (`/mine/rsvp`), and under it, on the same hover as the other badges, a card listing each - its title and when - soonest first, each to its page there (`showRSVP` in `alerts.js`). `initAlerts`, which the shell's `initTopbar` calls for every app, reads this badge and the two below in one `POST /api/query` on the page's own host, and this one is `events?waiting` (`docs/api.md`, Helios When): the events the viewer's household is invited to, still to come, not called off, that they do not host and have not answered - what My Events' RSVP holds.

## Approvals

For whoever may approve something waiting in HCA-Team, Helios Celebrate or Helios When, while anything does: an hourglass with the count on an amber dot beside the RSVP calendar - going to the approvals of the app with the first of them (`/approvals` on HCA-Team and Celebrate, Admin Tools on When), and under it the same card as RSVP's listing each - its title, its app and its day - soonest first, each to its page there, with no link across the foot since the things come from several apps (`showApprovals` in `alerts.js`). What waits is three reads, each kept to what the viewer's `can` says they may approve: HCA-Team's `activities?can=approve` (an admin, or whoever runs what a pending activity sits under), Celebrate's `parties?status=pending&can=status` and the calendar's `events?status=pending&can=approve`, the shared events waiting for approval (`docs/api.md`). The calendar and the hourglass are drawn as large as the directory's count beside them.

## Late birthdays

First among the badges while a Staff Birthdays step the viewer owes is past its day (`birthdays?late`, each birthday's `late` field worked out by `lateFor` in `internal/model/birthdays_late.go`, `docs/api.md`, Staff Birthdays; `showLate` in `alerts.js`): a birthday assigned to them whose charity is not in by its due-by day (Birthday's `Due By Lead Days` setting, two days before the newsletter unless changed), or, before then, whose outreach is not marked done after its day to ask; and - for someone on the Birthday team's Comms Team - a birthday with its charity in whose newsletter has gone out without it marked used. A Birthday admin (the Admins tab and the super admins) sees everyone's, the unassigned outreach included. A cake with the count on a red dot, going to Birthday's My Jobs (an admin's, Process, the badge knowing an admin by `birthday.admins` in `/api/me`), and under it a card listing each, oldest first - the staff member, the step and the day it was due, and for an admin whose assignee's step it is or Unassigned - to their page there.

## The account

The avatar is the hero photo the directory leads with for the viewer - their own, else their family's - or their initial when there is none, filled by `renderAvatars`; each app's model carries `user.photoUrl` and `user.initial` for it. There is no name label: the avatar's only job is opening the account menu, whose contents are the app's own (My Privacy in Who?, Show Hidden Things in HCA-Team, Edit Page in Heliosian, Sign Out in all, which ends the address's sessions on every browser, `docs/config.md`) under two rows every app has: the signed-in address, and View Profile, the person's own page in Who? - `/people/<local part of the email>` on Who?'s origin, which `renderProfileLink` in `usermenu.js` points the row at in every app but Who?, which fills its own from its model. The menu opens on hover - it shows while the mouse is over the avatar or the menu itself, and goes once the mouse has left both, after a 150ms grace so crossing the gap between them does not shut it - and a click with the mouse leaves it open (or opens it again after Escape); on a phone, where nothing hovers, a tap toggles it. `initUserMenu` in `usermenu.js` wires both, and the shell calls it for every app, keeping only its document-click and Escape closers.

## Admins

There is no admin mode. An app's admins hold every allowance its admin list confers (`docs/dev.md`, The rules live with the thing) always, and a super admin every app's admin powers and the super-admin ones; their pages show every control those allow. Staff Birthdays, on the resource API, shows each control by the resource's `can`, and its admin-only pages by the viewer holding `birthday.configure` in `/api/me`; Helios Celebrate and HCA-Team likewise, their admin pages and controls by the `celebrate.` or `team.` allowance each one's rule checks. Heliosian shows its editing and Admin Tools by `home.configure` in `/api/me` (`isAdmin` in `web/home/state.js`). In the other apps `isAdmin()` is being on the app's admin list, what the pages' admin controls go by. The Admin Tools links are the shell's, from `/api/me`.

## Spoof Mode

Before the avatar, for the platform's super admins alone (the `super-admins` group, below), an eye in the brand teal: the switch to viewing every app as someone else. It drops down on hover like the bar's other menus (`initSpoof` in `spoof.js`): the last five people viewed as, most recent first, each with the word that places them, then a search of the whole directory by name, address or that word, eight rows at a time, Enter taking the first or the one arrowed to (the directory is asked for when the menu first opens; while it is on its way the results say Loading…, and if it cannot be had they say why, and the next opening asks again) - and, while a spoof is on, a head naming whom with a Stop. The mouse leaving the menu does not close it while the search box has focus. Picking someone starts the spoof and reloads the page in place, so the admin sees the same page as that person; while it is on the whole bar turns hot pink in every app and every theme (`body.is-spoofing`), so nobody forgets whose account they are in, and the eye becomes a white pill, "Viewing as *Name*" with a cross that stops it and reloads in place (on a phone the pill is the red eye and the cross alone, the name in the menu under it). Everything else in the bar - the avatar, the badges, the account menu, and every page behind them - is then that person's, since the page is drawn for them.

The spoof is sign-in's, not any app's (`internal/auth/spoof.go`), which is what makes it hold everywhere: it is a second signed cookie beside the session's, on the same tier-wide domain, naming who is really signed in and whom they are viewing as, good for a day at most; `Wrap` (and the sample server's `Fixed`) resolves it once per request, so `auth.Email` - what every app already reads - answers with the person viewed as, reads and writes alike, and `auth.RealEmail` keeps the admin. A cookie that does not verify, names another admin, has run out, names someone sign-in would no longer admit, or belongs to someone since taken out of the `super-admins` group is simply ignored. The switch's own routes - `GET /auth/spoof` (whether to show it, who is viewing as whom, the recent five) and `POST /auth/spoof` (an address to start, blank to stop) - answer for the admin really signed in, so the way back is never locked out by the spoof it ends; the search reads the data model's people through `web/common/directory.js` (`docs/api.md`, Client), as the person viewed as; the recent five ride in a third cookie, `spoofed`, a year long. `internal/app` builds the one `auth.Spoof` every app's sign-in shares from the data model (`spoofing` in `internal/app/directory.go`): who may spoof is `db.Model.SuperAdmin`, an effective member of the data model's `super-admins` group (not the Config sheet's list, which gates the old apps' admin tools), and whom is `db.Model.SignedInAs`, someone sign-in would admit, known by their primary address and shown name. Signing out ends the spoof too. The request log records the admin as `user` and the person viewed as as `as` (`docs/deploy.md`), and every app's Change Log records the person viewed as under Actor and the admin under Real Actor, which holds `auth.RealEmail` for every row and is blank only for a change nobody signed in made, such as an unsubscribe by mail. What the admin sends and the ownership cells it writes (`Added By`, `Created By`, an RSVP's `Answered By`) stay the person viewed as's.

## The app switch

The Heliosian mark on its white tile with a chevron, at the bar's far right. The tile is a link home - Heliosian on the page's own tier, its `href` set by `initAppSwitch` - so a click goes there, and while the mouse is over it (or over the list) it opens a list of every community app, on the same hover-with-grace as the account menu; on a phone a tap toggles the list instead, that being the only way to it there, and a tap elsewhere or Escape closes it. A phone is told by `(hover: none)` - a device whose primary pointer cannot hover - which `hoverClick` in `appswitch.js` asks before it trusts the click's own pointer type, since some mobile browsers report a tap as a mouse click; on a laptop with a touchscreen the pointer type decides, a finger toggling and the mouse going home. The list holds every community app - the registry's, `model.Apps`, less any that Heliosian's Visibility tab narrows to people other than the reader - each with its transparent-background mark, name and tagline (the tagline the tab's, editable in Heliosian's admin), the current one tinted, and under them two lines: "Report a problem or idea", which opens the feedback form (`docs/feedback.md`), and "Built for the community, by the community" behind the GitHub mark, linking to the repository. The list is Heliosian's (`model.HomeApp` and `Apps`, the one registry of the apps, which the front page's apps section and the Visibility tab share): `initAppSwitch` in `appswitch.js` fills and wires every `.app-switch` in the page after reading `/api/apps` from the page's own origin (`docs/api.md`, Heliosian) - Heliosian and then the apps in order, each with `me.listed` false when the `Visibility` tab narrows it to a list this person is not on (`docs/home/data.md`) - and leaves those rows out, except the app being viewed, which stays listed as the current one since the reader is already there; if the read fails the list holds only its foot, the tile itself still the way home, this being visibility and not access. The links go to each app's host label - its registry key, except where the registry says otherwise (the calendar's is `when`) - and follow the tier of the page's own hostname: from `who.heliosian.com` the portal is `team.heliosian.com`, from `who.heliosiandev.com:8080` it is `team.heliosiandev.com:8080`, and Heliosian is the apex (`heliosian.com`, or `heliosiandev.com` on a developer's machine); an alias hostname counts as its app's, as `appFor` in `internal/app` routes it. The tile and the marks live in `web/public/common/brand/` (`heliosian-tile.png`, and `apps/<key>.png` for each registry key; an app without transparent art yet serves a stand-in there, noted in its own docs), which every app serves under `/brand/`.

## Admin Tools

Every app's Admin Tools is drawn by one builder, `adminPage({appName, allowed, email, sections})` in `web/common/admin.js`, on the chrome in `web/common/admin.css` (scoped under `.admin`): a teal header with the app's icon tile (its `/brand/icon-192.png`), "Admin", the signed-in address and a close button back to the app; a white rail of tabs in groups (Display, Editing & Control, and in Who? Data Overrides) on the left, a group with no tabs left out; cards on the right, one tab's panel at a time. Each tab is `{key, label, card, count}`, `card` building its panel and `count` an optional badge. The open tab is written into the address as `?tab=` and remembered for the page's life, so a redraw after a save, a reload or a link lands on the same tab. Someone `allowed` is false for sees "Admin access required" instead; each app's server serves its shell at `/admin` to anyone signed in and gates the admin routes behind it. Every app draws it inside its own shell at `/admin`, whose rail, toolbar and tab bar step aside (`body.is-admin`). On a phone the rail becomes a strip of pills.

The admin lists are one editor, `adminsCard({hint, title, load, save})` in the same file: the list with each person's name and address and a Remove, and the person picker (`picker.js`) to add someone from the directory or an address typed whole, each change saved at once. `appAdmins(app)` gives an app's `load` and `save`: the app's `admin-lists` resource, read by the app's key (`GET /api/admin-lists/{app}`) and written whole with its `edit` action (`docs/api.md`, Admin lists), over the app's Admins tab and the platform super admins. Who?'s Admin Tools has no admin list: Who?'s admins and the super admins are the data model's admins groups (`docs/datamodel.md`, Groups). The picker and the names beside each address come from the data model's people (`web/common/directory.js`, `docs/api.md`, Client).

## On phones

The bar is the topmost thing in every app, pinned under the status bar, at 48px: Who? and HCA-Team add a hamburger at its left for their drawer, the search box stays a box (no magnifier-and-overlay), and the keyboard hint and the switch's chevron drop. Under it Who? and HCA-Team keep a slim teal strip for the page title (or a back arrow and a record's name); Heliosian's rail is a drawer behind the hamburger, the same column slid in from the left.
