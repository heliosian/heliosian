# Volunteer portal

HCA-Team, the HCA Volunteer Portal, is where the Helios Community Association asks for help and where families answer: every event, committee, and idea the association runs each school year, the roles under each, and who signed up. It serves at the first of its `Hosts` in `internal/model/home.go`; the others there send their root to it, answer an old address below, and 404 anything else (`route` in `internal/app/server.go`). Its data is groups and memberships in the data model (`docs/team/data.md`); the pages are `web/team/`. The server's part is shared code named for what it does: the previews, share cards and redirects of activity pages are `ActivityPages` in `internal/db` (`activitypages.go`, `activitytree.go`, `redirects.go`), the description writer is `POST /api/do/describe-activity` beside `describe-group` in `internal/db/draft.go`, and the page routes and picture routes are wired in `internal/app/wiring.go`.

## Entities

- **School year** — the group everything of one year sits under, `2026 - 2027`. School years turn over on July 1; the current, last, and next years are computed from the date.
- **Category** — a heading on the Opportunities page ("Headline Events", "HCA Committees", "Just an Idea"), with a blurb and a picture, or one of an event's own categories, which the things under it are grouped by on its page. Categories display in their order.
- **Activity** — one thing to help with in one school year: title, description, picture, flyer, when it happens (a start and end, or words like "All Year"), where, how many spots, and its status. Activities form a tree: a committee, task, booth, or shift is an activity under the event it belongs to, and it can hold activities of its own. Each takes sign-ups itself, or only through the things under it (Allow volunteers). Switching sign-ups off keeps whoever had already signed up.
- **Sign-up** — a person on an activity at any depth, as a volunteer, a volunteer open to co-chairing, or a co-chair, with an optional note - shown to everyone as a notepad on the corner of the face in the Who's Involved list; resting on any face gives the sign-up's day, the note, and - to whoever runs the thing - who made the sign-up when it was somebody else and their RSVP when the thing has an invite on Helios When.

Activities repeat year to year by being copied: "Spring Celebration" in one year and the next are two activities, each carrying its own tree and sign-ups. Running an activity means running everything under it: a co-chair of the event edits its shifts too.

## Status

An activity is Pending, Open, Done, or Hidden. Pending is a suggestion waiting for approval; open takes sign-ups; done is over; hidden is parked where only those who run it see it. Whether something is full is not a status: a spots count sets the capacity, and the sign-up count against it says the rest.

## Who may do what

The policies decide every write (`internal/db/policy.go`); the page offers only what they allow.

- **Anyone signed in** browses everything open, signs themselves, their household or anyone else they see up, removes their own and their household's sign-ups, and suggests an activity wherever adding is allowed - under the year's "Just an Idea" heading through Suggest an Idea, or under an event's category that takes them. A suggestion waits as Pending, seen by whoever made it, those who run what it sits under, and the Team admins; the maker says on the form whether they are on it themselves (Sign me up as: Volunteer or Nothing). Where a list is hidden, they see its co-chairs, their own entry and their household's.
- **Offering to co-chair** is open to anyone signed up for an activity whose co-chairs have opened it to offers (`data.md`, Offers to co-chair): the sign-up's "Volunteer, and open to co-chairing", or Offer to Co-Chair under "Could you co-chair this?" in the chairs card in the rail, which thanks them once they have. It makes nobody a co-chair. Who has offered is seen by whoever runs the activity alone - its co-chairs, those of anything above it and the Team admins - and by each person who offered and their household for their own: there they follow the chairs in Who's Involved tagged CHAIR OPT, are marked "(Co-Chair Opt)" in a row's line of names, and appear under Co-Chair Options in the chairs card, each with a Make co-chair button that appoints them after a word of confirmation, beside the Open to co-chair offers switch. Everyone else sees an offerer as a volunteer.
- **Co-chairs** run an activity and everything under it: its own co-chairs - those in its managers group - and those of anything above it. They change it, add under it (live at once), hide and show it, set its volunteer settings and its own categories, move a thing only under something else they run, approve what is suggested under it, delete what has no one signed up and nothing under it, and make someone a co-chair or unmake one: a Co-Chair choice in the sign-up editor's As list that only they see, which puts the person in the activity's leads group and its managers group. Clicking a volunteer's face opens them a two-tab window - that sign-up first, then the contact card - where everyone else gets the card alone. An existing sign-up's editor also offers **Signed up for**: what the thing sits under, the things beside it and the things under it; picking another moves the sign-up there in one change, with no thank-you mail for a move. They see a hidden volunteer list in full. Show Hidden Things in the account menu is offered to anyone who co-chairs something, and reveals the hidden and pending things they run.
- **Team admins**, the members of the `team-admins` group, plus the super admins (`docs/datamodel.md`), do all of that on every activity and heading, and also set High Priority, manage the year's headings, the settings, the redirects and the two member lists in Admin Tools, and see Approval Needed. Show Hidden Things reveals everything to them. Spoof Mode - a super admin viewing the portal, and every other app, as someone else - is the toolbar's switch (`docs/toolbar.md`): the portal acts as whoever sign-in says.

## Pages

One shell, `web/team/index.html`, routes every page on the client: a left toolbar (Opportunities with each category and its count, My Sign Ups, Calendar, Approval Needed for admins, and Suggest an Idea) beside the shared top bar (`docs/toolbar.md`), whose search filters My Sign Ups and elsewhere opens a list under the box (`web/common/searchmenu.js`, shared with Celebrate) - a page with nothing to search, an activity or the calendar, carries the words to the opportunities page first - and `/` jumps to it. The list finds the year's activities at any depth by title and description, and people by name, one row per sign-up with their role and the activity ("Co-Chair · International Night"): upcoming first, then the year's past ones, then under a divider the year before in smaller grey rows. Each row carries a picture (the activity's, else its event's, or the person's photo) and the day (its event's for an undated committee, All Year for none), and opens the activity; the arrow keys and Enter pick one. Suggest an Idea is the short form - a lead-in, title, description, a rough When, and Sign me up as - filed under this year's "Just an Idea" heading.

- **Opportunities** (`/`) — cards for this school year's root activities, grouped under their categories in their order, with a category chip row that filters the grid - led, while an admin has marked anything this year a priority, by **High Priority** (a deep red chip behind a star, `?category=high-priority`), the events marked or holding something that is - and an academic-year dropdown. The **High Priority** chip carries how many things are marked a priority on a small round badge. Under the chips, with All picked, a **High Priority** panel - pale red, its heading beside a bolt in a red disc, "These need your help soon." - lists a row for each thing marked a priority that is still open and wants people: the thing itself at any depth, so a role under an event is its own row. Each row has its picture (else its event's), the things above it in small capitals ("HELIOS CARES › COMMUNITY SERVICE") over its title, two lines of its description and its event's category as a small tag in that category's chip colour, its day and hours (wrapping when long), and a quiet Sign up outlined in red - Signed up once the viewer is on it - the whole row opening its page. The panel takes what the page lists (so a past or full event's roles go with it until Show Completed Events), soonest first; under the High Priority chip it stands alone, without the grid. An event's card in the grid calls out what under it is a priority - "High priority: Decor · Childcare", each a link to the thing, or "High priority" for the event itself - and on an activity's page each row under it that is one carries **High Priority** beside its title, in the same deep red. The category picked - from a chip or the rail - is the address's, `?category=headline-events` (its title in lowercase words joined by dashes), so a reload, a copied link and Back keep it. Undated activities follow dated ones. Hidden and pending activities stay off the grid unless Show Hidden Things is on, and then only those the viewer runs; Show Completed Events brings back what is done, past, or full. Another year is `/years/{year}`.
- **My Sign Ups** (`/my`) — the viewer's own sign-ups at any depth, one year at a time from a pill beside the title, as the opportunities page's cards in date order, one per event (undated events last), each naming the viewer's sign-ups on it - the event itself, then the things under it, each with a pencil into the sign-up, where it can be changed or removed - in place of the Join button. Anyone with a household in the directory gets it under My Sign Ups in the rail - Me, then their partner and children, each with their count for the year - and `/my/{email}` is that person's page, the same cards with the same pencils; anyone else's address is not a page.
- **Calendar** (`/calendar`) — a month grid of every dated open or done activity, each in its event's category colour (things under an event in the lighter tint), with the viewer's own sign-ups in yellow behind a star. A thing under an event that happens when the event does - no time of its own, or the same one - stays off the grid, since the event stands for it, unless the viewer is on it. Chips across the top filter to Mine or one category; pending and hidden things appear only with Show Hidden Things on.
- **Approval Needed** (`/approvals`) — admins: every pending activity at any depth, shown as Root ▶ Child, each row with Approve, which opens it, and Hide, which parks it where only those who run it see it, and a click anywhere else on the row into its page. That page opens marked Needs approval, with the same two buttons beside the mark. Anyone else gets Not here; a co-chair finds what awaits them on their event's page, where a pending thing lists muted and marked Needs approval.
- **Activity** (`/v/{short name}`, or `/v/{id}` for one without a short name) — one page for every node. A hero image with the date card every app shares (`web/common/datecard.js`; `docs/dev.md`) - the day's tile with the sun as it stands at the start, the hours, the place, Add to Google Calendar; a thing with only timing words shows them on a stamp instead - then the description, rendered from markdown, beside a rail of where, who runs it (headed Chair for one person, Co-Chairs for more), the flyer when there is one (click for full size), and who to ask. My Family's Roles, above Who's Involved, lists the viewer's household's sign-ups on the thing and everything under it, a row each with the face, name and what they are on, and Edit into that sign-up. A child's chip is a link up to its parent; a root's is its category. Opportunities & Needs lists what sits under it grouped by the event's own categories — each with an Add button for whoever runs the event, and a Suggest button for everyone else where the category takes suggestions; hidden and pending rows join the list, muted, for whoever runs them. While an activity's page is open the toolbar carries its event too: the event itself, then its committees grouped by the event's categories, nested to any depth and closed until opened, each with its sign-up count or "2 of 5" against a set number of spots. Anyone can open the hero image at full size and share the page from the button at its corner (`detailHero`, `docs/dev.md`). Whoever runs it has Edit Activity - Basics, When, and Short name, which is its page address and its email list's and carries "Email list reaches everything under it" (`data.md`, The email list) - the banner's Edit image menu (upload, or find one in a stock library through a server-side search, `internal/imagesearch`; crop; remove; `web/common/heroimage.js`), the flyer's upload and remove, drag-and-drop that orders the rows and moves them between categories, and Volunteer Settings. Volunteer Info opens the table of everyone signed up there and under it - the shared data grid (`web/common/datagrid.js`) - with a filter choosing which activities it lists and the Add dropdown that adds the parents, children or siblings of those listed (`familyDropdown`, `web/common/rules.js`). For an admin, the top row carries a **High Priority** checkbox while the thing still wants people.
- **Volunteer Settings** — beside Edit Activity: the thing, the event's categories, and everything under it, each with Allow volunteers (Unlimited, Limited with its number of spots, No More, and Complete where it is already set), Show volunteers (Visible or Hidden) and Allow new activities (Allowed, Approval needed, Prohibited), tinted by value and saved as picked, and an eye for showing or hiding it; hiding one hides everything under it, whose eyes show closed until it is shown again. Its rows drag by their grip: onto the middle of another a thing becomes one of its sub-activities, onto its top or bottom edge it goes just before or after it, onto a category's heading it moves into that category. Beside each thing and category a pencil renames it in place and a + adds a thing under it by name alone; on an event the popup also adds and edits its categories.
- **Admin Tools** (`/admin`) — drawn by the Admin Tools builder every app shares (`docs/toolbar.md`): the selected year's headings (with pictures, reorderable), the settings (the Intro and the expense form), Email Notifications (the members of this year's `notify` group), Admins (the members of `team-admins`), and Redirects. The two member lists save through the shared group-members card (`groupMembers` in `web/common/admin.js`).

On phones the rail becomes a drawer behind the hamburger in the shared toolbar (`docs/toolbar.md`), which stays on top with the search box, a slim title strip sits under it, and a bottom tab bar holds Opportunities, My Sign Ups, and Calendar.

Copying an event and everything under it into the next year is `copyToNextYear` in `web/team/edit.js`, which no page offers a button for.

## Redirects

An old address on this site sends whoever holds it to where the thing is now, ahead of sign-in, so a link from the volunteer site this one replaced (`hca.heliosian.com/dl/signup/s/…`), an old nested link (`/v/inight/poland`, `/activities/{old id}/…`) or a renamed short name lands on the page's sign-in with that page's preview (`ActivityPages.Redirected` in `internal/db/redirects.go`, wrapped round the host ahead of sign-in in `internal/app`, and round a 404 on HCA-Team's other hosts, `Core.Aliased`). Only a plain fetch of a page is redirected, the query travelling along:

- a path under `/v/` or `/activities/` whose last part is an activity's id, short name or `ALIAS` answers `301` to the activity's address;
- anything else follows Team's `REDIRECT` rows: an old address matches exactly or as a prefix (`/dl/signup/s/768d91` sent to a party sends `/dl/signup/s/768d91/r/x` under it), a chain is followed to its end and one that comes back on itself goes nowhere, and the answer is `302` to an activity's address, a whole address elsewhere, or another path here.

Admin Tools › Redirects lists, adds, edits and removes them. Inside the shell, a `/v/` address the page cannot place is looked up through `ALIAS` and the address bar corrected (`resolveAlias` in `web/team/state.js`).

## Names and photos

Names and photos come from the directory (`PERSON`, through `web/common/directory.js`), so a volunteer looks the same here as in Helios Who?. Someone the directory does not list is a guest person in the data model, shown by the name they were given. Every activity is an email list on Helios Loop at its short name (`data.md`, The email list).

## Editing

In place, through modals, every change one `/api/q` batch: the policies refuse a write before anything is stored, and a batch is all or nothing. Deleting an activity is refused while anyone is signed up for it or anything sits under it.

## Email

Mail follows the data (`internal/db/membershipmail.go`): a person's own write sends it, once the write commits, never the sync's or a system's. Each letter is laid out the same way - the HCA-Team bar, a heading and a line of explanation, a details box (title, what it is part of, when, where, and rows for the occasion) and a button to the page - and is recorded as a `MESSAGE` with a `RECIPIENT` per person.

- **Thank you for volunteering** — to whoever was signed up and a student's parents, copied to the co-chairs of the thing and everything above it, who are its Reply-To. Says who signed them up when it was someone else, their role, the note - "Your note" when they wrote it, "Note from" and the writer's name when someone else did - and who runs it level by level ("Sealand Leads" for the thing itself and anything between, "Event Chairs" for the event, a thing on its own just "Chairs"). When the thing or the nearest thing above it has a date, it has an Add to Calendar button, and a second note follows - **Calendar invite: …** - to the volunteer and a student's parents alone, carrying the thing as a calendar invite (`METHOD:REQUEST`, from the portal's address as organizer, the thing's hours, titled "Clean Up Crew (International Night)", one `UID` per thing and volunteer). Timing words alone are not a date: no invite, no button.
- **Removed** — to whoever held a sign-up that was removed and a student's parents, copied to the co-chairs, carrying a cancellation (`METHOD:CANCEL`, the same `UID`), naming who removed it when it was someone else; only for a thing that had a date.
- **You're a co-chair** — to someone made a co-chair, copied to the other co-chairs.
- **Notices** — to the members of the `notify` group of the thing and of every group above it (the school year's, set in Admin Tools › Email Notifications): a new sign-up, an offer to co-chair ("Offer to co-chair: …", saying its co-chairs can make them one), and a new thing added under another, saying whether it awaits approval; opening an activity to offers sends nothing. The person acting and the person signed up never get one.

Mail goes out through Mailgun from the portal's address (`docs/deploy.md`); in sample mode each message is written as an `.html` file under `local/mail/` to open in a browser.

## Sign-in and install

A link to an activity previews in chat apps and social feeds even though the site is behind sign-in: the sign-in page served at an activity's address carries its Open Graph tags (its title and what it sits under, the date line and the start of its description, its address), and `/open/share/{id}.png` is a public 1200x630 card drawn on the server: on the pale wash, the horizontal lockup, the title with the yellow swoosh and what it sits under above it, the day behind a calendar glyph and the time behind a clock (or the timing words alone), the rail's meadow small in the bottom-left corner, and on the right the flyer shown whole or, without one, the picture. A thing takes the nearest date, timing, flyer and picture above it when it has none of its own. Only an activity that is open or done, with it and everything above it visible to everyone and nothing above it pending, previews or has a card; anything else, and every other page, gets the portal's own preview, whose card, `/open/share/upcoming.png`, lists the next four of this year's open root activities that still want people ("Volunteers needed"). The tags say what a poster on the wall would say and never who signed up; anyone holding the link sees them. `internal/sharecard` draws the cards, with Montserrat bundled in `web/common/fonts/`.

Everything else sits behind Google sign-in restricted to the school domain, sharing a session with the other apps on the same tier. It installs to a home screen like the directory (`docs/who/pwa.md`), with its icons and splash screens under `web/public/team/`.
